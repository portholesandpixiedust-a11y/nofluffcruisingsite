Every year on the President's Cruise, Michael Bayley takes loyalty and policy questions from cruisers on the record. The same six requests come up, and the answer has been no every time.

The video walks each one and, more usefully, the reason behind the no. Several of them are decisions about where Royal Caribbean makes its money rather than about what guests want.

If you want the other side of this, the requests leadership has quietly said yes to are in [5 Things Royal Caribbean's Leadership Just Confirmed](/news/).
