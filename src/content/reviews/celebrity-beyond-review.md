Celebrity Beyond is an Edge-class ship, stretched, and it is Celebrity's argument that premium cruising is worth paying for. The review goes onboard to test that claim with no sponsor attached.

## What the review covers

The arrival experience, the ship's design and public spaces, the cabins, the food, and who this ship is really built for.

## Where it lands

Coming from Royal Caribbean, the differences that matter are the food and the noise level rather than the hardware. The full walkthrough and specs are on the [Celebrity Beyond ship page](/ships/celebrity/celebrity-beyond/).
