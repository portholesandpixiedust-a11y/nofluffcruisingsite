If you are Diamond Plus on Legend of the Seas' transatlantic, the buy-one-get-one specialty dinner is off for this sailing. [Royal Caribbean Blog](https://www.royalcaribbeanblog.com/2026/10/23/royal-caribbean-has-so-many-loyal-cruisers-one-ship-its-taking-away-popular-perk), Matt Hochberg, updated 23 Oct 2026, prints the email. [Cruise.blog](https://cruise.blog/2026/10/royal-caribbean-suddenly-removes-loyalty-perks-legend-transatlantic) prints the same decision the same day.

The cruise leaves Barcelona on Sunday 25 Oct 2026. Neither article links a Royal Caribbean newsroom post.

The photo on this page is the lead image on the Royal Caribbean Blog story, an aerial of Legend of the Seas. The article text retrieved on 23 Oct does not name a photographer.

## The sentence in the email

Royal Caribbean Blog says the email went out Thursday night to Diamond Plus and Pinnacle Club members. The sentence it prints is: due to exceptionally high demand for specialty dining and fully booked restaurant venues, the BOGO Loyalty Dining Benefit for Diamond Plus members will not be available during this sailing.

Cruise.blog prints Benefits, plural, in that sentence. The rest matches. This post follows the blog's singular Benefit and notes the other spelling.

The blog says the letter does not explicitly say a count of top-tier members caused the cut. The blog's own opening talks about a higher than normal concentration of Diamond Plus and Pinnacle Club members. Cruise.blog's opening says the high number is why not every perk is available. Those causes are the articles. The email sentence names demand and full restaurants.

The blog also says all other loyalty food and beverage benefits remain available onboard. Cruise.blog does not print that sentence.

## What the perk usually is

Royal Caribbean's [Crown and Anchor benefit grid](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/content/dam/royal/resources/pdf/crown-and-anchor-society-benefit-grid.pdf), read 23 Oct 2026, describes the standing offer in these words. You purchase a specialty restaurant dinner cover charge during the first or second night of your cruise and enjoy complimentary dining for the second guest. It is valid for dinner reservations only on the first or second night.

The grid excludes Celebration Table, Chef's Table, Supper Clubs, Hooked, Izumi Hibachi and Teppanyaki, Omakase, Izumi in the Park, Johnny Rockets, Playmakers, Portside BBQ, and Royal Railway.

Royal Caribbean Blog describes that perk as the one you get at Diamond Plus. The email says it will not be available on this sailing. The grid itself does not mention 25 Oct, and the tier marks beside that row were not clean enough in this check to assign the row to one column. The email is the withdrawal, and it names Diamond Plus.

## Island Vibes

Both articles print a replacement on this crossing only. The email calls Island Vibes a brand-new dining concept created exclusively for Diamond Plus and Pinnacle Club members.

| What the email lists | Detail in both articles |
|---|---|
| Where | Surfside Eatery |
| When | Every day, 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. |
| Price | $25 a person |
| How | Reservation. You can book it onboard at any time |
| Who | Diamond Plus and Pinnacle Club, on this crossing |

The quoted description says it brings together fresh coastal flavors, tropical-inspired favorites, and an atmosphere designed to transport you straight to the islands.

Royal Caribbean Blog adds one Pinnacle line the other article does not print. Pinnacle Club members may use their one-time $25 specialty dining benefit at Island Vibes, and at other specialty dining venues as usual.

The benefit grid's footnote for that $25 discount says it is for adult Pinnacle Club members, once per cruise, on sailings of 5 nights or longer on Oasis, Quantum, and Icon class ships. Legend of the Seas is Icon class, and this cruise is 13 nights, so the length and the class match the footnote. The venue list in the footnote does not name Island Vibes. The email is what adds it.

Cruise.blog names Barcelona, Palma de Mallorca, Malaga, and Cadiz, and calls this the first Icon class ship to cross the Atlantic on a revenue voyage. It does not print the hours. The 11:59 a.m. arrival in Malaga is the time on the Royal Caribbean page.

The naming cruise and the Caribbean season after Fort Lauderdale are a different post. [Lori Robinson is godmother of Legend of the Seas](/news/legend-of-the-seas-robinson-godmother-fort-lauderdale/) is not this email.

## What this means if you are Diamond Plus or Pinnacle

If you are Diamond Plus, do not plan the first-night or second-night BOGO on this crossing. The email says that benefit will not be available. Other loyalty food and beverage benefits remain, in the Royal Caribbean Blog report of the letter.

If you want Island Vibes, it is $25 a person, 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m., and you reserve it onboard. If you are Pinnacle, the blog says your one-time $25 specialty dining benefit can be used there. The grid's venue list does not name that room, so use the email for Island Vibes and the grid for the other rooms it already lists.
