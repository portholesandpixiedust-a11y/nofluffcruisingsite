MSC cancelled the Sunday 1 Nov 2026 sailing on MSC Poesia. [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/coast-guard-detains-msc-poesia-cruise-ship-miami/), Cristian Benavides, updated that report at 5:33 p.m. EST on Tuesday 3 Nov. MSC confirmed the cancellation to CBS the same day.

The fare comes back. A future cruise credit equal to the fare is in one outlet's account of a letter in the cabin. CBS does not print the credit.

An earlier note on this site, [Coast Guard holds MSC Poesia in Miami](/news/msc-poesia-coast-guard-miami-november-2026/), is the morning of 3 Nov. That page was written before this cancellation. Use this note for the refund.

The photo is the lead image on [Cruise Hive's 4 Nov story](https://www.cruisehive.com/msc-poesia-guests-get-full-refund-plus-100-cruise-credit-after-cancelled-sailing/222574). The credit is Ritu Manoj Jethani. The caption says MSC Poesia in Florida. It does not date the frame. It does not say the photo shows this pier.

## What MSC told CBS

MSC's statement says the line made "the difficult decision to cancel the current sailing." It apologizes for the disruption.

The reason in that statement is a "regulatory matter" that arose before departure. MSC says it could not resolve the issue in time to sail.

CBS says you get a full refund of the cruise fare. CBS says MSC is helping people get home. The statement does not print a clock, a credit, or a hotel rule.

A source familiar with the matter told CBS that more than 2,000 people were on board. CBS does not name the source. Do not treat that line as a manifest.

CBS still prints MSC's capacity line. More than 3,200 passengers and over 1,000 crew. That is the size of the ship. It is not a headcount for Sunday.

## What the Coast Guard told CBS

Capt. Frank Florio, commander of the Miami sector, spoke to CBS on Tuesday.

He said the Coast Guard "identified deficiencies with systems on board the vessel that we are addressing with MSC." The ship "will remain detained until the deficiencies are corrected and it is cleared for departure."

The statement does not name the system. The Monday statement on the same page still says teams are checking international standards and U.S. regulations. Miami sector detained the ship on Sunday.

## The cabin letter, from one outlet

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/msc-poesia-guests-get-full-refund-plus-100-cruise-credit-after-cancelled-sailing/222574), Melissa Mayntz, 4 Nov 2026, prints a letter it says was posted on Facebook and then put in staterooms. Those lines rest on this page. CBS does not print them. This desk did not open the Facebook post.

The article calls the trip an 11-night Southern and Western Caribbean cruise that was due out of PortMiami at 5 p.m. on Sunday and did not leave. CBS does not print 11 nights, 5 p.m., or the region.

Cruise Hive says you were told just after 10 a.m. on Tuesday. The captain, not named on this page, said the team could not solve "the regulatory matter that delayed our departure," so the cruise is cancelled.

The letter, as the article prints it, apologizes for limited information. It says the matter arose on Sunday evening, before departure, and that the resources to fix it were not ready in time.

The same article says the Tuesday announcement lets you stay aboard until noon on Thursday 5 Nov. It says Wi-Fi is now complimentary.

On money, the article's account of the letter has four parts.

- The cruise fare, plus eligible prepaid purchases, goes back to the original payment within 14 days. The examples it gives are beverage packages, specialty dining, internet, and shore tours booked through MSC.
- A future cruise credit equal to 100% of the fare. Any MSC sailing. You redeem it by confirming a booking by 2 Nov 2027.
- Change fees and transportation, if the new trip is "in the same category of service, including the same destination as your originally booked journey."
- A hotel, if you cannot get home the day you leave the ship. You submit the claim. The article prints no dollar cap.

Cruise Hive also says announcements onboard described the delay as unrelated to a health or safety issue. Florio's statement to CBS is the deficiencies line above. This note does not pick between them. Read both.

The article says its ship tracker still showed Poesia in Miami on Tuesday night.

## What to do if you are on this sailing

Read the letter in the cabin, and the message in the MSC app, before you buy a new flight.

The fare refund is in MSC's statement to CBS. The credit, the 14 days, the Wi-Fi, and the hotel claim are in Cruise Hive's account of the letter. If your replacement travel is a different class or a different city, that article does not print a promise.
