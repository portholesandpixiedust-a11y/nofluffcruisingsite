Carnival opened its 2028/29 Miami schedule. [The newsroom post](https://www.carnival-news.com/2026/11/12/carnival-cruise-line-opens-2028-29-miami-sailings-with-346-departures-and-217-visits-to-celebration-key), dated 12 Nov 2026, puts 346 sailings on sale from April 2028 through April 2029. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/carnival-opens-2028-29-miami-season-with-346-sailings-on-5-ships/) the same day repeats the count. Neither page prints a fare.

The photo is the lead image on the Carnival post. It is an aerial of Carnival Celebration underway, blue hull and red funnel, with a city shoreline behind the ship. The file name is Carnival Celebration_social.jpg. The post does not name a photographer, and it does not name the port. It is not a picture of a 2028 rate.

## The five ships and the window

The sailings are on Carnival Celebration, Carnival Conquest, Carnival Magic, Carnival Vista, and Carnival Venezia. The window in the release is April 2028 through April 2029. The release says the trips visit 22 destinations across The Bahamas and the Caribbean, from three-day getaways to 11-day Carnival Journeys.

Celebration Key, on Grand Bahama, is on 217 of the visits. The release calls it part of the Paradise Collection. It does not print a price for a cabana, a pass, or a day on the island.

This is a new set of cruises to book. It does not move a sailing someone already holds, so there is no refund line and no port-skip table.

## The short cruises

Conquest and Magic carry 174 sailings of three to five days. The ports named for those trips are Celebration Key, Nassau, Grand Turk, and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay. The release does not say which of those four is on a three-day cruise and which is on a five-day cruise. Match the date on carnival.com before you treat all four as one week.

## Carnival Celebration's week

Celebration keeps seven-day Eastern and Western Caribbean cruises from Miami. The ports named are Amber Cove, San Juan, St. Thomas, Cozumel, and Isla Tropicale. The release says the sailings include those places. It does not say every seven-day cruise calls all five.

## The longer trips

Vista and Venezia offer 39 eight-day southern Caribbean sailings. Those call Aruba and Curacao. Most also call Bonaire. The release does not say which of the 39 skip Bonaire.

Conquest adds two 11-day Carnival Journeys to the southern Caribbean. The calls named are Antigua, Barbados, Dominica, Grenada, Guadeloupe, St. Maarten, and St. Thomas. The release does not print the two departure dates. Do not fill a date in from a roundup that is not this post.

## What is not in the release

No fare. No deposit. No onboard credit. No tax line. No quote from a named executive. The booking paths in the post are 1-800-CARNIVAL, carnival.com, and a travel advisor.

Cruise Industry News repeats the ships, the 346, the 217, the 174, the 39, and the two 11-day trips. It also says the Miami opening follows Carnival's 2028-29 Port Canaveral lineup, more than 300 departures on five ships. That sentence is the trade story's. It is not in the Miami newsroom post. This page does not copy the Port Canaveral ships or dates into the Miami list.

The same Cruise Industry News story prints almost 2.5 million Celebration Key visits in the destination's first year, then 2.7 million guests in 2026 and 3.5 million in 2027. Those three figures are not in the 12 Nov newsroom post. They are already on [Celebration Key expects 3.5 million guests in 2027](/news/celebration-key-princess-aida-costa-3-5-million-guests-2027/). This Miami opening does not update them.

## If you are booking

Pick the ship first. Conquest and Magic are the three- to five-day pair. Celebration is the seven-day ship in this release. Vista and Venezia are the eight-day southern Caribbean pair. Conquest is also the ship with the two 11-day trips, and the dates for those two are not in the release. The phone number in the post is 1-800-CARNIVAL. The price is whatever carnival.com shows for the date you want. This release does not lock a percent off.
