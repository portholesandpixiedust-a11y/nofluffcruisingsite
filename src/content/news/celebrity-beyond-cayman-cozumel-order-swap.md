Celebrity Beyond changed the Western Caribbean order on the 20 Sep 2026 Miami sailing. Celebrity's own guest letter is the Tier 1 record. Cruise Timetables shows the same revised calls.

## The new port order

You still leave Miami on Sunday 20 Sep at 4:00pm. Perfect Day at CocoCay stays Monday, now 7:00am to 4:30pm. Tuesday remains a sea day.

Wednesday 23 Sep is George Town, Grand Cayman, 10:00am to 4:30pm by tender. Thursday 24 Sep is Cozumel, 11:00am to 7:30pm alongside. Friday is at sea. Saturday Nassau runs 10:30am to 5:30pm. Sunday return to Miami is 7:00am.

That is a swap of the Mexico and Cayman sequence, plus small timing moves at CocoCay and Nassau. Cozumel gets a longer evening window than many Western Caribbean calls.

## Tours and refunds

Celebrity says Shore Excursions will automatically reschedule prepaid Celebrity tours hit by the change. Tours that cannot move are cancelled and refunded to the original payment method. Check the Celebrity app or My Celebrity Cruises before you buy a replacement.

Independent operators are on you. If you booked Grand Cayman or Cozumel tours outside Celebrity for the old day order, call those vendors now.

## Tender access in Grand Cayman

The letter is blunt on mobility. Tenders work for collapsible wheelchair users who can take a few steps. Full-time scooter or wheelchair users who cannot take a few steps may not get ashore in George Town. Celebrity points those guests to (866) 592-7225 or special_needs@celebrity.com before sailing.

Plan a ship day for Grand Cayman if tender access does not fit your needs. Cozumel docks, so that day is the cleaner ashore option for limited mobility on this revised loop.

## What you should do

Open the Celebrity app and confirm your tour list against Wednesday Grand Cayman and Thursday Cozumel. Screenshot the letter times for anyone meeting you ashore.
