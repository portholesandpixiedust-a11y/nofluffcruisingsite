Princess marked a Star Princess call at Celebration Key. [The line's release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/princess-cruises-commemorates-inaugural-season-at-celebration-key-with-star-princess-302939740.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Fort Lauderdale, 15 Nov 2026, 12:50 p.m. ET.

The photo is on that release. The file name is Gus-Antorcha-and-Captain-Arrigo.jpg. Gus Antorcha and Captain Paolo Arrigo stand with a sand plaque. The plaque reads Princess inaugural visit, November 15, 2026, with both names. The arch behind them reads Celebration Key. The release caption names the two men. It does not describe the plaque wording. The wording is on the plaque in the photo.

## What the release counts

The release calls this the inaugural call of Star Princess, Princess's newest ship, at Celebration Key on the south side of Grand Bahama. It says the call is the beginning of:

- 69 scheduled calls in the 2026-27 season
- 32 planned calls in 2028
- Six ships

It does not name the six ships. It does not list the dates. It does not print a fare, a cabana rate, or a guest count. Antorcha's quote uses the word "thousands." [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/princess-commemorates-celebration-key-inaugural-season-with-princess/) on 16 Nov prints the same 69, the same 32, and the same six ships, and it does not add names.

The release describes beaches, freshwater lagoons, family activities, private cabanas, shopping, and Bahamian food. It does not price any of them. The phone number in the release is 1-800-PRINCESS, also printed as 1-800-774-6237.

The September post, [Celebration Key's 2027 guest count](/news/celebration-key-princess-aida-costa-3-5-million-guests-2027/), has the earnings-call figures for the island. Those figures are not in this release. This release does not say Princess began calling in October.

## What Cruise Hive adds

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/princess-cruises-newest-ship-finally-gets-its-celebration-key-ceremony/223702), Melissa Mayntz, 16 Nov, says Star Princess called Celebration Key on 1 Nov, and that 15 Nov was the visit with the ceremony. It says guests on 1 Nov got pins, with a note that called that visit the inaugural sailing. It also says the ceremony sailing left Fort Lauderdale on 14 Nov for seven nights, and that Celebration Key was the first port.

The Princess release does not mention 1 Nov, the pins, the 14 Nov departure, or a seven-night length. It calls the visit in this release the inaugural call. The two accounts are not the same claim. Use the release for the 69 and the 32. Treat 1 Nov as Cruise Hive's account until Princess prints it.

## If Celebration Key is on your cruise

The release is a season count, not a boarding change. Match Celebration Key against the ships and dates on your booking. The six ship names are not in the release. The phone number above is the one the release prints.
