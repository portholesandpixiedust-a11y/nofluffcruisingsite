Royal Princess is out of a Singapore dry dock with a new pub, a larger bar, 20 more cabins, and a Sanctuary Club that is still open to the rest of the adults onboard.

The [Princess release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-princess-emerges-from-drydock-with-new-sanctuary-collection-302925275.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Fort Lauderdale, 30 Oct 2026, 3:00 p.m. ET. It says the dock ran from 16 Oct through 30 Oct. It calls the ship a 3,595-guest ship. Rodrigo Llaguno, the chief operating officer, is the executive in the quote.

The photo is the release image. The caption goes left to right: O'Malley's Irish Pub, the Suite Lounge, and Bellini's. It calls them newly enhanced spaces on Royal Princess. The source line is Princess Cruises. A separate photographer is not named.

## What moved on the ship

O'Malley's Irish Pub takes the old Vines spot on deck 5. Bellini's grows into the old Photo Gallery and looks over the Piazza. The release describes Irish-inspired food and drinks in the pub, and Bellini cocktails, champagne, sparkling wine, and other specialty drinks in the bar.

The cabin math in the release is six mini-suites, seven oceanview cabins, two interiors, and five single interiors. That is 20. The new oceanview cabins can be booked as Sanctuary Collection rooms, and so can suites, mini-suites, deluxe balconies, and balconies. No fare is printed.

The Sanctuary Restaurant is on deck 6, beside the Concerto Dining Room. The release says it is only for Sanctuary Collection guests.

The casino loses the staircase between decks 6 and 7, and the floor is given more room for slots. The Effy boutique is refreshed. The Shops of Princess switch to the retail partner Starboard.

## The club rule, and the bill

The footnote is the line that matters if you sail soon. The Sanctuary Club becomes part of the collection on 22 April 2027. Until that date, guests 16 and older may use it. The release says the room used to be the Retreat Pool and The Sanctuary.

An earlier [Princess release](https://www.princess.com/news/news-releases/2026/10/princess-cruises-expands-sanctuary-collection), dated 22 Oct 2026 on the page and 21 Oct in the dateline, put Sanctuary cabins on sale for a 12-day South Pacific cruise on 4 Jan 2027. People who book the collection once it is on sale get Princess Premier. The release lists drinks, specialty and casual dining, Wi-Fi, crew appreciation, room service, and OceanNow in that package. Cabanas at the club still cost extra.

If you booked before 22 Oct and you already held a Reserve Collection cabin, that release says you get the Sanctuary experience at no added cost. Premier is left out of that conversion.

The 30 Oct release calls the collection all-inclusive. It does not repeat the Premier exclusion. For the bill, use the 22 Oct booking rules.

## The season after the yard

The 30 Oct release says the ship then sails a Sydney season in Australia, New Zealand, and the South Pacific. She is one of three Princess ships in the region for 2026-27. The counts are 42 itineraries and 62 departures. The other two ships are not named.

That is a different note from the [2028-29 Australia season](/news/princess-2028-29-australia-new-zealand-grand-world-odyssey/) already on this site. This dry dock does not change those 2028 dates.

The 22 Oct release also times the other four ships. Regal goes on sale 5 Nov 2026, Discovery on 4 Dec, Sky on 10 Dec, and Enchanted on 17 Dec. Those dates are sale dates. They are not this Singapore dock.

## What this means for you

If you board before 22 April 2027 and you are 16 or older, the release says the Sanctuary Club is yours to walk into, whatever cabin you booked. The restaurant on deck 6 stays with the collection.

If you held Reserve Collection before 22 Oct, look for Premier on the folio. The conversion in the earlier release leaves it off. If you want a Sanctuary cabin that includes Premier, the first sailing named for that sale is 4 Jan 2027. Check the category on princess.com, or call 1-800-PRINCESS, before you assume the package is in the fare.

Facts above follow the Princess release on 30 Oct 2026 and the Princess newsroom page dated 22 Oct 2026.
