Three of the four new rooms on Marella Discovery 2 cost extra. [TUI's newsroom release](https://www.tuigroup.com/en/newsroom/news/marella-cruises-launches-its-first-ever-karaoke-venue-plus-a-secret-speakeasy-1920s-escape-room-and-creative-retreat-as-marella-discovery-2-goes-adults-only), stamped 6 Nov 2026 at 08:25 UTC, is the line's account. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/hotel-operations-entertainment/karaoke-venue-leads-new-areas-as-discovery-2-goes-adults-only), Holly Payne, and [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/marella-discovery-2-gets-karaoke-room-other-venues-as-it-goes-adults-only/) report the same prices the same day.

The photo is Seatrade's rendering of The Loft. The credit on that page is Marella Cruises. The TUI page has an image and does not name a photographer, so that file is not the hero.

## What opens, and when

The four spaces replace former kids' areas. They are only on Discovery 2. TUI says they open by early 2027, as the ship becomes adults-only. Seatrade and Cruise Industry News print that same window.

Cruise Industry News says the adults-only plan was announced in June. That June page was not opened for this post. The 6 Nov release does not print the first adults-only sailing date. Do not invent one.

## What you pay

TUI's prices, matched by Seatrade and Cruise Industry News:

- **Amplify Karaoke.** From £14.95 per room per hour, for up to seven people. You book the room and pick the songs. TUI calls it the line's first dedicated karaoke venue.
- **Red Handed.** £16.95 per person. Teams of up to six get 60 minutes. The setting is backstage at the fictional Cabaret Rouge in the 1920s. The task in the release is Lady Ruby Bardot's diamond necklace. Seven logic puzzles. Two dressing rooms. One is hidden. A host can give clues on a vintage telephone.
- **The Lost Luggage Bureau.** £19.95 per person. The price includes three signature cocktails. Non-alcoholic versions are part of the release. The show runs 90 minutes, for up to 42 people. Two stories: The Case in Question, and Smugglers Society. You walk in through what looks like a lost-property office.
- **The Loft.** Included. No booking. Open 24 hours. It has a library, board games, and crafts. TUI and Cruise Industry News say Crafts Ahoy runs one hosted session on port days and two on sea days. The list is jewellery making, macramé, watercolour painting, sketching, button art, and quilling. Seatrade says the programme runs sessions and does not print that split.

You book the paid rooms at reception or in Navigate. Pre-booking for The Lost Luggage Bureau is also through Cruise Control. Those paths are in the TUI release. Seatrade repeats them. Cruise Industry News does not.

The prices are in pounds. The release does not print a dollar conversion. Use the pound figures on the page.

## The climbing wall

TUI says the climbing wall stays all-inclusive. The refresh is a wider mix of hold sizes, in the style of modern climbing centres and Olympic-standard walls. Cruise Industry News includes the Olympic-standard line. Seatrade stops at modern climbing centres. None of the three pages prints a date for the new wall.

## If you are booked on Discovery 2

If your cruise is in early 2027 or later, the three fees are the extra spend for those rooms. The Loft and the climbing wall are the included pieces in this release. Check the pound price again before you sail. A fee card can move, and this one was read from the 6 Nov 2026 release.
