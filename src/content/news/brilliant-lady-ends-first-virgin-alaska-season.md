Brilliant Lady closed Virgin Voyages first Alaska and British Columbia season in early September 2026. Cruise Industry News covered the wrap on 10 Sep 2026.

## What the inaugural season looked like

The ship left Seattle from late May through early September. Cruise Industry News counted more than 15 sailings. Lengths ran seven to 12 nights across 12 destinations.

The final Alaska cruise was eight nights and ended in Vancouver. Ports on that last loop included Ketchikan, Skagway, Icy Strait Point, and Sitka. If you sailed that finale, you closed the season in Canada rather than Seattle.

## Mid-September reposition

After Vancouver, Brilliant Lady began a mid-September reposition toward Los Angeles. California calls on the move include San Francisco, Santa Barbara, San Diego, and Catalina. Those coastal days are the bridge between Alaska and winter warm-weather product.

## Fall and winter schedule

Fall Mexican Riviera sailings leave from the World Cruise Center in San Pedro. The seven-night loop hits Cabo San Lucas, Puerto Vallarta, and Mazatlán. Virgin also scheduled five-night Baja sailings before a late October reposition to Miami.

Winter 2026-27 splits time between four-night Bahamas cruises and 10-night Eastern Caribbean cruises. That pattern is the post-Alaska product you will see on booking tools once the ship clears the west coast move.

## Alaska returns in 2027

Cruise Industry News reported a second Alaska season for late May through early September 2027. Departures again use Seattle and Vancouver. If you liked the 2026 product and want another try, watch those 2027 windows when they open for your cabin type.

## What you should do

Guests booked on fall San Pedro or Baja sailings should confirm embark terminal and hotel nights around the reposition calendar.
