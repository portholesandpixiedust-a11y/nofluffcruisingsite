Royal Caribbean put Voyager of the Seas on Japan and Asia cruises for spring 2028. Cruise Industry News, dated 1 Oct 2026, sets the window from late February through April 2028 and describes the routes. TRAICY Global, dated 2 Oct 2026, prints departure dates in March and April. TRAICY uses Yokohama. Cruise Industry News uses Tokyo. No English Royal Caribbean press release for this spring program was available for this post. A press-center page titled for Voyager's 2028 Asia Pacific season returned no article text. The English line release that does exist covers a different season.

## Routes in Cruise Industry News

Three products sit in the 1 Oct article.

A 10-night open-jaw runs from Hong Kong to Tokyo. It calls Nagasaki and Kagoshima, stays overnight in Kobe, then calls Osaka and continues to Tokyo.

Three roundtrips from Tokyo run 11 to 12 nights. Cruise Industry News says they explore Japan and South Korea. Ports named across that set are Kobe, Osaka, Kumamoto, Fukuoka, Nagasaki, Kagoshima, and Sasebo. Select cruises call Jeju at Seogwipo and Busan. Other highlights are Mount Fuji at Shimizu and an overnight in Okinawa. The article does not assign those ports to one date.

A 16-night crossing runs from Tokyo to Seattle. The ship calls Sapporo at Muroran, with an overnight in Hokkaido. Sea days follow, including a crossing of the International Date Line. The cruise ends in Seattle.

Cruise Industry News says the line highlighted these trips as ways to see parts of Japan during cherry blossom season, among other things. Treat that as the article's season framing. The article does not promise blossoms on your date.

TRAICY also prints two one-ways. The Hong Kong sailing is a 10-night cruise on 10 Mar 2028 to Yokohama, titled Nagasaki, Kagoshima, Kobe and Osaka. The Seattle crossing is a 16-night cruise from Yokohama on 13 Apr 2028, titled Transpacific.

TRAICY also prints a day count next to each night count. An 11-night cruise is labeled 12 days. The 16-night crossing is labeled 16 days. Book the night count and the date. The day label is TRAICY's.

TRAICY prints two departures on 10 Mar 2028. One is the Yokohama roundtrip. One is the Hong Kong one-way. One ship cannot leave both ports that morning. The translation warning sits on that page. Open the date before you treat 10 Mar as settled.

Cruise Industry News says the program runs from late February through April. TRAICY's earliest printed departure is 10 Mar. TRAICY's window line says March through April. A February date appears in Cruise Industry News as late February. That article does not print the February calendar day.

## Separate from the Alaska season

[The 2028 Alaska post](/news/royal-caribbean-2028-alaska-four-ships-booking/) puts Voyager on seven-night cruises from Seattle from April through September. This Japan program is the earlier Asia season. The 16-night crossing ends in Seattle. Book that crossing on its own date. Use the Alaska post for the summer Seattle cruises.

Cruise Industry News also places these Japan cruises inside Voyager's wider 2027-28 Asia Pacific season, with trips from Brisbane, Sydney, Singapore, and Hong Kong. That wider season is a different product from the Alaska release.

An English PR Newswire release on 27 Aug 2026, timed 13:29 CST, homeports Voyager in Hong Kong from January to February 2028 for Chinese New Year. Sailings in that release run 4 to 10 nights, including Hong Kong trips to Ishigaki and Okinawa. Bookings in that release are for the China market. That release does not list the March and April dates in this post.
