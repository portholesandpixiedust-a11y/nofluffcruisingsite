Whittier's newer cruise dock took a serious hit in mid-September 2026. Cruise Hive first reported a fire at the Chugach Glacier Gateway Cruise Terminal on 14 Sep. The tour boat Klondike Express was involved. Norwegian Jade was docked there when the fire started.

## Ship status

Jade moved away from the berth without damage. Guests already onboard were not dealing with a shipboard fire. The operational problem was the pier itself.

Anchorage Daily News reported on 16 Sep 2026 that the Alaska fire marshal's office declared the dock a total loss. Cruise Hive followed that finding in a later piece. Cause remained under investigation in those reports. Until investigators publish a cause, treat rumors about wiring or fuel as unverified.

## Where embarkation went next

Norwegian shifted embarkation to the existing Whittier Alaska Cruise Ship Terminal 1. If you were joining Jade after the fire, your check-in address changed even if the sailing date did not.

Bring printed or offline copies of the new terminal instructions. Whittier logistics already involve the Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel timing. A last-minute terminal swap makes that timing tighter. Miss the tunnel window and you can miss the gangway even when the ship is healthy.

## Wider Whittier impact

A total-loss dock changes how many ships Whittier can work at once until repairs or replacement progress. Lines that planned on Chugach Glacier Gateway capacity may lean harder on Terminal 1 or other Gulf ports. Watch your own line's port alerts if you hold later Whittier calls this season.

Shore excursion meeting points can move when the dock footprint shrinks. Confirm bus numbers and curb letters the night before landing.

## What you should do

Jade guests for sailings right after 14 Sep should confirm terminal name, luggage drop, and parking on their Norwegian documents. Independent tour operators meeting that ship should verify the pickup curb at Terminal 1.
