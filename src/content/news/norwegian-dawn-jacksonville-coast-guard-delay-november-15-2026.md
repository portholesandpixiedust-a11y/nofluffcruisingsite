Norwegian Dawn's first Jacksonville departure of the season is two hours later. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/coast-guard-inspection-to-delay-norwegian-dawn-departure-from-jacksonville/223204), Melissa Mayntz, and [The Travel](https://www.thetravel.com/norwegian-cruise-line-email-raises-concerns-us-coast-guard-disruption-dawn-jacksonville/), Alessandro Passalalpi, both on 9 Nov 2026, quote a Norwegian Cruise Line email for the sailing that leaves on Sunday 15 Nov 2026.

The photo is the lead picture on the Cruise Hive story. The credit on that page is Dennis MacDonald. It is not a photo of the 15 Nov departure. The Travel's picture is marked Gemini-enhanced, so it is not used here.

No Norwegian newsroom post for this delay was open at this check. The email is the account both stories quote. Cruise Hive says a booked guest posted it on Reddit. This check did not open that Reddit post.

## The time in the email

Both stories quote the same change. A scheduled U.S. Coast Guard inspection that morning pushes boarding later. Departure moves from 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Travel prints 5:00 p.m. ET. Cruise Hive prints 5:00 p.m. and does not add the zone. Jacksonville is in Eastern time.

The email, as both stories quote it, tells you to arrive two hours later than the port arrival time you picked at online check-in. If you picked 11:00 a.m., the example says come at 1:00 p.m. It asks you not to arrive earlier than the revised time. Boarding will not be open yet.

One sentence in The Travel says the ship is set to leave earlier. The email on that same page moves 3:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. Use the email.

## What the email does not say about the ports

The quoted lines do not cancel a port. Cruise Hive says there are no planned changes to the 5-night Bahamas itinerary. It lists Freeport on Monday 16 Nov from 11:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Great Stirrup Cay on Wednesday 18 Nov from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and a return to Jacksonville on Friday 20 Nov. It does not print a return clock.

The Travel's table has the same four dates and no hours. Those hours stay Cruise Hive's. They are not in the email quotes. The email does not print a call on 17 Nov or 19 Nov, and neither story prints one.

Cruise Hive says this is the regular inspection a foreign-flagged ship gets at the first U.S. port after a season abroad, and it says Dawn is flagged in the Bahamas. Those two lines are the article's. They are not in the email. The email's word is scheduled. It does not say the ship is being held.

The Travel spends much of its story on MSC Poesia's cancelled Miami cruise. That is a different ship and a different inspection. The post is [MSC cancels the 1 Nov Poesia sailing and refunds the fare](/news/msc-poesia-miami-sailing-cancelled-refund-credit-november-2026/). The Dawn email does not offer a refund or a future cruise credit. Neither story prints one.

## The cruise before this one

Cruise Hive says Dawn left Lisbon on Sunday 1 Nov and arrives in Jacksonville on 15 Nov, with Great Stirrup Cay on Friday 13 Nov along the way. The Travel calls that inbound trip a 14-night sailing from Lisbon and also puts Great Stirrup Cay on 13 Nov. That Friday call is the repositioning cruise, not a stop on the 15 Nov sailing.

## If you are booked on 15 Nov

Plan on a 5:00 p.m. sailaway and on the later arrival slot from the email. The 11:00 a.m. example becomes 1:00 p.m. Do not treat a public listing that still says 3:00 p.m. as newer than the email. Cruise Hive's port hours are that article's account of Freeport and Great Stirrup Cay. Read the email again before you leave for the terminal. This check found no Norwegian newsroom post that restates the 5:00 p.m. time.
