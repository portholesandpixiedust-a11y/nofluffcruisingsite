Eurodam's first Asia season is now in a Holland America release. [The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/holland-america-lines-2028-2029-asia-cruise-season-blooms-with-cherry-blossoms-and-five-new-ports-302946552.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Seattle, 20 Nov 2026, 5:32 a.m. ET.

The public wire page leads with a Holland America logo. An editor's note points to a photo library that asks you to log in. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/eurodam-to-make-asia-debut-for-holland-america-in-2028-29/) on 20 Nov shows a file named Eurodam-at-sea.jpg. The article does not name a photographer. This post uses neither image.

The season runs from September 2028 through May 2029. Cruises run from 11 to 27 days. You leave from Tokyo, Hong Kong, or Singapore. The release sends the ship through Japan, South Korea, China, Taiwan, the Philippines, and Southeast Asia. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/eurodam-to-make-asia-debut-for-holland-america-in-2028-29/) prints that same window and those same three cities.

This is a different release from yesterday's Alaska Cruisetour opening, [Holland America opens 2028 Alaska Cruisetours](/news/holland-america-2028-alaska-cruisetours-open/). Eurodam is in both. The ships and the regions are not the same offer.

## Five new ports, and two new overnights

The release names five ports it calls new.

- Coron, on Busuanga Island in the Philippines. The release points to the Twin Lagoons.
- Hirara, on Miyako Island in Japan. The release points to beaches.
- Wakayama, in Japan. The release points to Wakayama Castle and the temples at Koyasan.
- Magong, on Penghu Island in Taiwan. The release points to a basalt coast.
- Muara, as a gateway to Brunei. The release names the Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque.

Overnights and late departures cover 14 ports. The overnight names in the release are Bali, Halong Bay, Hong Kong, Incheon (Seoul), Keelung (Taipei), Laem Chabang (Bangkok), Manila, Hiroshima, Osaka, Maizuru, Phú Mỹ (Ho Chi Minh City), Shanghai, and Singapore. The release names Halong Bay twice in that sentence. The new overnights, as the release labels them, are Keelung and Phú Mỹ.

Late departures add time in Fukuoka, Hiroshima, Maizuru, Osaka, Halong Bay, and Shanghai. The release does not print the clocks.

Spring sailings are the cherry-blossom window. The release names one shore excursion from Kobe, to Himeji Castle and Koko-en Garden. It does not print a price for that tour.

## The sailings the release dates

Paul Grigsby, vice president of deployment and revenue planning, is the executive in the quote. These are the dated trips in the release.

- Circle Japan, 11 or 14 days, roundtrip or between Tokyo and Yokohama. The 14-day cruises visit up to 10 ports, with some longer calls in Hiroshima. The 11-day cruise on 22 Apr 2029 overnights in Maizuru.
- Two 14-day Japan and South Korea cruises, with an overnight in Incheon and longer calls in Osaka, Fukuoka, or both. An 11-day Southern Japan and Jeju cruise leaves 3 May 2029.
- A 14-day cruise on 5 Nov 2028, Tokyo to Hong Kong, with overnights in Osaka and Keelung. A 14-day China and Japan cruise on 25 Feb 2029 overnights in Shanghai and Osaka.
- A 14-day cruise on 19 Nov 2028, Hong Kong to Singapore, with overnights in Laem Chabang and Halong Bay. A 17-day holiday cruise on 17 Dec adds Nha Trang and Kota Kinabalu. The release does not print the year on 17 Dec.
- A 14-day cruise on 3 Dec, Singapore to Hong Kong, six ports, with overnights in Laem Chabang and Manila. The release does not print the year on 3 Dec.
- A 13-day crossing from Vancouver to Tokyo, with Ketchikan and Inside Passage scenic cruising. A 14-day crossing from Tokyo to Seattle, with Kushiro and three Alaska ports. The release does not name those three ports.

You can join some cruises back to back. The release calls those Collectors' Voyages and does not list them.

## The 27-day voyage was already on a 16 Nov page

The 20 Nov release includes a 27-day Coffee, Tea and Spice Route. It leaves 3 Jan, roundtrip from Singapore, calls 13 ports in seven countries, and overnights in Bali, Hong Kong, and Phú Mỹ. It does not print the other ports, and it does not print the year on that 3 Jan date. Cruise Industry News calls this voyage recently announced and does not print the port list.

[Holland America's 16 Nov page](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/us/news/2026/November/news-11-16-2026-coffee-tea-spice) is the one with the ports. It dates the cruise 3 Jan to 30 Jan 2029. After Singapore it lists Jakarta, Semarang (Borobudur), two days in Bali, Muara, Kota Kinabalu, Manila, Kaohsiung, Naha on Okinawa, Ishigaki, an overnight in Hong Kong, Nha Trang, and an overnight at Phu My. It says bookings open later that week. The 20 Nov release does not print that sentence, and it does not print a fare.

The 16 Nov page also names Art of Tea and Beukenhorst for drinks on board. The 20 Nov release says the ship will carry regionally inspired tea and coffee, and it does not print those two names. Grigsby's title on the 16 Nov page is vice president of itinerary planning and deployment. The 20 Nov release uses the deployment and revenue planning title. This post does not merge the titles.

## The bonus, with no end date

For a limited time, a 2028-2029 Asia booking with Have It All includes shore excursions, specialty dining, a Signature Beverage Package, and Surf Wi-Fi. The release adds free prepaid crew appreciation and free upgrades to the Elite Beverage Package and Premium Wi-Fi. It does not print an end date. It does not print a dollar value for the excursions, the dining, the drinks, or the Wi-Fi.

Mariner Society members can get up to $400 onboard credit per stateroom. The release does not split that figure by tier. The 16 Nov page used the same bonus language for the 27-day voyage alone. The 20 Nov release puts it on the Asia season.

Menus in the release include Japanese rockfish and sushi, Korean bibimbap, Thai mango sticky rice, Filipino chicken adobo, matcha lava cake, Indonesian coconut pancakes, and Vietnamese spring rolls. That list is the line's. It is not a price.
