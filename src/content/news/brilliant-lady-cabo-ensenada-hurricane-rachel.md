Virgin has made the call it was holding. Brilliant Lady's 3 Oct 2026 sailing from Los Angeles does not go to Cabo San Lucas. [Virgin's notice](https://www.virginvoyages.com/travel-advisories/stories/weather-update-for-brilliant-lady), updated 2 Oct 2026 and still dated that way on 5 Oct, swaps that stop for Ensenada.

The earlier post on this site said no decision had been made: [Brilliant Lady's 3 Oct sailing still lists Mexico while Virgin watches Hurricane Rachel](/news/brilliant-lady-hurricane-rachel-october-3-backup/). That was the 29 Sep hold. This is the final update Virgin posted after it.

If you are on the ship, Ensenada was yesterday. Today, Monday 5 Oct, is the sea day that replaced Cabo. Mazatlán is tomorrow, on a later clock.

## The table Virgin printed

Original week on that page: Los Angeles Saturday 3 Oct, 6:30am to 5:00pm. A sea day Sunday. Cabo San Lucas Monday 5 Oct, 1:00pm to 8:00pm. Mazatlán Tuesday 6 Oct, 9:00am to 6:00pm. Puerto Vallarta Wednesday 7 Oct, 8:30am to 5:00pm. Sea days Thursday and Friday. Los Angeles Saturday 10 Oct, 6:30am to 5:00pm.

Updated week on the same page:

- Saturday 3 Oct: Los Angeles, 6:30am to 5:00pm
- Sunday 4 Oct: Ensenada, 7:30am to 2:00pm
- Monday 5 Oct: sea day
- Tuesday 6 Oct: Mazatlán, 11:00am to 8:00pm
- Wednesday 7 Oct: Puerto Vallarta, 8:30am to 5:00pm
- Thursday 8 Oct: sea day
- Friday 9 Oct: sea day
- Saturday 10 Oct: Los Angeles, 6:30am to 5:00pm

Virgin's short version says the Mazatlán call now arrives at 11:00am and leaves at 8:00pm. [Cruise.Blog](https://cruise.blog/2026/10/hurricane-rachel-cruise-itinerary-changes-mexico), dated 2 Oct, prints the same Mazatlán window and the same port order. The 7:30am to 2:00pm Ensenada window is on Virgin's table. It is not in Cruise.Blog's bullet list.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/brilliant-lady-drops-port-as-hurricane-rachel-swells-linger/219644), also dated 2 Oct, has the Cabo cut and the Ensenada add. It says the rest of the itinerary is unchanged. That does not match Virgin's Mazatlán hours. Use Virgin's table.

A later paragraph on Virgin's page says everything else about the voyage stays as planned. Puerto Vallarta's hours match the original table. The Los Angeles return matches too. The pieces that moved are Cabo, Ensenada, the Monday sea day, and Mazatlán's clock.

## Why Cabo is off

Virgin's wording: residual swell from Hurricane Rachel is strong enough that tendering into Cabo San Lucas would not be safe or comfortable. The same notice says Rachel has moved away from the route and is no longer a risk to the voyage.

Virgin also says no packing change is needed, because the trip stays in warm-weather ports. That replaces the jacket advice in the 29 Sep backup note. Ensenada is not a sail north to San Francisco.

The 26 Sep sailing, the one Polo rerouted, is a different cruise: [Brilliant Lady drops Mexico ports for a California week after Polo](/news/brilliant-lady-hurricane-polo-california-reroute/).

Cruise.Blog adds storm and port lines that are not in Virgin's notice. It cites Surfer.com on 20-to-25-foot swells, a Friday morning National Hurricane Center wind figure, and a U.S. Embassy note that Cabo closed on 30 Sep. Cruise Hive says the port had been closed since 30 Sep with no reopening date, and that Brilliant Lady's next Los Angeles departure, on 10 Oct, was still scheduled to call Cabo on 12 Oct. Those next-sailing and closure lines are Cruise Hive's. They are not a Virgin promise about the 10 Oct cruise.

## Cabo tours

Virgin says Shore Things you booked in Cabo San Lucas refund automatically to the original form of payment. Ensenada Shore Things were listed as available to book soon in the Virgin app. If you already had Sunday ashore, that window was 7:30am to 2:00pm.

A tour you bought from someone other than Virgin is not in that automatic refund. Virgin tells you to contact the operator.

The notice does not mention a fare refund or an onboard credit for losing Cabo.

## What this means for you

If you are on the 3 Oct sailing, treat Monday as a sea day and Tuesday as Mazatlán from 11:00am to 8:00pm. Puerto Vallarta is still Wednesday, 8:30am to 5:00pm. You are back in Los Angeles Saturday 10 Oct, 6:30am to 5:00pm, which is the same return window as the original table. Watch the card that paid for a Cabo Shore Thing. Leave an outside Cabo deposit to the company that sold it.

If you are on the 10 Oct departure, Virgin's 2 Oct notice does not change your Cabo day. Cruise Hive's note that 12 Oct was still on the schedule is that outlet's check, not a new Virgin letter.

Facts above follow Virgin's 2 Oct 2026 notice, rechecked 5 Oct 2026, with Cruise.Blog and Cruise Hive the same day.
