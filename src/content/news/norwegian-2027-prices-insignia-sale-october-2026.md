Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings put a guest-facing update in its [29 Oct 2026 earnings release](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/news-events/press-releases/detail/835/norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-reports-third-quarter-2026), Miami, 6:30 a.m. EDT. Prices for 2027 are ahead of last year at this point in the year. First-half bookings are still behind last year. Second-half bookings are in line.

The photo on this page is the Norwegian Aura float-out file [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results/) used on its earnings write-up. The hull in the frame reads Norwegian Aura. The NCLH release does not include that photo. This post does not treat the frame as Oceania Insignia, Oceania Sirena, Seven Seas Prestige, or Seven Seas Voyager.

## What 2027 prices mean for a booking

The release splits the year.

| 2027 window | Price versus the same time last year | Booked position versus last year |
|---|---|---|
| First half | Ahead | Below |
| Second half | Ahead | In line |

John W. Chidsey, chair and chief executive, expects net yield growth of 1% to 2% in 2027, with a meaningful acceleration in the back half. Net yield is the company's revenue measure. It is not a fare on your invoice. The release prints no cabin price.

The booked position for the next 12 months has improved modestly since the last earnings release. It is still below the range the company calls optimal. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/finance-legal-regulatory/nclh-s-q3-outperforms-full-year-guidance-goes-up), Anne Kalosh, 29 Oct, prints the same split.

If you are shopping a 2027 Norwegian cruise, the company is not describing a broad price cut. A first-half sailing can still have more open space than it did last year. The price they are comparing is already ahead. Check the live fare. This release is not a coupon.

The same release says the base-loading approach is being applied across 2027 and 2028 sailings. What that method is, and what Chidsey said about it in July, is in [Norwegian is pricing cruises earlier](/news/norwegian-cruise-line-earlier-competitive-pricing-2026/). Today's release does not restate a price-drop rule.

## Promos and air

The release announces additional run-rate savings of about $100 million. The figure is printed with a tilde. The work is guest and crew air optimization, and other tactical changes in promotional offerings. Those changes phase in through 2028.

The release does not name a promo that is ending. It does not say a fare you already hold is void. If you are sitting on a promotional offer, this paragraph is a warning to read the next offer sheet, not a cancellation of the one in your booking.

## Insignia and Sirena

Two Oceania ships are in the highlights.

The company entered a memorandum of agreement to sell Oceania Insignia. Delivery to the buyer is expected in September 2027. If you hold an Insignia cruise after that month, ask the line whether that sailing is still on Insignia. The release does not list the sailings.

Seatrade says Phoenix Reisen has announced it will operate the ship as Alvara. That name is Seatrade's, on 29 Oct. It is not in the NCLH highlights checked today.

The sale of Oceania Sirena is complete. A charter keeps the company operating the ship through spring 2028. Seatrade calls that sale previously announced, and says the ship is going to Ambassador Cruise Line. If your Sirena cruise is before that charter ends, the release still has Oceania operating the ship. If it is after spring 2028, do not assume the same ship and the same brand.

## Prestige and Voyager

The fourth-quarter net yield guide stays at a decline of about 6.5% in constant currency. The release says a better underlying result was offset by two items. Delivery of Seven Seas Prestige is delayed to late December. Select voyages on Seven Seas Voyager that were to transit the Suez Canal are cancelled.

The release does not name those voyages. It does not print a refund.

Prestige's maiden date was already public. [Regent's 6 Oct 2026 release](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/news-events/press-releases/detail/830/regent-seven-seas-cruises-unveils-new-bar-and-beverage) says the maiden leaves Miami on 28 Dec 2026 for 11 nights, roundtrip, with calls in Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras, Jamaica, and the Cayman Islands. Today's earnings line does not replace that date, and it does not add a new cancellation list. If you were moved off an earlier Prestige sailing, use the letter Regent already sent you.

If you are booked on Seven Seas Voyager through the Suez, open that booking. The 29 Oct release says select transits are cancelled. It does not say which ones.

Great Tides Waterpark is in the release as opened on 4 Sep 2026 at Great Stirrup Cay. That opening is not new today. This post does not restate the slide list.

## What you should do

If you are pricing a 2027 Norwegian cruise, compare the live fare with what you are willing to pay. The company says prices are ahead of last year, and it expects yield to firm, more so in the second half. A first-half sailing may still have more unsold space than last year. That is not a published discount.

If you hold Insignia past September 2027, or Sirena past spring 2028, ask whether your ship is still the one in the brochure. If you hold a Voyager Suez transit, confirm the port list in the app or with the line. Facts above follow the NCLH release at 6:30 a.m. EDT on 29 Oct 2026, Seatrade the same day for the buyer names, and Regent's 6 Oct release for the Miami maiden date.
