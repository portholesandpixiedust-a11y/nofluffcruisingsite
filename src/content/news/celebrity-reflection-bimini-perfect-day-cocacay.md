Celebrity Reflection is not calling at Bimini on the sailing that left Fort Lauderdale on Sunday 4 Oct 2026. The line's guest letter blames a berth conflict and puts Friday at Perfect Day at CocoCay instead.

If you are onboard, today is the sea day on that revised table. The new island day is Friday.

## The week Celebrity printed

The letter calls this the revised 4 Oct 2026 itinerary:

- Sunday 4 Oct: depart Fort Lauderdale at 4:00pm
- Monday 5 Oct: sea day
- Tuesday 6 Oct: George Town, Grand Cayman, 7:00am to 4:00pm
- Wednesday 7 Oct: Cozumel, 10:00am to 8:00pm
- Thursday 8 Oct: sea day
- Friday 9 Oct: Perfect Day at CocoCay, 7:00am to 5:00pm
- Saturday 10 Oct: Fort Lauderdale at 7:00am

Bimini is not on that list. The letter does not say which day Bimini used to be, or what the old hours were. It does not name the other ship in the berth conflict. Do not fill those blanks from a guess.

Friday is a full day at CocoCay, 7:00am to 5:00pm. The letter describes the call as Perfect Day at CocoCay. It mentions an overwater cabana, the Oasis Lagoon pool, Adventure Pool, and Daredevil's Peak. It does not print a price for any of those.

## The Bimini tours

If you bought a Bimini shore excursion from Celebrity, the letter says you do not have to do anything. Celebrity will refund it to the original form of payment.

The letter tells you to look at other excursions in the Celebrity app or on My Celebrity Cruises. It does not say a CocoCay booking is automatic. If you want a reserved spot on Friday, open the app and see what is still for sale.

The letter does not mention a fare refund or an onboard credit for losing Bimini.

A tour you bought from someone other than Celebrity is not covered by that automatic refund. Contact that operator.

## What this means for you

If you are on this 4 Oct Reflection cruise, keep Grand Cayman on Tuesday and Cozumel on Wednesday at the hours above. Plan Friday as CocoCay, not Bimini. Let a Celebrity Bimini excursion refund itself, and check the card it was bought on. Independent Bimini deposits are your call to cancel.

The return stays Saturday 10 Oct at 7:00am. A flight off that arrival is still the one on the letter. The letter does not move debarkation.
