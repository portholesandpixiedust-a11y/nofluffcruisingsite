Oceania has priced a new classroom on Marina. [The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oceania-cruises-unveils-new-chefs-studio-aboard-oceania-marina-302945184.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Miami, 19 Nov 2026, 9:00 a.m. ET.

The photo on this page is the file on that release, Oceania-Cruises-Image.jpg. The release says Credit: Oceania Cruises. It does not name a photographer. The caption reads "The new Chef's Studio aboard Oceania Marina." The frame shows marble-topped tables, blue chairs, a demonstration counter, two dark screens, and shelves along the windows. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/oceania-unveils-new-chefs-studio-on-marina/) on 19 Nov uses a wider crop of the same room and does not name a photographer. This post uses the release file.

## What opens on 25 Nov

The studio starts welcoming guests on 25 Nov 2026. It holds 18 guests. The release calls the sessions workshops, seminars, and Test Kitchen experiences, led by the line's chef instructors.

The priced piece is the Test Kitchen Series. A class is one hour, on a sea day, at $49 a person. The named topics are The Spice of Life, knife skills, pantry organization, cooking with high heat, and destination classes on regional ingredients, traditions, and cooking practices. The release does not print which class runs on which sailing. It does not print a deck number.

Jason Montague, chief luxury officer, says the studio complements The Culinary Center and is a different style of class. The release says Marina opened with The Culinary Center 15 years ago, and that the studio follows a bow-to-stern refurbishment last month. It does not list that yard work. Kathryn Kelly, executive chef and director of culinary enrichment, is quoted on knife skills, regional ingredients, and the shoreside tours. Cruise Industry News prints Montague's lines and the same $49 price. It does not add a fee the release omits.

This is separate from the percent-off sale already on this site, [Oceania takes up to 50% off 2027 voyages through 1 Dec](/news/oceania-black-friday-50-percent-2027-voyages/). That post is the fare. This one is the $49 class.

## Tours, and the later ships

The studio is also the meeting room for Culinary Discovery Tours. Guests gather there before a small-group tour, then come back with the chef for a glass of wine and questions. The release does not print a price for the tour.

In 2027 the line expects more than 100 chef-led Culinary Discovery Tours, and it names Iceland, Australia, and Uruguay. The Chef's Studio program expands next year onto Oceania Riviera, and it debuts on Oceania Sonata and Oceania Aurelia. The release does not print a month for those ships.

To see the classes on a booking, the release says OceaniaCruises.com, 855-OCEANIA, or a travel advisor. If you are on Marina on or after 25 Nov, the $49 line is for a sea-day class, and the release does not say the class is included in the fare.
