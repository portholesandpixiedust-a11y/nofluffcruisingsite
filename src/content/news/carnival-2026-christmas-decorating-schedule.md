If your Carnival sailing leaves between late November and New Year's Eve, the date that matters is the first decorated departure for that ship. Leave before it, and the Christmas displays should not be up. Leave on that date or later, through 31 Dec, and they should.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-reveals-when-christmas-decorations-go-up-on-all-29-ships/221386), Melissa Mayntz, 19 Oct 2026, reports the schedule Carnival brand ambassador John Heald posted to Facebook. [Cruise Fever](https://cruisefever.net/when-each-carnival-cruise-ship-will-be-decorated-for-christmas-in-2026/), Ben Souza, 20 Oct 2026, prints the same fleet list with one date that does not match. This desk did not open the Facebook post. The dates below are what those two reports say Heald posted. No Carnival newsroom page for the schedule was found on this check.

## First decorated departure

Cruise Hive says each date is the first departure that will have the decorations. This desk counted 29 names in that list. Cruise Hive calls it the full fleet.

| First decorated departure | Ships |
|---|---|
| Sunday 22 Nov | Encounter (Brisbane) |
| Friday 27 Nov | Conquest, Glory, Firenze |
| Saturday 28 Nov | Magic, Mardi Gras, Paradise, Radiance, Jubilee, Sunshine, Splendor |
| Sunday 29 Nov | Celebration, Venezia, Legend, Panorama, Liberty, Pride |
| Monday 30 Nov | Freedom, Spirit, Breeze, Adventure |
| Thursday 3 Dec | Sunrise, Miracle, Valor, Elation |
| Saturday 5 Dec | Horizon, Dream, and Vista in the Cruise Hive list |
| Monday 14 Dec | Luminosa (Brisbane) |

Cruise Fever matches those rows, with two differences. It puts [Carnival Vista](/ships/carnival/carnival-vista/) on Sunday 6 Dec, alone, and it keeps [Carnival Horizon](/ships/carnival/carnival-horizon/) and [Carnival Dream](/ships/carnival/carnival-dream/) on 5 Dec. It also spells Firenze as Frienze. Cruise Hive spells it Firenze and groups Vista with Horizon and Dream on 5 Dec. If you are on Vista, 5 Dec is the Cruise Hive date and 6 Dec is the Cruise Fever date. This desk cannot see Heald's post, so it cannot pick a winner.

Cruise Fever files Splendor on 28 Nov and Adventure on 30 Nov under Australia, which is the same pairing Cruise Hive prints. It does not move any other ship.

## The first ship and the last ship

Cruise Hive says the first decorated departure is Carnival Encounter on Sunday 22 Nov from Brisbane. That cruise is six nights and calls Airlie Beach, Cairns, and Willis Island.

The last ship in both reports is [Carnival Luminosa](/ships/carnival/carnival-luminosa/), on Monday 14 Dec. Cruise Hive says that departure is a four-night cruise from Brisbane to Airlie Beach, and that it is the first sailing from the Australia homeport after a dry dock in Singapore from 23 Oct to late November. Cruise Fever prints 14 Dec for Luminosa and does not print the dry dock or the Airlie Beach call. The Singapore dates are Cruise Hive's account of the Heald post. They are not in the 15 Oct newsroom note already on this site, [Carnival Luminosa and Adventure leave Australia in March 2028](/news/carnival-luminosa-adventure-leave-australia-march-2028/). That post is about the ships leaving Australia. It is not this decorating schedule.

## What stays up, and who hangs it

Both reports say the decorations stay up through the New Year's Eve cruises. Cruise Hive says Heald confirmed that after guests asked when the displays come down. The same article says the decorations are not changed specifically for New Year's. Most ships will offer countdowns and balloon drops for 2027. On or after 1 Jan, some decorations may start coming down. Crew pack them so they can come off at the next turnaround.

Cruise Hive says the crew does not hang the displays. A different team boards at the homeport. When the decorations go up, Christmas music joins the ship's music. Cruise Fever also says professional decorators come aboard. It does not print the music line or the 1 Jan takedown.

Cruise Fever adds a Hanukkah line that is not in the Cruise Hive report: a nightly menorah lighting from 4 Dec through 12 Dec 2026. Treat that as Cruise Fever's report, not as a date in the Cruise Hive list.

## What this means for you

Find your ship in the table. If you sail before that date, do not expect the Christmas displays. If you sail from that date through 31 Dec, the reports say the displays are up, including on New Year's Eve. Vista is the exception to treat as unresolved: 5 Dec in Cruise Hive, 6 Dec in Cruise Fever. The team that decorates boards at the homeport, so a call in the middle of a cruise is not when the trees go up. Facts above follow Cruise Hive on 19 Oct 2026 and Cruise Fever on 20 Oct 2026.
