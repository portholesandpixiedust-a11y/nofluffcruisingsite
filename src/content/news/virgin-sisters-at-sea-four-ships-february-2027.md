Virgin Voyages published its first four-ship Sisters at Sea meetup for 16 Feb 2027. The line's own Ahoy story dated 17 Sep 2026 is the Tier 1 source for the date, ships, and coordinates.

## Which ships and where

Scarlet Lady, Valiant Lady, Resilient Lady, and Brilliant Lady all join the same point in the Caribbean. Virgin listed the coordinates as 22°12'57\"N, 77°16'07\"W. That puts the gathering about 38 nautical miles north of Nuevitas, Cuba.

You will not step onto Cuban soil for this event as described. The meetup is at sea. Shared celebrations run across the four ships. Virgin said more programming details will follow.

## Why this matters for bookings

Four-ship meetups are rare for Virgin. Until now the brand has staged smaller Sisters at Sea moments. Putting the whole fleet in one ocean square is a marketing and photo moment, and it can tighten inventory on those sailings once cabin hunters notice.

If you already hold a February 2027 sailing on any of the four ships, check whether your itinerary still lines up with 16 Feb. Itineraries can shift after publication. Confirm the date still sits mid-cruise for your cabin before you plan private events around it.

## What is still unknown

Virgin has not published a full hour-by-hour program in the Ahoy post. Expect announcements on cross-ship entertainment, photo opportunities, and any limited-time bars or menus closer to sailing. Until those land, treat the meetup as a confirmed date and location, not a finished schedule.
