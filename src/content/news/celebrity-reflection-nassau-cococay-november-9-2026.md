Celebrity Reflection is not calling at Nassau on the sailing that left Fort Lauderdale on Monday 9 Nov 2026. [The line's guest letter](https://www.celebritycruises.com/content/dam/celebrity/pdf/RF_11-9-26_guest-letter.pdf) blames a berth conflict and puts Wednesday at Perfect Day at CocoCay instead.

The PDF has no photo. The picture on this page is Celebrity Reflection in Piraeus, from Wikimedia Commons. It is not a photo of this cruise.

If you are on this sailing, the letter's table puts the ship in Key West today, Tuesday 10 Nov.

## The week the letter prints

The letter calls this the revised 9 Nov 2026 itinerary:

- Monday 9 Nov: depart Fort Lauderdale at 4:00 p.m.
- Tuesday 10 Nov: Key West, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Wednesday 11 Nov: Perfect Day at CocoCay, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Thursday 12 Nov: sea day
- Friday 13 Nov: arrive Fort Lauderdale at 7:00 a.m.

The letter says the CocoCay call is followed by a sea day on Thursday, then the return. It does not print Nassau's old day or Nassau's old hours. Do not fill those in from an old brochure.

## What you get back

If you bought a Nassau shore excursion from Celebrity, the letter says you do not need to do anything. The refund goes to the original form of payment. Replacement tours are in the Celebrity app or on My Celebrity Cruises.

A tour you booked with someone other than Celebrity is not in that sentence. Ask that operator.

The letter does not print a fare refund. It does not print onboard credit. It does not print a price for a CocoCay cabana or a slide. The other ship in the berth conflict is not named.

## A different Reflection cruise

This is not the 4 Oct sailing. That one dropped Bimini for a Friday at CocoCay. The post is [Celebrity Reflection drops Bimini for a Friday at CocoCay](/news/celebrity-reflection-bimini-perfect-day-cocacay/). The letter on this page is the 9 Nov cruise, and the port it drops is Nassau.

No Cruise Hive, Seatrade, Cruise Industry News, Cruise Critic, or Royal Caribbean Blog story of this letter was open when this page was checked on 10 Nov 2026. The PDF is the source.

## If you are booked

Tuesday on this table is Key West, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Wednesday is CocoCay, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Thursday is a sea day. Friday's arrival in the letter is 7:00 a.m. Match a pickup to that clock. The letter does not say the fare changed.
