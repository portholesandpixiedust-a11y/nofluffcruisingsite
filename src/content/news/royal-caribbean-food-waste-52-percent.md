The reduction comes from predictive modelling rather than portion cuts, which is the same AI forecasting the company's leadership has described publicly. It can now forecast burger demand in fifteen-minute windows on a given ship.

Worth knowing what this is not: a change you will notice at the buffet. It is a supply-side efficiency, and the savings stay with the company.
