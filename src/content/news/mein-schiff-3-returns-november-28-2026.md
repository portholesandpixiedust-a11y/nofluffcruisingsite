Mein Schiff 3 is set to sail on 28 Nov 2026. [TUI's English notice](https://www.meinschiff.com/en/current-information), in the section Latest information on Mein Schiff 3, is stamped 19.11.2026 at 14:00 am. The page does not name a time zone. It prints am next to 14:00.

The notice says the ship resumes cruise operations on 28 November. The trip is a four-night short cruise from Bremerhaven via Oslo to Kiel. The notice does not print a departure clock or an arrival date.

The page has no ship photo of this notice. This post has no separate image.

## What changed since 5 Nov

On 5 Nov, TUI said that, as things stood, regular service would resume on 28 Nov. That notice did not name Oslo or Kiel. The cancellation post, [TUI cancels Mein Schiff 3's 14 Nov Norway cruise](/news/mein-schiff-3-norway-cruise-cancelled-november-14-2026/), has that stamp. Today's notice is the line naming the four-night route.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/mein-schiff-3-nov-28-sailing-to-go-ahead-as-planned/) on 19 Nov prints the same date, the same four nights, and Bremerhaven to Kiel via Oslo. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ship-operations/tui-cruises-confirms-mein-schiff-3-s-return-to-service-date), Anne Kalosh, on 20 Nov, says the line confirmed on Thursday a four-night sailing on 28 Nov from Bremerhaven to Kiel. Thursday was 19 Nov. Seatrade's opening lines do not print Oslo. Use the TUI notice, and Cruise Industry News, for Oslo.

## What this notice does not do

The 14 to 28 Nov cruise stays cancelled. The 19 Nov notice does not mention it, and it does not restore it. The 5 Nov post is still the cancellation.

The notice prints no fare, no refund, and no credit. Cruise Industry News and Seatrade do not print a refund either. If you are waiting on money from the Norway cruise, this page does not answer it.

Seatrade still describes the yard stay and the 25 Oct collision. Those facts are already on [Mein Schiff 3 moves to Lloyd Werft](/news/mein-schiff-3-lloyd-werft-next-cruises-open-october-2026/) and on the 5 Nov cancellation post. This note does not reopen them.

## If you hold 28 Nov

Read the booking for the clock. TUI's notice says the cruise sails, and it says the route is Bremerhaven via Oslo to Kiel, four nights. It does not say what time you board, and it does not say what day you reach Kiel.
