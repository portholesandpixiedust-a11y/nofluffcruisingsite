Aman at Sea has put twelve more Caribbean voyages on sale for Amangati. They run from 2 Jan through 20 March 2028. If you were waiting on the part of the first Caribbean season that sits after New Year, this is that list.

[Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/people-opinions/aman-at-sea-taps-capt-ertan-vasvi-extends-caribbean-season), Anne Kalosh, 20 Oct 2026, calls them a dozen itineraries added on top of the autumn 2027 program announced in June. The [Aman at Sea site](https://www.amanatsea.com/), read 21 Oct 2026, says twelve new voyages. [an Bord](https://anbord.de/aman-at-sea-extends-amangatis-inaugural-caribbean-season-into-2028/) on 21 Oct carries an Aman at Sea press text with the same twelve and the 2 Jan start. No Aman newsroom URL turned up on this check beyond the line's own site.

| Guests | 94 | Aman site. Seatrade quotes the captain on 94 passengers |

[Robb Report](https://robbreport.com/travel/destinations/aman-amangati-cruise-ship-caribbean-itineraries-1238780844/), Rachel Cormack, 21 Oct 2026, says Amangati will operate 19 cruises from 21 Nov 2027 through 20 March 2028. That span starts with the autumn program, not with 2 Jan. Twelve is the number of voyages added for the winter. Nineteen is Robb Report's count for the whole Caribbean season in that window. This desk did not add an autumn count to twelve, and it did not open a booking grid. Use twelve when you mean the new winter list.

## Where the winter voyages go

Seatrade and the press text split the new sailings into four groups. Ports below are the ones both of those accounts name. The press text is the one that dates the winter block to 2 Jan.

Greater Antilles voyages go west in January, from St. Maarten and the British Virgin Islands to Hispaniola. Samana, Cap Cana, and La Romana are the Dominican Republic calls. Seatrade says those calls add the Dominican Republic to the program.

Windward Islands voyages leave St. John's, Antigua, and Bridgetown, Barbados. New names in this winter list are Saint-Pierre in Martinique, Marie-Galante in Guadeloupe, and Bequia. Returning calls are Portsmouth in Dominica, Soufriere in St. Lucia, Anse d'Arlet in Martinique, and Canouan in the Grenadines.

Leeward Islands voyages use Little Bay in Montserrat, Basseterre in St. Kitts, Gustavia, Jost Van Dyke, Virgin Gorda, the Prickly Pear Cays off Anguilla, Philipsburg, and a Marina Day at Barbuda.

Dutch Caribbean voyages leave Bridgetown and Oranjestad. They call the Tobago Cays and St. George's, Grenada, then Willemstad in Curacao and Kralendijk in Bonaire.

The pattern Seatrade prints for the whole season is late departures, overnight calls, and a preference for anchorages over berths. Shore plans are cultural, culinary, and naturalist-led, plus bespoke trips. Seatrade says further details are still to come. The press text says the autumn chapter, which is the earlier program, ends with a New Year celebration at a private resort in Nevis and a two-night anchorage. That Nevis call is in the press text. It is not in the 20 Oct Seatrade article.

## The ship, and who is in command

The Aman site says the yacht carries 94 guests in 47 suites. Seatrade quotes Capt. Ertan Vasvi on a ship of just 94 passengers. Those two 94s match. Vasvi also says he looks forward to welcoming the first guests in May 2027. That month is his quotation in Seatrade. The Aman homepage title says sailing in spring 2027 and does not print May.

Seatrade says Vasvi will command Amangati, stay with the build at T. Mariotti, and train the deck and bridge teams. It says he has been with Seabourn since 2018, spent two years at the yard for Seabourn Venture and Seabourn Pursuit, has held command since 2023, and was with Silversea from 2009 to 2018. Robb Report says the yard is in Genoa and calls the yacht 600 feet, with suite sizes from 731 to 3,811 square feet. Those measurements are Robb Report's. They are not on the Aman page read for this post, and they are not in the Seatrade article.

The picture on this page is the aerial rendering the press text credits to Aman at Sea. Seatrade's own photo is of the captain.

## Mediterranean endings and the Atlantic crossings

Four Mediterranean voyages are now on sale, in both Seatrade and the press text. Seatrade does not print the year. The press text places them ahead of the Caribbean season, so they sit in 2027 if you are reading them against the 21 Nov 2027 Caribbean start already on the books from June.

| Voyage | Dates in both reports | Route Seatrade prints |
|---|---|---|
| Aegean Treasures | 10-18 Oct | Peloponnese, then Dubrovnik and Kotor |
| Italian Flavours | 18-24 Oct | Bari, through the Ionian, to Sicily |
| Coastal Highlights | 24 Oct-1 Nov | Palermo, Naples, Rome, the Ligurian shore, the Cote d'Azur |
| Western Mediterranean | 1-8 Nov | Catalan and Valencian coast, then Andalusia, arriving Malaga |

The press text calls the westbound Atlantic Passage 13 nights, from Malaga through Cadiz and Ponta Delgada to Antigua. Seatrade's October article does not print that night count. It does print the return: Amangati leaves St. John's on 20 March 2028, calls Philipsburg and Gustavia, and sails 11 nights to Funchal, Madeira. The press text adds an arrival on 31 March 2028. That arrival date is the press text. Seatrade stops at the 11-night length.

## How to ask for a booking

The Aman site lists three phone lines: +1-833-833-2061 for the U.S. yacht concierge, +1-754-345-2339 for the U.S. and international line, and +44-2046283130 for EMEA, plus info@amanatsea.com. The press text prints the 754 number and the email. No report read for this post prints a fare or a deposit. Open the specific voyage before you treat any price as final.

## What this means for you

If you want Amangati after New Year 2028, the new inventory is twelve voyages from 2 Jan to 20 March, five to eight nights, with the Dominican Republic, Saint-Pierre, Marie-Galante, and Bequia among the calls that were not in the earlier Caribbean announcement. The guest count to quote is 94, in 47 suites. Do not quote 19 voyages unless you mean Robb Report's figure for the whole run from 21 Nov 2027. The first guests, in the captain's words, are in May 2027. A delivery date can still move. None of these pages print a fare.
