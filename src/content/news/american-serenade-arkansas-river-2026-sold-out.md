The 2026 Arkansas River cruises are sold out in American Cruise Lines' own release. The Serenade page still shows a price for 15 Nov.

[The release](https://www.americancruiselines.com/media/american-celebrates-first-arkansas-river-cruise) is datelined Guilford, 10 Nov 2026. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/american-cruise-lines-new-arkansas-river-cruise-sets-sail), Anne Kalosh, published the same itinerary on 11 Nov. The ship in both is American Serenade.

The photo is the lead image on the Seatrade story. American Cruise Lines is the credit. The bow lettering reads American Symphony. The Serenade page calls Symphony the sister ship. This is not a photo of the Arkansas inaugural.

## The two clocks in the release

The release says the first Arkansas River cruise set sail yesterday. The dateline is 10 Nov, so yesterday in that sentence is 9 Nov. The next paragraph says the inaugural began on 8 Nov with an included hotel stay in Memphis. The release does not print a clock for leaving the dock. Keep both sentences when you plan a transfer.

The second sailing begins 15 Nov with an included hotel stay in Tulsa. Seatrade prints the same hotel nights.

## Where the 9-day cruise goes

Most of the 9-day cruises run between Tulsa and Little Rock. The stops named in the release and in Seatrade are Muskogee, Fort Smith/Van Buren, and Dardanelle, plus Little Rock. A number of cruises run between Memphis and Tulsa and also visit Little Rock.

The shore time named in both is Little Rock explorations, Art Deco walking tours in Tulsa, a scenic train ride through the Boston Mountains, and a Muskogee stop tied to the song Okie from Muskogee. The release does not print a price for any of those.

Charles B. Robertson, president and CEO, is the executive in the quote. Seatrade's version of that quote hyphenates world-class and prints USA. The release spells worldclass as one word and prints U.S.A. The quote is the release's.

## Sold out, and the price still on the page

The release says the first two November 2026 dates sold out almost as soon as they were announced in late 2025. It then says the 2026 sailings are already sold out. Seatrade prints the first half of that, the two dates this month, and says the line said 2027 dates are already selling out.

The [Serenade page](https://www.americancruiselines.com/ships/american-riverboat-cruise-ships/american-serenade), read 12 Nov 2026, still lists an Arkansas River cruise on 15 Nov 2026 from $6,575. It does not print sold out next to that row. It does not say whether $6,575 is per person. Call before you treat that row as an open cabin. The phone on the release page is 800-460-4518.

The same ship page lists 180 guest capacity. The release does not print a guest count. Seatrade does not either.

## If you want a later year

The release says next year has two departures in May 2027 and four more in November. It says the 2028 Arkansas schedule more than triples, with departures in March, April, May, October, November, and December. It says cruises through 2029 are on the company website. It does not print a 2027 fare. Seatrade does not print those counts.
