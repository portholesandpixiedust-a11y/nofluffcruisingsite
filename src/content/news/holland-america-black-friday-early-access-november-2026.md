Holland America opened its Black Friday presale this morning. [The line's release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/holland-america-line-gives-travelers-early-access-to-exceptional-black-friday-savings-302935839.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Seattle, 11 Nov 2026, 6:02 a.m. ET.

The photo is the lead image on that release. The bow lettering reads Nieuw Amsterdam. The ship is alongside a pier, with mountains behind it. The file name is Whittier_Ship-Train_Port_20220904_0162.jpg. The caption on the wire is a sentence about the sale. It does not name the ship or the port. The photo is not a picture of a Black Friday rate.

## The two booking windows

Sign up on HollandAmerica.com. The release says you receive a code. That code is the early access for a presale that runs 11 Nov through 17 Nov.

The same offer opens to everyone from 18 Nov through 29 Nov. The public window adds 50% reduced deposits. The early-access paragraph does not say the presale includes that deposit cut. The release does not print the code, and it does not say whether the code arrives by email or on the screen.

## What the release prices

The sale covers more than 800 cruises departing December 2026 through spring 2029. The pieces named:

- Up to 30% off cruise fares. The release does not print a sample fare, so 30% is the ceiling, and the cruise you want can be under it.
- Free prepaid crew appreciation. The release says this can be more than $180 a person, depending on the stateroom category and the length of the cruise. It is for the first and second guests in the stateroom only.
- Free or reduced fares for a third and fourth guest in the same stateroom. The release does not say which guest is free, which guest is reduced, an age limit, or a dollar amount.

The release says taxes and fees are included in all cruise fares. That sentence is in this release. It does not show a fare table.

## Have It All on top

The Black Friday pieces can be combined with Have It All, the premium fare. The release says Have It All adds four things: shore excursions, specialty dining, Wi-Fi, and a beverage package. It lists those on top of the fare discount, the free crew appreciation, reduced deposits, and the third and fourth guest offer. It does not print a dollar value for the shore excursion credit, the dining, the Wi-Fi, or the drinks.

The reduced-deposit line in that Have It All sentence is the release's. The only place the release puts a figure on the deposit is the public window: 50% reduced deposits from 18 Nov through 29 Nov.

## Where it applies

Up to 30% off is listed for Alaska cruises and Cruisetours, Asia, Australia and New Zealand, the Caribbean, Canada and New England, Hawaii, the Mediterranean, Mexico, northern Europe, the Pacific Coast, the Panama Canal, South America and Antarctica, and Tahiti. The release folds in what it calls the newly released Year-Round Europe catalog. It does not describe that catalog.

Select Legendary Voyages, which the release defines as cruises of 25 to 59 days, are included. Back-to-back Collectors Voyages are included. The release does not list the sailings.

Dan Rough, vice president of revenue management, is the executive in the quote. He points the offer at Alaska, including Cruisetours that pair a voyage with Denali National Park, and he says Denali Lodge enhancements are set to debut next summer. The quote does not print a room count or a fare. The October post, [Holland America's 80th Alaska season](/news/holland-america-2027-alaska-80th-season-denali-lodge/), is the one with the six ships and the 72 added Denali rooms. This release does not repeat those figures.

## What the release leaves out

Grand Voyages are excluded. 2028 Alaska Cruisetours are excluded. Read that second line against the Alaska pitch. The quote invites an Alaska Cruisetour. The exclusion line takes 2028 Alaska Cruisetours out of this sale. The release does not say which earlier Alaska Cruisetour years stay in.

## If you are pricing one this week

The presale code is the 11 Nov through 17 Nov path. The 50% reduced deposit, as the release dates it, starts when the sale opens to the public on 18 Nov and runs through 29 Nov. Match the cruise against the two exclusions before you treat 30% or the free crew appreciation as yours. The phone number in the release is 1-877-724-5425.
