Victory Cruise Lines has four 2027 cruises with Michelle Bernstein aboard for the opening days, if you use the line's own page. [That page](https://victorycruiselines.com/chef-michelle-bernstein), read 16 Nov 2026, prints "Chef sails first few days" under each date. The trade pieces do not all print that limit.

The photo is the lead image on [Seatrade's 14 Nov article](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/people-opinions/four-victory-cruises-to-feature-chef-michelle-bernstein). The credit is Victory Cruise Lines. The caption puts Bernstein at center, John Waggoner and Claudette Waggoner to the viewer's left, and David Martinez and David William Kelly to the right. The backdrop reads Victory Cruise Lines and chef michy. The file name is Victory-Cruise-Lines-Michelle-Bernstein.jpg.

## The four dates on the line's page

Each block includes one hotel night in the night count the page prints. None of the blocks prints a fare. The phone on the page is (855) 556-7193.

- Victory I, Jacksonville roundtrip, 30 Mar to 10 Apr 2027. The page calls it an 11-night cruise, one hotel night and 10 cruise nights, with 7 shore excursions included. The port list is Jacksonville, Savannah, Charleston, Port Royal (Beaufort), West Palm Beach, Freeport, Norfolk / Newport News, Jacksonville (Fernandina Beach), and Jacksonville, plus scenic days.
- Victory II, Chicago to Toronto, 12 May to 22 May 2027. A 10-night cruise, one hotel night and 9 cruise nights, 7 shore excursions included. Ports: Chicago, Milwaukee, Escanaba, Mackinac Island, Sault Ste. Marie, Detroit, Cleveland, Niagara Falls (Port Colborne), and Toronto, plus a scenic day on Lake Huron.
- Victory II, Quebec City roundtrip, 28 Jul to 9 Aug 2027. A 12-night cruise, one hotel night and 11 cruise nights, 7 shore excursions included. Ports include Montreal twice, Kingston, Clayton, Trois-Rivieres, Baie-Comeau, and Saguenay.
- Victory I, Chicago to Montreal, 9 Sep to 21 Sep 2027. A 12-night cruise, one hotel night and 11 cruise nights, 10 shore excursions included. The port list runs through the Great Lakes calls above and ends in Montreal, with Toronto still on the route.

The short blurbs above the "first few days" line name a shorter stretch: Jacksonville to Charleston, Chicago to Mackinac Island, Quebec City to Kingston, and Chicago to Mackinac Island again. The page does not say those place names are the days she leaves the ship.

## Where the trade pieces stop

[Seatrade on 14 Nov](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/people-opinions/four-victory-cruises-to-feature-chef-michelle-bernstein) says she joins the first part of the Jacksonville cruise and part of the 28 Jul to 9 Aug Quebec cruise. It says she will be aboard the 12-22 May Chicago to Toronto voyage, and it names the 9-21 Sep Chicago to Montreal voyage. Those two sentences do not say "first part."

[Travel Weekly the same day](https://www.travelweekly.com/Cruise-Travel/victory-cruise-lines-sailings-to-feature-chef-partner-michelle-bernstein) lists the four ranges and does not print "first few days." It prints a 28 Jul start for the Quebec cruise and does not print 9 Aug. It says signature dishes are served every night in the Coastal Dining Room, and it dates the chef-partner appointment to July. The line page says the dishes are on every voyage and does not name that room.

Seatrade quotes David William Kelly. Travel Weekly quotes John Waggoner. This post does not merge the two quotes into one.

If you already hold one of the four dates, the line page is the one that prints "first few days" on every cruise. The May and September limits are not written the same way at Seatrade. No fare for the hosted sailings is in these sources.
