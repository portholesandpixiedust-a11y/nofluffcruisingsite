MSC Poesia did not leave PortMiami on Sunday 1 Nov 2026. [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/coast-guard-detains-msc-poesia-cruise-ship-miami/), Cristian Benavides, says the U.S. Coast Guard is holding the ship at the pier with passengers aboard, and that the itinerary is on hold.

The page was published at 9:44 p.m. EST on 2 Nov. Its last-modified time is 3 Nov 2026 at 12:12 GMT, which is 7:12 a.m. EST. Read on 3 Nov, the article still says the ship is detained.

The photo is the one on that CBS page. The caption places MSC Poesia in Warnemünde, on the Baltic, and credits Stefan Sauer, picture alliance, via Getty Images. The caption does not date the frame. It does not say the photo shows this Miami hold.

## What the Coast Guard told CBS

The Coast Guard's statement to CBS is dated Monday. It says Miami sector detained the ship for inspection on Sunday.

The teams "are verifying the cruise ship's compliance with international standards and U.S. regulations." The statement does not name a deficiency.

It also says the Coast Guard's job is safe and secure commerce, and that the Captain of the Port's priority is the safety of passengers, crew, the vessel, and the environment. No official's name is printed.

## What MSC told CBS

MSC's statement to CBS says the departure "has been delayed while the U.S. Coast Guard conducts an inspection aboard the ship while it is docked in port."

The company says it is cooperating. It says it cannot give further information while the inspection continues. It says it is keeping you informed about an anticipated departure, and it asks for patience.

That statement does not print a clock time. It does not print a cancelled port. It does not print a refund, a credit, or a compensation figure.

CBS says MSC describes a capacity of more than 3,200 passengers and over 1,000 crew. CBS says the number of people on this sailing is unclear. Use the capacity line as the size of the ship. Do not use it as a headcount for Sunday.
