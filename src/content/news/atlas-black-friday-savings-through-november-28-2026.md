Atlas has a Black Friday window that runs through 28 Nov. [The terms](https://atlasoceanvoyages.com/terms-of-use), read 26 Nov 2026, cover new individual bookings made from 24 Nov through 28 Nov. [The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/atlas-ocean-voyages-announces-best-offer-of-the-year-with-black-friday-sale-302948518.html) is datelined Fort Lauderdale, 23 Nov 2026, at 13:13 ET. It says the sale begins on Tuesday 24 Nov and concludes on Saturday 28 Nov. Neither page prints a clock.

The release's share image is a logo. This post does not use it.

## How the percent is built

The terms add 25 percent off the published cruise fare. That 25 percent is applied after Bonus Savings of up to 40 percent on qualifying voyages. The total is up to 55 percent. The amount varies by departure and by suite.

Fares are per guest, in U.S. dollars, based on double occupancy. They include government taxes and fees. The terms print that range as $385 to $990 a guest, and they say those taxes and fees are not eligible for a further discount. Reduced deposits do not apply. The offer is capacity-controlled.

A booking that cancels and rebooks does not qualify. Group bookings, charters, and transfers do not qualify. Expedition experiences, excursions, insurance, air travel, and pre- or post-expedition tours do not qualify.

The offer combines with Explorer's Choice only. It does not combine with group amenities, Onboard Savings, Back-to-Back Savings, Atlas Yacht Club Loyalty Savings, or Military Edge Savings.

## Air credit is a fare cut

The terms include up to $2,000 in Air Credit Savings per suite on qualifying voyages, if you select the Air Credit amenity through Explorer's Choice. The credit comes off the cruise fare after the bonus savings and after the extra 25 percent. You arrange your own flights. The release prints the same $2,000 ceiling and does not say the credit is a reduction to the fare.

The release says the sale is on nearly every voyage, and it names select journeys to Antarctica, the Mediterranean, Northern Europe, and the Caribbean. The terms leave these out: Atlas Adventurer voyages, Antarctica voyages that depart in the fourth quarter of 2028 and the first quarter of 2029, Grand Voyages, and these codes: WNA270610, WTR270619, WVO270623, WTR270727, WVO270806, WTR270923, WNA271119, WTR280203, WNA280407, WNA280414, WVO280420, WVO280428, and WTR280531. An excluded voyage can still use Explorer's Choice.

The release names World Navigator, World Traveller, and World Voyager, with fewer than 200 guests. It says those ships include premium beverages. It says Atlas Adventurer, a sailing yacht, is due in 2028. The terms keep Adventurer voyages out of this sale.

## The priority hold has closed

The terms say a priority reservation made from 11 Nov through 23 Nov, through a travel advisor or by calling 844.442.8527, is confirmed starting 24 Nov. Atlas Yacht Club members are confirmed first. A priority reservation does not add the extra savings, and it does not guarantee a suite. That hold window has closed. The release prints the phone as 1-844-44-ATLAS.

## If you are booking this week

The terms page closes the extra 25 percent with a booking on 28 Nov. It does not print a clock. Read the voyage code against the exclusion list before you use the 55 percent line. The air credit is money off the fare, and you still buy the flight. The release prints no sample fare, and the terms page does not either.
