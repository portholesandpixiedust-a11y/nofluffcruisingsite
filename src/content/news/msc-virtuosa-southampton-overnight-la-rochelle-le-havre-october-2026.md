MSC Virtuosa did not sail away on Saturday night. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/msc-virtuosa-held-in-southampton-as-storm-and-strikes-upend-itinerary/221774), Melissa Mayntz, 25 Oct 2026, and [Cruise Mummy](https://www.cruisemummy.co.uk/storms-and-strikes-cause-more-southampton-cruise-disruption/), 25 Oct, both print a letter that kept the ship in Southampton overnight and cut both French ports. Vigo is the added call. The cruise is still 7 nights.

No MSC newsroom post for the letter turned up in this check.

The photo on this page is the lead photo on the Cruise Hive story, MSC Virtuosa in Southampton, credited to P.Cartwright. It is not the departure photo on the [Thursday arrival post](/news/storm-austen-southampton-arvia-virtuosa-preziosa-october-2026/). The caption does not date it to this cruise.

## What the letter says

Both articles say guests boarded on Saturday 24 Oct and got the letter in the stateroom. The planned departure was 8:00 p.m. Saturday. The new departure is 7:00 p.m. Sunday 25 Oct.

Cruise Hive quotes the decision. The line says it adjusted the itinerary for your safety and comfort after assessing Storm Austen, which was expected to bring challenging weather across the English Channel and the Bay of Biscay, and strike action affecting parts of France. Cruise Mummy reports the same two reasons and does not print that full sentence.

The cruise stays 7 nights because you already boarded on Saturday and the return day stays Saturday 31 Oct. You lose two port days. You do not lose a night off the booking in the way [Ventura's cruise](/news/ventura-southampton-delay-cherbourg-cancelled-october-2026/) does.

| Wed 28 Oct | A Coruña, as scheduled. No hours printed. |

Cruise Hive prints A Coruna without the accent. Cruise Mummy prints A Coruña. This post uses the accent and notes the other spelling.

Cruise Hive says the strikes are expected to close the port. Cruise Mummy says planned strike action is expected to prevent the port from operating normally. Neither quote names a union or a cargo. Do not add a reason that is not in the letter.

Cruise Hive calls the ship a 4,842-guest ship. Cruise Mummy does not print that count. It is not in the port feed.

## Tours, and the deck warning

Both articles say a shore excursion bought through MSC for La Rochelle or Le Havre is cancelled and refunded to the onboard account. Vigo tours are in the MSC for Me app. A tour you booked yourself is not in that sentence. Call that operator.

The letter quotes do not print a fare refund or a flat onboard credit for the two lost ports.

Cruise Hive quotes a second part of the letter. The ship is designed to operate safely in these conditions, and some movement may still be experienced. For your safety, avoid outer decks and balconies, use handrails, and note that outdoor activities and pools may be temporarily unavailable. Cruise Mummy quotes the movement sentence and paraphrases the rest. Use the Cruise Hive wording for the balcony line.

The Sunday departure is not on the feed read 26 Oct 2026. The row that is there is the return, and it has no clock.

Those timestamps are more than a year old. This row is not a new instruction from Sunday's letter. The [schedule page](https://www.southamptonvts.co.uk/live_information/shipping_movements_and_cruise_ship_schedule/cruise_ship_schedule/) says the list is provisional. Confirm the terminal with MSC before you leave for the port.

The earlier post on this site recorded the cruise that ended Thursday. [Arvia, Virtuosa, and Preziosa reach Southampton on Thursday night](/news/storm-austen-southampton-arvia-virtuosa-preziosa-october-2026/) says the feed read 22 Oct listed this ship leaving at 8:00 p.m. Saturday from Horizon, and that no itinerary change was confirmed yet for the next cruise. The letter in today's reports is that change. This note does not rewrite the Thursday arrival clocks.

## What this means if you are on MSC Virtuosa

Monday is a sea day if the letter was followed. Do not plan on La Rochelle. Friday is a sea day. Do not plan on Le Havre. Thursday is Vigo, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Bilbao and A Coruña are still on the letter, without new hours.

Look for the French tours on the onboard account. Book Vigo in the app if you want a tour through MSC. The letter does not print a fare refund.

If the ship is moving, the Cruise Hive quote says to stay off outer decks and balconies and to expect a pool to close. That is the letter, not a forecast pasted in here.

If you are meeting the ship on Saturday, the port still says TBA at Horizon. Use MSC's message for the time.
