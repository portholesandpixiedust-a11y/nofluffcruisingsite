## What changes on the menu

The Test Kitchen, the tasting-menu restaurant from chef Matt Lambert, adds an a la carte option on every ship. You can order single dishes from a menu that changes. The release says those dishes are included in the fare. The five-course tastings stay, and more rotations join in 2027. Seatrade prints the same split. Neither page prints a cover charge.

The Galley refresh is dated 2027. Bento Baby adds Japanese street food, including chicken katsu boxes. Grounds Club Too adds fresh juices, electrolytes, gut-focused mixes, and more smoothies. The release also announces new dishes at Extra Virgin and Gunbae. It does not print a start date for those two. Seatrade says new dishes are planned there. This post does not turn that into a month.

The Ahoy page says The Beach Club at Bimini has a relaunched menu from chef Rashida Holmes, starting with her mother Joy's curry chicken roti. It says that menu is live on Resilient Lady and Scarlet Lady sailings, with Brilliant Lady and Valiant Lady next. The release does not print this menu. If you are on Brilliant Lady or Valiant Lady, the recap still has you waiting.

## Four new shows

The release gives each of these a ship:

- Red Hot, on Valiant Lady. It celebrates the brand, the music, and Richard Branson.
- Curtain Call, a prequel to Murder in the Manor, in Valiant Lady's Red Room later in 2027. The Ahoy page sets it in a neon, big-hair era and says it follows Booked, an earlier Red Room prequel.
- The Tricksters: Inside Penn & Teller's Vault, on Brilliant Lady.
- Sip, Snacks & Sass with the Diva, on Scarlet Lady, on select longer voyages, with bottomless bubbles. Seatrade says "longer voyages" and does not print "select."

The release also says Happenings Cast members can be booked for private parties in the US in 2027. The Ahoy page says Abi becomes The Siren, hosting VIP rooms, and that The Conjurer and The Ritualist join in 2027. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/virgin-voyages-details-resilient-lady-glow-up-loyalty-only-london-club/) says there are new shows and does not name them.

## Barcelona, Athens, Alaska, and the Baltics

These are the new trips a guest can look up. No fare is printed on any of them.

Resilient Lady sails an 11-night eclipse cruise, round trip from Barcelona, 28 Jul to 8 Aug 2027, for the total eclipse on 2 Aug. The release says more than four minutes of totality, and calls at Palma de Mallorca, Malaga, Cadiz for Seville, Funchal, Tangier, and Valencia. The Ahoy page says this voyage is already on sale. The release does not print "on sale" for this one.

Scarlet Lady's summer 2027 season from Athens adds a first call at Sarande, Albania. The Ahoy page says those Athens cruises run seven to 11 nights. Her 21 Jul 2027 departure adds an overnight in Mykonos. That overnight is in the release.

In October 2027, Scarlet Lady does a 12-night crossing from Barcelona to San Juan and makes the line's first call at Lanzarote. The Ahoy page puts it at the end of October and calls it the first westbound crossing from the Mediterranean to the Caribbean.

Her 6 Sep 2028 departure adds an overnight in Alexandria, for Cairo. The release also says new calls in Italy and Slovenia. It does not name those cities.

The Ahoy page, and not the release, adds three more deployments. Scarlet Lady's winter 2027-28 season is six to eight nights in the Southern Caribbean from San Juan, calling Aruba, with a late night just before Christmas, plus Antigua, St. Lucia, St. Maarten, and Barbados. Brilliant Lady's Alaska season in summer 2028 is longer, May to September, from Seattle and Vancouver, and the recap says it is on sale now. It does not print the ports or the number of nights. Valiant Lady has five new Scandinavia and Baltic itineraries in summer 2028, also on sale now. The recap does not name the ports.

## Sailings with a theme

Sisters at Sea, the four-ship meetup in February, counts as two voyages toward Sailing Club status. The release and the Ahoy page both say that. They do not reprint the day. The date already on this site is in [the February 2027 meetup post](/news/virgin-sisters-at-sea-four-ships-february-2027/).

The release repeats a Sailing Club end date of 31 Dec 2028. That date is already in [the 24 Sep status post](/news/virgin-voyages-sailing-club-status-match-2028/). It is not a new end date.

The Ahoy page says True Crime, the podcast voyage, returns on 4 Mar 2027. It does not name the ship.

The Red Hot Sailing Club is two sailings on the Ahoy page. Scarlet Lady leaves Barcelona on 4 Jul 2027. Valiant Lady sails the Caribbean on 8 Nov 2027. The release does not print these two dates.

Valiant Lady's New Year's Eve cruise is four nights, 30 Dec 2027 to 3 Jan 2028, and the release says it is already on sale. Stops are Key West and The Beach Club at Bimini. After the first countdown the ship sails west, crosses a time zone, and the clocks go back one hour for a second midnight. The release does not name the homeport. Seatrade and [Travel Weekly UK](https://travelweekly.co.uk/news/virgin-voyages-to-make-hawaii-debut-in-2028) both say the cruise leaves Miami.

Creator Voyage is 19-22 Apr 2027, round trip from Miami to The Beach Club at Bimini. Applications open 20 Nov 2026. That open date is in the release and on the Ahoy page. Seatrade prints the April dates and Miami to Bimini, and it does not print 20 Nov. The release and the Ahoy page both cite 250 million TikTok views from the earlier voyage. The release calls that earlier sailing Boatchella.

## Smaller changes you will meet on board

After Resilient Lady's September 2027 dry dock, Redemption Spa takes the name The Well. The release says the rooms stay the same and the treatments and protocols change. Seatrade says the physical space does not change.

Shake for Shake lets you shake the phone and order a high-protein, low-calorie smoothie, brought to you. It starts on Resilient Lady, then the other ships. Champagne shakes stay for a toast. The smoothie is the new order.

In 2027 the line adds more of the included fitness classes, and two more Happenings Cast members to point you at them.

The Red Case is a minibar of small self-care items. The release names eye patches, electrolytes, magnesium, and lotions. You can buy pieces or take the whole case home. The release says more detail is expected in early 2027. It is not a price list yet.

Gift cards start in early December, first on Virgin's own consumer and business gifting sites, with a plan to add other retailers and stores later. The release does not print a day in December or a card value.

From 2028 you can marry at The Beach Club at Bimini, with up to 100 guests. The release says nearly 100 couples have already married with the line, on the ships and, for the Mediterranean, ashore. The extras it lists are a longer cocktail hour, a private cabana, a thermal spa pass, a bridge tour, flowers, live music, and rose petals in the cabin. It does not print a wedding price.

From 2027, a booking includes 60 days of Canva Pro at no cost, and a way to make custom merchandise from Virgin designs. That is the guest half of the Canva note in the release.
