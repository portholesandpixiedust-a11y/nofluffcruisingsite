Haptic vests are now at the silent disco on every Disney ship. Braille and tactile guidebooks are on request, including on the two Bahamas islands. [Disney Experiences](https://disneyexperiences.com/cruise-line-accessibility-enhancements/) published that on 27 Oct 2026. [WDW News Today](https://wdwnt.com/2026/10/disney-cruise-line-introduces-haptic-vests-for-silent-discos-tactile-guidebooks-fleetwide-to-improve-accessibility/), Shannen Ace, reported the same two additions the same day.

The photo on this page is the lead image on the Disney Experiences post. The page does not print a caption or a photographer. This post does not treat that frame as a picture of a vest.

## The vests

Disney says the vests are for you if you are deaf or hard of hearing. They turn music and sound effects into touch, so you can feel the beat and the rhythm. They are at the silent disco on all Disney Cruise Line ships.

The post does not print a fee. It does not say how many vests are on a ship. It does not tell you to reserve one. WDW News Today does not add a fee or a count.

## The guidebooks

The guidebooks are braille and tactile. Disney says they are available fleetwide on request. Ask at Guest Services. You can also ask at Disney Castaway Cay and at Disney Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point.

The books include tactile maps and venue information. The point, in Maura King's words, is that you can understand the layout of a ship or an island if you are blind or have low vision. King is manager of accessibility at Disney Cruise Line. WDW News Today prints the same request points and does not add a deadline.

## What the post lists as already there

The vests and the guidebooks are the new lines. The same Disney post also names services it already offers.

| Item | What Disney Experiences prints |
|---|---|
| Staterooms | Accessible staterooms. |
| Theater screens | Captioning on Funnel Vision. |
| AquaMouse | An accessible transfer option on Wish class ships. |
| Sound | Assistive listening systems. |
| Movement | Wheelchair-accessible public spaces. |
| Service dogs | Relief locations. |
| Before you sail | Special Services, a shoreside team. Send a request form, or contact the team. |
| The islands | Sand wheelchairs, beach mobility mats, accessible transportation, and some excursions for limited mobility. The post says these may be offered. |

WDW News Today repeats that list in shorter form. It also says Disneyland Paris added vibrating vests last year. That sentence is not in the Disney post. This page does not use it.

The Disney post does not print the address of the Special Services form. Use the contact path on your booking if you need the form.

## What this means if you sail Disney

The vest is at the silent disco. The 27 Oct post does not tell you to reserve one before you sail. If you want the guidebook, ask at Guest Services, or on Castaway Cay or Lookout Cay. If you need a room, a transfer, or a tour set up before you sail, use Special Services. The post tells you to send the form before the cruise.
