Star Clippers has a Black Friday and Travel Tuesday credit that closes on 10 Dec. [The U.S. offer page](https://www.starclippers.com/us-dom/landing/black-friday-2026/), read 27 Nov 2026, is the booking page. The offer text sits on the line's own embed. The page does not print a clock.

The photos on that page do not name a photographer. This post does not use them.

## What you get

The offer covers 48 sailings on Royal Clipper, Star Clipper, and Star Flyer, from December 2026 through December 2027. The page splits them as 25 Caribbean and Central America sailings and 23 Mediterranean sailings.

The first two guests in cabin categories 2 through 6 get prepaid gratuities and an air credit for the cabin. On the U.S. page the credit is $300 on a cruise of 5 or 6 nights, $400 on 7 to 9 nights, and $500 on 10 nights or more. You book with code BLACK. The phone on the page is 1-800-442-0551.

The terms say the credit applies to air arranged through Star Clippers, and they tell you to contact the line for the details. They do not say the credit comes off the cruise fare. A third guest does not get the offer. Group bookings are out. It does not combine with another offer or discount. It is not retroactive. The line can change or withdraw it. The U.S. page says it is for U.S. and Latin American residents only.

## Two fares, and four sailings that say call

The from-prices are category 6 advance-purchase fares, per person, double occupancy, cruise only, with port charges included. The page says they were listed 24 Nov 2026 and may change.

Star Clipper's five-night Corsica and Italy cruise on 31 May 2027 is from $1,790, plus a $300 air credit. Star Clipper's seven-night Antigua roundtrip on 13 Mar 2027 is from $2,010, plus a $400 air credit.

Four December 2026 rows say there is limited time to arrange air, and they say to call to book. They are Star Flyer on 12 Dec 2026, Treasure Islands, 7 nights, from $2,010; Star Clipper on 19 Dec, Panama to Costa Rica, 7 nights, from $2,010; Star Flyer on 19 Dec, Leeward Islands, 7 nights, from $2,010; and Star Clipper on 26 Dec, Costa Rica and Panama, 7 nights, from $2,210. Those are the same category 6 from-prices.

## The Canadian page, and a different line in the trade story

[The Canadian page](https://www.starclippers.com/ca/landing/black-friday-2026/), read the same day, uses the same ships, the same 48 sailings, the same code, and the same 10 Dec close. The amounts are in Canadian dollars: $400, $500, and $600 a cabin on those length bands. The two sample fares are from $2,200 CAD and $2,480 CAD. That page says it is for Canadian residents only.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/star-clippers-launches-black-friday-offer-with-air-credits/) on 27 Nov matches the ships, the 48 sailings, the 10 Dec date, the USD bands, and the two U.S. sample fares. It also says the credit can be used as a discount against the cruise fare, and that the offer is only for North American residents. The line's terms, read 27 Nov, do not print the fare-discount line. They split residency: U.S. and Latin American residents on the U.S. page, Canadian residents on the Canadian page. Use the line's pages for who can book and for what the credit is.
