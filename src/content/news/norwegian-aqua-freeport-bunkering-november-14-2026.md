Norwegian Aqua is cutting time in Tortola and at Great Stirrup Cay so the ship can take fuel in Freeport on Saturday night. Guests stay on the ship for that stop. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/norwegian-adjusts-aqua-itinerary-for-bunkering-in-freeport/) on 13 Nov 2026 and [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/norwegian-aqua-shortens-two-port-calls-to-make-room-for-freeport-fuel-stop/223450), Melissa Mayntz, on 12 Nov both quote a statement Norwegian sent to people on board. No Norwegian newsroom post turned up for this change.

The photo is the lead image on the Cruise Hive story. The caption says Norwegian Aqua in the Bahamas. The credit is Pedro H C Carvalho. The bow lettering reads Norwegian Aqua, and the ship is alongside a pier. It is not a picture of the Freeport anchorage, and it is not dated to this cruise. Cruise Industry News uses a file named NCL-Aqua-Miami.jpg and does not name a photographer. That file is not the picture here.

## The cruise this applies to

This is the seven-night Eastern Caribbean that left PortMiami on Sunday 8 Nov 2026. Both reports say the itinerary also includes Puerto Plata and St. Thomas. Cruise Hive, written while the ship was in Tortola on 12 Nov, says those two calls were already done.

Friday 13 Nov stays a sea day. The calls still ahead on the revised plan are Great Stirrup Cay on Saturday and the Freeport anchorage that night.

## Tortola, already shortened

The original Tortola call was 6:45 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on Thursday 12 Nov. The ship now leaves half an hour earlier, at 1:30 p.m. Cruise Industry News states the half hour and the original window. Cruise Hive prints the new clock as 1:30 p.m. The arrival time does not move.

If you were in Tortola yesterday, that earlier departure is the one that already happened. This page is not a new skip of the island.

## Great Stirrup Cay on Saturday

Cruise Hive says the private-island call was planned from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on Saturday 14 Nov. The new window is 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. That is one hour earlier on arrival and two hours earlier on departure.

Cruise Industry News prints the same new clocks, 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., and the same two deltas. It calls the change a minor adjustment and says guests can still have a day on the island. It does not print the old 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. Those two old clocks stay with Cruise Hive. The arithmetic matches.

## Freeport is not a port day

After Great Stirrup Cay, the ship goes to Freeport. Both reports put the ship at anchor from 7:30 p.m. to 11:59 p.m. on Saturday 14 Nov. The purpose in the guest statement is bunkering, which is the fuel stop. The statement says the work will not affect the safety of the ship. It does not say what is wrong with the fuel plan, and it does not say this is a repair.

Guests cannot get off. Cruise Hive quotes the letter calling Freeport a technical call, not a port of call. Cruise Industry News says the same: technical only, no disembarkation while the ship is at anchor.

## The tour refund

Shore excursions booked through Norwegian for Tortola or Great Stirrup Cay will be adjusted to the new hours. A tour that cannot fit is cancelled. The refund is the full tour cost, credited to the onboard account. That is both reports.

A tour you bought from a company other than Norwegian is not in that sentence. Ask that operator.

The statement does not print a fare refund, an onboard credit for the lost hours, or a move to another sailing.

## What only Cruise Hive adds

Cruise Hive says the letter is signed by Captain Ashutosh Chandra and that the letter was posted on Reddit. Cruise Industry News does not print a name. This check did not open the Reddit thread. The captain's name is Cruise Hive's account of that letter.

Cruise Hive also says the return to Miami stays 7:00 a.m. on Sunday 15 Nov, and that Norwegian had not confirmed a change to the next cruise, which it describes as the same Eastern Caribbean itinerary. Cruise Industry News does not print the return clock or the next cruise. Use the boarding pass for the Miami arrival if your flight is tight. The 7:00 a.m. line is one report.

Cruise Hive says people commenting on the Reddit post described a similar stop on the 25 Oct 2026 sailing. Those are comments. Norwegian has not said the October cruise changed. This page does not treat that as a second cancellation.

## If you are on this ship

Today, Friday 13 Nov, is still a sea day. Tomorrow morning is Great Stirrup Cay from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., three hours shorter than the 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. plan Cruise Hive prints. Tomorrow night is an anchorage at Freeport, not a night ashore. If a Norwegian tour no longer fits, look for the credit on the onboard account.
