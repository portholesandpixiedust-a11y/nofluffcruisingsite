Marella and TUI River Cruises have put Black Friday codes on sale through 1 Dec. [TUI's newsroom post](https://www.tuigroup.com/en/newsroom/news/marella-cruises-and-tui-river-cruises-black-friday-savings-up-to-300-off-ocean-and-river-cruise-holidays), dated 20 Nov 2026, says bookings run from Friday 20 Nov until midnight on Tuesday 1 Dec.

The lead image on that page does not name a ship, a port, or a photographer. The frame shows two people on a stone terrace, a red gate, and a blue-hulled ship offshore. This post does not use it.

The ocean codes and the river code are not the same offer. [The terms page](https://www.tui.co.uk/destinations/info/cruise-deals-terms) the newsroom points to splits them. Read the block that matches the holiday.

## Marella, with no midnight on the terms

BFSAIL200 is £200 off selected Canary Islands and Mediterranean sailings. BFSAIL300 is £300 off selected Caribbean and transatlantic repositioning sailings. The newsroom dates those departures from 1 Jan 2027 to 30 April 2028. Chris Hackney, chief executive of Marella Cruises, is quoted on regional flying from UK airports and on departures running until April 2028.

The Marella block on the terms page, read 23 Nov, says the saving is per booking, before extras such as insurance and flight extras. New bookings only. The cruise must be at least seven nights. Cabin-only is excluded. There is no minimum spend. BFSAIL300 says repositioning cruises are included. Type the code in upper case on the holiday payment page. It cannot be used with another discount code. The offer may be withdrawn without notice.

That ocean block says bookings run until 1 Dec 2026. It does not print midnight, and it does not print Tuesday. The newsroom prints midnight for the shared window. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/tui-and-marella-highlight-black-friday-deals/) on 22 Nov also prints midnight on 1 Dec, and it does not print the seven-night rule or the cabin-only exclusion. Use the terms page for those two lines.

The newsroom says Marella's fleet is five ships: Marella Discovery, Marella Explorer, Marella Voyager, and the adult-only Marella Discovery 2 and Marella Explorer 2. It says tips, flights, food and drinks, accommodation, and service charges are included as standard. It does not say the £200 or £300 is a cut to that included fare rather than a cut before extras. The terms page is the one that says before extras.

## TUI River, which does print midnight

BFRIVER is the river code. The newsroom says £200 on a holiday costing £2,500 or more, or £300 on a holiday costing £3,500 or more, on a river cruise of six nights or longer. Departures in that post run from 1 Jan through 31 Dec 2027. It names the Rhine, the Danube, and the Nile. Katy Berzins, head of river cruises, is quoted on savings of up to £300 per booking and on a run until 1 Dec.

The river block on the terms page, read 23 Nov, uses a minimum spend of £2500 and £3500, before insurance and flight extras. A cruise of five nights or less is out. Cabin-only is included, and so are multi-centres. That is the opposite of the Marella block, which excludes cabin-only. New bookings only. The code works with TUI retail, the contact centre, and affiliated third-party agents. It cannot be combined with another code. Bookings run from Friday 20 Nov until midnight on Tuesday 1 Dec 2026.

## The free upgrades are not cash

The newsroom also lists a free all-inclusive upgrade on selected European river sailings from 1 July through 31 Aug 2027, and free cabin upgrades on selected Nile sailings from 1 Jan through 31 May 2027.

The terms page says each upgrade shows as £0, and that you have to select it while you book. The all-inclusive one means selecting the all-inclusive option. The Nile one means selecting the cabin option. Neither has a cash alternative. Both use the midnight window. Cruise Industry News prints the dates and does not print the £0 line or the no-cash line.

The newsroom's about section names four adult-only European river ships, TUI Isla, TUI Skyla, TUI Maya, and TUI Aria, plus TUI Al Horeya and TUI Bahareya in Egypt. It also names later ships, TUI Elara, TUI Luzia, TUI Zora, and TUI Velera. Those names are not attached to the discount codes. The codes do not print a sample fare.

## If you are booking

For Marella, the terms want a new booking of at least seven nights, and they leave out cabin-only. For a river cruise, the terms include cabin-only once the holiday meets £2,500 or £3,500 and the cruise is at least six nights. Do not put both codes on one booking. The terms say they do not combine. The ocean terms do not print a clock on 1 Dec. The river terms stop at midnight that Tuesday. The newsroom is the page that puts midnight on both.
