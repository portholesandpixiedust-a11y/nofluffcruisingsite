Arvia, MSC Virtuosa, and MSC Preziosa are on Associated British Ports' Southampton list for Thursday evening, 22 Oct 2026. Cruise Hive says those arrivals are early, ahead of Storm Austen.

The clocks below are from the port feed the [cruise schedule page](https://www.southamptonvts.co.uk/live_information/shipping_movements_and_cruise_ship_schedule/cruise_ship_schedule/) loads. That page says the list is provisional. Confirm the terminal and your boarding time with the cruise line before you leave for the port.

The feed was read on 22 Oct 2026. The schedule page maps the berths to terminals.

The same feed still lists Queen Mary 2 at 6:00 a.m. Friday 23 Oct, on berth 38/9, the QEII terminal. This post is not a change notice for that ship.

## What Cruise Hive says moved

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/storm-austen-forces-three-cruise-ships-into-southampton-early/221580), Melissa Mayntz, 22 Oct, says the Thursday arrivals replace a later plan. Arvia and MSC Preziosa had been due Friday morning. MSC Virtuosa had been due Saturday morning. The article points at the ABP schedule for the new clocks. Those clocks match the feed above.

The article is also the report of an MSC alert to guests booked on the next MSC Preziosa sailing. Be at City Cruise Terminal no later than 2:00 p.m. on Friday 23 Oct. Cruise Hive says the sailing had been due to leave at 8:00 p.m. and will now leave at 6:00 p.m. The port feed lists that 6:00 p.m. departure. It does not print the 2:00 p.m. terminal deadline or the old 8:00 p.m. time. No MSC newsroom post for the alert turned up in this check. No P&O post for Arvia turned up either.

Cruise Hive's cruise lengths are its own. Arvia is ending a 13-night Mediterranean sailing that left Southampton on Saturday 10 Oct. MSC Virtuosa is ending a 5-night Northern Europe sailing that left Southampton on Monday 19 Oct. MSC Preziosa is ending a Northern Europe cruise. The article lists Arvia at 5,282 guests and MSC Preziosa at 3,959 guests. Those counts are not in the port feed.

The photo on this page is the lead photo on that Cruise Hive story, MSC Virtuosa departing Southampton, credited to Martin Augustus. It is the article image. It is not a picture of Thursday night's arrival.

## Storm Austen, and where Southampton sits

The [Met Office media centre](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/about-us/news-and-media/media-centre/weather-and-climate-news/2026/amber-warning-issued-as-storm-austen-approaches), at 10:47 a.m. UK time on Wednesday 21 Oct, issued an amber wind warning for the whole of Cornwall, the Isles of Scilly, and parts of West Devon. Deputy Chief Forecaster Chris Bulmer said gusts could reach 60-70 mph, with heavy rain, and that the exact track still has some uncertainty. The release says a wider yellow wind warning runs from Thursday evening into Friday. It does not name Southampton.

The [warnings page](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/warnings-and-advice/accessible-uk-warnings), read 22 Oct, does list Southampton, inside a yellow wind warning for Friday 23 Oct that also covers Hampshire, Portsmouth, and the Isle of Wight. The amber detail on that page names Cornwall, Devon, the Isles of Scilly, and Plymouth, and it adds the chance of gusts around 80 mph on exposed headlands. The yellow detail says gusts of 50-60 mph are likely, with 70 mph or more possible on some coasts, and a spell of southerly gusts of 50-60 mph along the south coast on Thursday night. The page carries a banner that the office is having issues and the warnings may be out of date. An update stamp at 10:00 a.m. UK time on 22 Oct says a warning time was brought forward, and that a wider yellow was extended through Friday afternoon.

Cruise Hive's hour window, amber from 3:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and yellow from midnight to 2:00 p.m., is that article's. It was published before the 22 Oct update on the warnings page. Use the warnings page for the current window, and read the banner.

The warnings page says road, rail, air, and ferry services may run late or cancel. Build that into a pickup on Thursday night and a drive to the port on Friday.

It says no itinerary changes are confirmed yet for those cruises. The MSC Preziosa departure time above is the clock change that article does report. A port list can still move. ## What this means if you are at Southampton

If you are getting off, plan on Thursday evening. Arvia and MSC Virtuosa are listed at 7:00 p.m. MSC Preziosa is listed at 9:30 p.m. A Friday morning pickup is the old plan in the Cruise Hive report, not the plan on the port feed read today.

If you are boarding MSC Preziosa on Friday, the port lists a 6:00 p.m. departure from the City terminal, berth 101. Cruise Hive says the line wants you there by 2:00 p.m. Treat 2:00 p.m. as the instruction in that report until the email in your booking says something else.

If you are boarding Arvia or MSC Virtuosa, this check did not find a new terminal deadline. Arvia is listed to leave at 5:00 p.m. Friday from Ocean. MSC Virtuosa is listed to leave at 8:00 p.m. Saturday from Horizon. Those departure stamps are older than today's arrival stamps. Read the line's message anyway. The port says the times can move.
