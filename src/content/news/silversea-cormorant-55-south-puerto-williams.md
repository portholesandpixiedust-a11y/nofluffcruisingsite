If you booked a Silversea Antarctica fly-cruise for this season, the plan includes a stay at The Cormorant at 55 South in Puerto Williams, Chile. The grand opening Seatrade prints is 5 Nov 2026. Sailings before that date are described as a preview, not as a wait until November.

[Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/hotel-operations-entertainment/silversea-opens-antarctica-season-with-its-new-chile-hotel-gateway), Anne Kalosh, 20 Oct 2026, is the season report. A [Silversea release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-cormorant-at-55-south-silversea-unveils-all-public-spaces-of-highly-anticipated-southernmost-hotel-302698593.html) dated 26 Feb 2026 is the older description of the hotel. Where they differ, the date is on the sentence.

## What opened this season

Seatrade says the 2026/27 Antarctica season has started. The first departures it names are Silver Endeavour from King George Island and Silver Wind from Puerto Williams.

Through March, Silver Endeavour, Silver Wind, and Silver Cloud operate 40 voyages. Twenty-nine of those are Antarctica fly-cruises, and each one includes a stay at The Cormorant. Seatrade also counts 11 voyages that cross the Drake Passage by sea in both directions and sail from Puerto Williams. 29 and 11 add up to the 40. The article does not call those 11 fly-cruises, and it does not say they include the hotel.

Charter flights, expedition experiences, and Zodiac outings are included on every trip, in that article. It does not print a fare.

The 14 double flights run from the Antarctic Sound to the Peninsula and the South Shetland Islands. The 15 one-way sailings are southbound on some departures and northbound on others. Seatrade does not print which date is which direction.

## Voyages Seatrade names

These dates are the highlights in the 20 Oct article. Nov. 7, Nov. 9, Dec. 9, and Dec. 22 do not have a year on the page. The March departure does.

- Silver Wind, 18 nights, roundtrip from Puerto Williams on Nov. 7. West Point Island, Saunders Island, and Port Stanley in the Falklands, then South Georgia, past Elephant Island, to the Antarctic Peninsula.
- Silver Cloud, four 15-night voyages combining South Georgia, Elephant Island, and the Peninsula. The first departs Nov. 9.
- Silver Cloud, 12 nights, roundtrip from Puerto Williams on Dec. 9, a day south of the Antarctic Circle.
- Silver Wind, 15 nights, from Puerto Williams on Dec. 22, to the Antarctic Peninsula and South Georgia.
- Silver Endeavour, 21 nights, departing 4 March 2027 from Puerto Williams and ending in Buenos Aires. The Peninsula and South Georgia, then Carcass Island, West Point Island, and Port Stanley, and Puerto Madryn, Argentina.

## November grand opening, October welcome

Seatrade says the hotel's grand opening is 5 Nov, and travelers on earlier voyages get a preview. The same article says the season's first ships have already departed. It does not say the rooms are closed until the ceremony. Preview is the word it uses for those earlier voyages.

The February release said the hotel was set to welcome guests in October 2026. It did not print 5 Nov. Seatrade is the later story, and it is the one that uses the words grand opening.

Silversea calls the property the southernmost hotel on Earth. That is the line's wording in the February release. Seatrade says it is billed that way, and it quotes Hernandez using the phrase. This post does not check that claim against any other hotel.

## How many nights, and what the February release still adds

The February release describes the fly-cruise pattern as a hotel stay in Santiago, a direct charter to Puerto Williams, one night at The Cormorant, then the flight on to King George Island for embarkation. On the way home, another overnight at the hotel, then the flight back to Santiago. It also says the hotel has 150 rooms, with forest or Beagle Channel views, and that it operates from October through March.

Seatrade's 20 Oct article says every fly-cruise includes a stay. It does not print 150 rooms, one night, or the Santiago hotel. Use one night each way, and 150 rooms, as the February release. Use "a stay" as the season report. No newer Silversea release read for this post retires either sentence.

Seatrade does add the charter details for this season: Silversea-branded flights from Santiago, extra-roomy seating, a three-course meal, and Silversea service. It says that private charter is the only direct flight from Santiago to Puerto Williams. Expedition staff who guide the Antarctic voyages rotate through the hotel and lead kayaking in the Beagle Channel and hiking on Navarino Island. Horseback rides are led by a Patagonian baqueano guide. The February release names nature walks, hiking, kayaking, and wildlife watching, and it names the Fireplace Lounge, Latitude 55, Bar Sur, and the Uncharted boutique. The picture on this page is Silversea's rendering of the Fireplace Lounge, published with the Seatrade story. It is not a photograph of the opened hotel.

## What this means for you

If your booking is one of the 29 fly-cruises, plan on the Puerto Williams hotel as part of the trip, not as an extra room you book yourself from this report. If you sail before 5 Nov, Seatrade calls that a preview of a hotel whose grand opening is still ahead. If you needed the night count, the only counted nights in hand are the February release: one night before King George Island, and one night on the return. The 11 voyages that cross the Drake both ways are a different product. Seatrade does not say those include the hotel. More Silversea reporting is on the [Silversea news hub](/news/silversea/).
