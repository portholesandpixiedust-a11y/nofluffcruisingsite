Viking has taken delivery of Viking Libra. [The release](https://www.vikingcruises.com/press/press-releases/255/viking-takes-delivery-of-newest-ocean-ship.html) is datelined Los Angeles, 25 Nov 2026. The ceremony was at Fincantieri's yard in Ancona, Italy. The page does not print a clock.

The image on this page is the lead file on [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/shipbuilding-equipment/fincantieri-delivers-hybrid-hydrogen-powered-viking-libra), Anne Kalosh, 26 Nov. The caption credits the photo to Viking. The file is Viking-Libra.jpg. The frame is a white ship underway, with a dark stripe along the hull. The bow name is not readable.

## What the release prints

Viking Libra is about 54,300 gross tons. It has 499 staterooms that can host 998 guests. The release calls every Viking ocean ship a small ship, and it calls Libra a sister ship with a different power system. That system will use liquefied hydrogen and fuel cells.

The first season is the Mediterranean and Northern Europe. The release prints no departure date, no port list, and no fare.

Viking Astrea is still under construction. It will have the same hybrid system. The release says Viking expects both ships to be operating on hydrogen in 2027.

Leah Talactac, president and chief executive officer, calls the delivery a proud moment and an important milestone. She says the two ships will set a new standard as that technology comes into operation in 2027.

The orderbook in the release is 25 more river ships by 2030, 10 more ocean ships by 2032, and two more expedition ships by 2031. Those deliveries would leave Viking with 123 river ships in 2030 and 28 ocean and expedition ships in 2032.

The about block prints the phone 1-800-2-VIKING, which it writes as 1-800-284-5464. A contact block on the same page prints 1-800-304-9616.

## What the other two pages add

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/viking-takes-delivery-of-the-hydrogen-ready-libra/) on 25 Nov matches the delivery, the 998 guests, the season, and the 2027 hydrogen line. It does not print the tonnage.

Seatrade on 26 Nov prints 998 passengers and 54,300gt. It also says Isotta Fraschini Motori fuel cells can produce up to six megawatts, and its opening line says the ship will be able to run emissions-free for hours-long stretches in sensitive areas. Those two lines are not in the 25 Nov release. This post does not treat them as part of the delivery notice.

## If you are pricing a Libra cruise

The release says the ship is delivered and names the first season. It does not name the first departure. The phone in the release text is 1-800-284-5464. No fare is printed.
