MSC has taken delivery of MSC World Asia. [The release](https://www.mscpressarea.com/press-releases/msc-cruises-and-chantiers-de-latlantique-celebrate-delivery-of-msc-world-asia/) is datelined Geneva, 25 Nov 2026. The ceremony was in Saint-Nazaire, at Chantiers de l'Atlantique. The page does not print a clock.

The image on this page is the featured file on [The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/cruise/msc-world-asia-ship-delivery), Gene Sloan, 25 Nov 2026. The caption reads "Featured image by Bernard Biger." The file name is Credit-Chantiers-de-lAtlantique-and-Bernard-Biger.jpg, so the file name also names Chantiers de l'Atlantique. The frame is a white ship underway. The hull name reads MSC WORLD ASIA. The funnel carries the MSC mark.

## Naming is Saturday, and guests board on 4 Dec

The release says the ship sails to Le Havre after delivery. The official naming there is 28 Nov 2026. [The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/cruise/msc-world-asia-ship-delivery) and [Cruise Critic](https://www.cruisecritic.com/news/msc-world-asia-joins-msc-cruises-fleet), Adam Coulter, both on 25 Nov, call that naming Saturday. The Points Guy says a champagne bottle was broken at the Saint-Nazaire handover, with Alexa Aponte Vago as godmother for that moment, and that Wednesday's event was not the official naming.

Guest cruises start 4 Dec 2026. The release says they are seven-night Mediterranean itineraries through summer 2027. The Points Guy says the first cruise with paying passengers leaves Barcelona, and that the seven-night voyages include stops in France, Italy, Malta, and Spain. Cruise Critic's 25 Nov story also starts the ship from Barcelona on 4 Dec. It does not print a port list. This post does not add Marseille, Genoa, Civitavecchia, Messina, or Valletta, because those names are not on the 25 Nov release, the Points Guy story, or that Cruise Critic story.

Cruise Critic says MSC has no plans to put the ship in Asia. The name is not a deployment.

The [sea-trials post](/news/msc-world-asia-sea-trials-december-debut/) already carried the September calendar for trials, naming, and the 4 Dec start. This post is the delivery.

## What the release says is on board

The release calls this the line's 24th ship and the 20th built for MSC at Chantiers de l'Atlantique. It describes a Pan-Asian venue that combines a restaurant, a bar, and a complimentary grab-and-go, plus the Aqua Food Market with five stalls by the pool. Family additions include two new LEGO rooms. The MSC Yacht Club adds a Royal Duplex Suite. MSC World Atlantic is the next delivery, in October. The release says four more World Class ships are on order at the yard through 2031.

## The guest count, and the Caribbean winter

The 25 Nov release does not print a passenger count. The Points Guy prints 216,638 tons, nearly 2,600 rooms, 20 dining options, and 6,758 passengers with every berth filled. [Baird Maritime](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/passenger/cruise/msc-world-asia-delivery), Alan Bosworth, on 25 Nov prints up to 6,758 passengers. A 26 Nov Baird roundup repeats that line. Cruise Critic prints a different passenger count. This post does not use it.

After summer 2027 the release sends the ship to the South Caribbean for winter 2027/28. [The 20 Aug release](https://www.mscpressarea.com/en_INT/press-releases/msc-cruises-revises-winter-20272028-deployment/) is the one that names the ports. Embarkation is Fort-de-France, Pointe-à-Pitre, and Bridgetown, on 7- and 14-night cruises. The calls it names are Castries, St. George's, Philipsburg, St. John's, Basseterre, Roseau, and Kingstown. Guests already booked on MSC World Asia in the Mediterranean for that winter will have bookings updated, and the line said it would contact them. The Points Guy dates the move to November 2027 and lists the same islands by country. The August release does not print the words November 2027. A Mediterranean port list in that August release belongs to MSC World Europa, not to this ship.

## If you are booked

The ship is delivered. The naming in the release is 28 Nov in Le Havre, and the first guest cruise is 4 Dec from Barcelona. If your winter 2027/28 booking was a Mediterranean cruise on MSC World Asia, the 20 Aug release said the line would contact you. The 6,758 figure is the maximum-berth count in The Points Guy and in Baird Maritime. It is not a number in the delivery release.
