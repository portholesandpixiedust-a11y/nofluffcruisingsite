Carnival opened its 2028/29 bookings for three ships. [The newsroom post](https://www.carnival-news.com/2026/11/18/carnival-cruise-line-opens-160+-sailings-for-2028-29-from-new-orleans-galveston-miami-and-mobile), dated 18 Nov 2026, lists more than 160 sailings on Carnival Sunrise, Carnival Sunshine, and Carnival Valor.

The photo is the lead image on that page. The file name is Carnival Sunrise_social.jpg. The hull name reads Carnival Sunrise. The funnel is red, white, and blue. The ship is alongside a pier, with turquoise water and a low island behind it. The page does not name a photographer. The photo is not a picture of a 2028 fare.

## The four ports

The sailings leave New Orleans, Galveston, Miami, and Mobile. Lengths run from four-day getaways to 14-day Carnival Journeys cruises. The post says guests can visit 21 destinations across The Bahamas, the Caribbean, Mexico, and Central America. More than 50 sailings across the three ships call Celebration Key.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/carnival-opens-2028-29-bookings-for-sunrise-sunshine-and-valor/) on 18 Nov matches those ports, ships, and counts. It also says Celebration Key is expected to welcome 3.5 million guests in 2027. The newsroom post does not print that number. Leave it on [the 30 Sep Celebration Key post](/news/celebration-key-princess-aida-costa-3-5-million-guests-2027/). This post does not treat it as a new count.

## Sunrise: Miami, then New Orleans

Sunrise opens the deployment with a spring season from Miami, beginning in April 2028. Those are four- to eight-day sailings. The newsroom list of calls includes Celebration Key, Nassau, Grand Turk, Ocho Rios, Grand Cayman, Cozumel, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay.

Starting June 2028, Sunrise repositions to New Orleans and sails from the city year-round. The schedule is six-, seven-, eight-, and nine-day cruises to The Bahamas and the Western Caribbean. Named calls include Celebration Key, Nassau, Key West, Cozumel, Belize, Grand Cayman, Montego Bay, and Isla Tropicale. The post highlights a series of seven-day Bahamas cruises with Celebration Key, Nassau, and Key West.

Sunrise also operates one 14-day Carnival Journeys cruise to the Caribbean and Panama, visiting Aruba, Curacao, Cartagena, Colon, and Limon.

## Sunshine: year-round from Galveston

Sunshine sails year-round from Galveston for the first time. The schedule is 29 four-day cruises to Cozumel or Progreso, and 24 ten-day voyages to the Western Caribbean, the Eastern Caribbean, and the Caribbean and Panama. Select Eastern Caribbean itineraries call Celebration Key, Grand Turk, Amber Cove, and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay.

That is a different Galveston plan from [Carnival Jubilee's four- and five-day cruises](/news/carnival-jubilee-short-cruises-galveston-2028/), already on this site. Jubilee is not one of the three ships in today's newsroom post.

## Valor: Mobile, including two long cruises

Valor keeps six-, seven-, and eight-day itineraries from Mobile to The Bahamas and the Western Caribbean. The newsroom list includes Celebration Key, Nassau, Bimini, RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, Cozumel, Belize, Grand Cayman, Montego Bay, and Ocho Rios.

Two 14-day Carnival Journeys voyages are on the schedule. One visits Aruba, Curacao, Cartagena, Colon, and Limon. The other calls San Juan, Aruba, Curacao, Amber Cove, Grand Cayman, and Ocho Rios.

## No fare on the page

The newsroom post does not print a fare. Cruise Industry News does not print one either. The post sends readers to carnival.com, a travel advisor, or 1-800-CARNIVAL.
