MSC World America leaves Miami earlier on Saturday 14 Nov 2026. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/msc-world-america-will-leave-miami-90-minutes-earlier-than-planned/223288), Melissa Mayntz, on 10 Nov 2026, quotes an email to booked guests.

The photo is the lead picture on that story. The credit is fitzcrittle. The caption says the ship is docked in San Juan. It is not a photo of the 14 Nov pier.

The article says the email was posted to Facebook. This check did not open that post. No MSC newsroom post for the new time was open either.

## The time in the email

The quoted lines set the departure at 4:30 p.m. EST. They say the doors close at 3:30 p.m. EST. They tell you to arrive at the check-in time on your latest e-ticket.

Cruise Hive says the ship had been planned for 6:00 p.m. That older clock is the article's sentence. It is not inside the quote. Use the quote for the new time, and use the e-ticket for the slot when you walk in.

Cruise Hive also says that once the doors close you will not be allowed to board, even if the ship is still alongside. That warning is the article's. The quote's words are the 3:30 p.m. close.

## What the article says did not change

The article calls this a 7-night Eastern Caribbean and Bahamas cruise. It says Puerto Plata, San Juan, and Ocean Cay are still on it, and that the port days and times are unchanged. It does not print those days or those clocks. Do not fill them in from a brochure.

It does not print a fare refund or onboard credit. It does not discuss a longer cruise that starts the same afternoon. If your ticket runs past the 7-night, the email still moves the Miami departure, and this article does not speak to the later ports.

The email, as quoted, gives the reason as operational. It does not name a pilot, a berth, or another ship. Cruise Hive prints a capacity of 6,764 guests. That figure is the article's.

## The other ships that afternoon

Cruise Hive lists six other ships leaving PortMiami the same day: Icon of the Seas, Freedom of the Seas, Scarlet Lady, Norwegian Luna, Carnival Sunrise, and Carnival Magic. This check did not open those schedules. The article's estimate of 60,000 guests through the port is not used here.

## If you are boarding on 14 Nov

Plan on a 4:30 p.m. EST sailaway and on doors at 3:30 p.m. EST. Match your ride to the check-in time on the latest e-ticket, not to a listing that still says 6:00 p.m. The port calls in the Cruise Hive report are unchanged, and the clocks for those calls are not in the article. This check found no MSC newsroom post that restates the 4:30 p.m. time.
