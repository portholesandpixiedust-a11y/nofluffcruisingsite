Silversea has put a new poolside restaurant on Silver Dawn in time for a cruise that leaves Rome today. [The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/silversea-extends-its-riviera-pool-and-dining-concept-to-silver-dawn-following-dry-dock-302948579.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Miami, 23 Nov 2026, 2:32 p.m. ET.

The wire's share image is a file named SSC_DA_FrenchWestIndies_Guadeloupe_0321.jpg. The release does not name a photographer. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/silver-dawn-emerges-from-drydock-with-new-pool-and-dining-concept/) on 24 Nov uses Silver-Dawn-Riviera.jpg and does not name a photographer. This post does not use either file.

## What opened on the pool deck

Riviera is a poolside dining, bar, and lounge. The release says it was introduced on Silver Spirit earlier this year and is now on Silver Dawn. The pool deck has new furniture and new seating layouts, plus cabanas, loungers, and umbrellas.

The three outdoor sports are a pickleball court, a golf putting area, and a golf hitting bay. The release says Silver Muse already had a pickleball court, a putting green, and a golf simulator. Dawn's golf pieces are a putting area and a hitting bay. The release does not say Dawn has the simulator.

Bert Hernandez, president of Silversea, says the additions give guests more ways to spend time on board, on Mediterranean and Caribbean seasons still ahead. The release does not print a cover charge for Riviera or a fee for the court. It also does not say a meal at Riviera is included.

Cruise Industry News on 24 Nov places the dry dock in Genoa. The release does not name the yard. The same article describes Riviera as part restaurant and part bar, with drinks, fresh seafood, morning yoga, and evening soirées. Those four details are not in the release. The article does not add a price.

The release lists what Silversea calls included on its ships: a butler in every suite, more than one restaurant, 24-hour in-suite dining, wines, spirits, and other drinks served around the ship, entertainment, Wi-Fi, gratuities, port fees, and taxes. Riviera is not named inside that list. This post does not move it in.

## The cruise that leaves today

The first cruise is 25 Nov 2026, 14 days from Civitavecchia, which the release also calls Rome, to Lisbon. The release says it calls at 10 ports in Italy, Malta, Tunisia, Spain, and Portugal. The ports it names are Naples, Valletta, Trapani in Sicily, Tunis, Cagliari in Sardinia, Cartagena, Malaga, and Cádiz. Valletta and Malaga are overnights. The word in the release is "including," so this post does not treat those eight names as the full list of 10. There is no day-by-day and no fare.

The release says the guests on this cruise are the first to use the new spaces. If you are on the 25 Nov sailing, the printed change is the pool deck and the three sports, and the release does not print a charge for them. If you are booked on a later Mediterranean or Caribbean cruise on Silver Dawn, Hernandez's line in the release puts the same additions on those seasons, and it does not print a date when they would come off the ship.
