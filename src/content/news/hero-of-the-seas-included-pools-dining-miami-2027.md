Royal Caribbean posted on 21 Oct 2026 that [Hero of the Seas](/ships/royal-caribbean/hero-of-the-seas/) includes 9 pools, 9 whirlpools, and 17 dining spots. The [post](https://x.com/RoyalCaribbean/status/2112921382518427876), at 10:58 a.m. ET, also says the ship arrives in Miami in summer 2027.

The link opens the [US ship page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-ships/hero-of-the-seas). That page still prints 28 dining venues. If you are adding the fare up, keep the 17 and the 28 as separate counts.

The post text is short. "What's included" is 9 pools, 9 whirlpools, 17 dining spots, shows and entertainment, and dozens of thrills. The second line is the Miami arrival in summer 2027.

No same-day story turned up on Royal Caribbean Blog, Cruise Industry News, Seatrade, Travel Weekly, or USA Today. Cruise Industry News's latest Hero piece found here is 9 Oct. The trade write-ups of the feature list are from the 30 Mar reveal.

## Hero of the Seas pools

Nine pools is the number to use. It is in the post, on the ship page, and on the [things-to-do page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-ships/hero-of-the-seas/things-to-do). That page calls it the most swim space on any ship at sea. It also says three exclusive pools, including the two largest swim-up bars at sea, and three kid-friendly pools.

The Hideaway is the adults-only area. The page says it has two pools, aft views, the largest swim-up bar at sea, and an infinity pool. Swim & Tonic is the expanded adults pool, with more in-water loungers. Splashaway Bay, in Surfside, has two drench buckets, 12 water jets, two waterslides for kids, and a Baby Bay.

A chill module on that page is marked (9). The names are Coconut Cove, Royal Bay, Water's Edge, Cloud 17, The Cove, The Grove, Swim & Tonic, The Hideaway, and Splashaway Bay. Hideaway appears once on that list. The same page says Hideaway has two pools. Keep the total at nine.

The [30 Mar release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbeans-hero-of-the-seas-introduces-the-next-evolution-of-family-vacation-experiences-302728652.html) says Coconut Cove is new, beside The Lime and Coconut, with Central Park views. The [6 Oct guide](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/guides/hero-of-the-seas-cruise) calls Coconut Cove cantilevered and calls Royal Bay the largest pool at sea.

[Meyer Turku](https://www.meyerturku.fi/en/products-and-technology/world-class-cruise-ships/hero-of-the-seas/) still says Icon Class ships have 7 swimming pools. That sentence conflicts with Royal Caribbean's nine. Quote nine.

The 9 whirlpools are in the X post. They are not in the included paragraph on the page that link opens. A [26 Jun guide](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/guides/free-things-on-hero-of-the-seas) gives Cloud 17 one cantilevered whirlpool. The 30 Mar release gives the Ultimate Family Treehouse one private whirlpool. Those two lines are not a count of nine.

## Hero of the Seas neighborhoods and features

The 30 Mar release, the 6 Oct guide, and the Japan page all say eight neighborhoods. The English pages name Surfside, Central Park, the Royal Promenade, and The Hideaway. The [21 Jul guide](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/guides/top-10-things-to-do-on-hero-of-the-seas) names Thrill Island. A full list of eight English names is not in the 30 Mar release. This post does not fill the gap from a fan site.

Central Park has more than 30,500 real plants, in the 30 Mar release. The Pearl, on the Royal Promenade, is called the world's largest kinetic art sculpture in that release.

Category 6 is called the largest waterpark at sea. The things-to-do page says six waterslides, two new family raft slides, the first funnel raft slide at sea, and new Storm Chasers twists. Splashaway Bay's two kid slides sit outside that six. The included block puts Category 6 in the fare.

Crown's Edge is 154 feet above the ocean. The 6 Oct guide puts it in the upgrade column, with the Grove Suite Sun Deck. The 21 Oct post says "dozens of thrills" and does not price them. Budget Crown's Edge as extra until the fare page says it is included.

The included block also names FlowRider, rock climbing, ice skating, mini golf, and laser tag. The 26 Jun guide calls Lost Dunes a nine-hole course. The 30 Mar release names courts for pickleball, basketball, and soccer. Absolute Zero is called the largest ice arena at sea in that release.

The things-to-do page leads its entertainment block with The SpongeBob Musical, the AquaTheater, and Absolute Zero. The included paragraph says three headline shows and live music at 10 spots are in the fare. It does not print the three titles in that sentence. The Royal Theater show is covered in [Hero of the Seas will stage The SpongeBob Musical in the Royal Theater](/news/hero-of-the-seas-spongebob-musical/).

The [rooms page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-ships/hero-of-the-seas/rooms) says the Ultimate Family Treehouse sleeps up to 12, runs three decks, and has 4 full bathrooms, a rooftop whirlpool, and a two-story teen hideout. The Japan page says 30 cabin categories and 12 suite types, and it calls Coastal Kitchen the suite dining room.
