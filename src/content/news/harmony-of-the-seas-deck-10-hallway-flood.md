Warm water flooded a Deck 10 hallway on Harmony of the Seas shortly before 7am on 29 Sep 2026. Royal Caribbean Blog and Cruise Hive both trace that to Facebook posts from one passenger, Kendra, credited as Kendra the Cruising farmer. Royal Caribbean has not commented. Neither report announces an itinerary change.

You are on a five-night cruise if this is your ship. It left Port Canaveral on 26 Sep 2026. Cruise Hive says Perfect Day at CocoCay was 27 Sep. Cozumel is the last call. Both outlets still list a Port Canaveral return on 1 Oct 2026. Royal Caribbean Blog cites CruiseMapper for that return.

## What she posted

Cruise Hive places the flooding shortly before 7:00am on 29 Sep 2026. Royal Caribbean Blog says she shared the first video around 8:00am EST, near the scheduled Cozumel arrival. Those are two times. One is when the water was running. One is when the video went up.

She wrote, in the words both outlets quote, that it was a first in 92 cruises. She called it a busted line. She said the crew was handling it well. She said the water was warm, and that it steamed up her phone while she recorded. Cruise Hive says she did not report burns or injuries.

Royal Caribbean Blog says the flood was on Deck 10. A video shows water pooling outside staterooms 10321 and 10319. The blog says both are aft balcony cabins overlooking the Boardwalk. If one of those is your cabin, that is the spot the video shows.

## How many cabins

The two outlets do not use the same count.

Royal Caribbean Blog's lede says a burst pipe flooded several passenger cabins and a corridor. The same article says it was not clear which cabins were affected. The cabin numbers it confirms are 10319 and 10321, from water outside those doors.

Cruise Hive calls it a suspected broken line. It says the passenger believes only two staterooms flooded.

Treat "several cabins" as Royal Caribbean Blog's framing. Treat "two staterooms" as what Cruise Hive says the passenger believes. The shared, visible fact in both reports is water in the Deck 10 hallway outside those two aft balcony cabins.

Cruise Hive says it is not clear what caused the line to fail, or what kind of line it was. Royal Caribbean Blog's headline calls it a burst pipe because that is how the passenger described a busted line. Royal Caribbean has not confirmed a cause. Cruise Hive says the line has not commented.

## What crew did

Her follow-up, in both reports, is about two hours later. She wrote that less than two hours after the flooding, the carpet had been pulled up in the affected room.

Cruise Hive says that photo looks into a stateroom where a crew member is working. The carpet inside is up. Drying fans are out. Royal Caribbean Blog says she praised the crew for moving fast and for checking on passengers.

That is the passenger's account of the cleanup. It is not a Royal Caribbean statement about repairs, relocations, or refunds.

Neither Royal Caribbean Blog nor Cruise Hive reports a move to another cabin. Neither reports a compensation offer, onboard credit, or fare refund for the flood. Do not put one in your budget from these posts.

## Your sailing

Cruise Hive says this was day four of the five-night Western Caribbean cruise. The ship left Port Canaveral on 26 Sep 2026, called CocoCay on 27 Sep, and was due in Cozumel when the hallway flooded. The return in that report is Port Canaveral on 1 Oct 2026.

Royal Caribbean Blog describes the same five-night Port Canaveral cruise, with CocoCay and Cozumel, and the same 1 Oct return via CruiseMapper.

Cruise Hive says no alteration to the itinerary has been announced. Keep Cozumel and the 1 Oct return on your plan until the app or a guest-services note says otherwise.

## What you should do

If you are in 10319, 10321, or a nearby Deck 10 aft cabin on this sailing, photograph the cabin and the hall, then talk to guest services on board. Ask what they will do about wet carpet or a wet cabin. These reports do not answer that.

If you are elsewhere on this cruise, the published ports are still CocoCay, Cozumel, and Port Canaveral on 1 Oct. This hallway report does not change them.

If you board Harmony on a later date, nothing here is a fleet-wide notice. It is one passenger's posts from 29 Sep 2026, written up the same day by Royal Caribbean Blog and Cruise Hive. The photos in those stories are hers. This page uses a separate Wikimedia photo of the ship.
