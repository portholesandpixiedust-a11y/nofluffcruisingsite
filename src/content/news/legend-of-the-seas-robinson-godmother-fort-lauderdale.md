Royal Caribbean put a date and a godmother on Legend of the Seas' U.S. chapter. The line's 15 Sep 2026 newsroom release is Tier 1 for the naming and the Fort Lauderdale Caribbean shape.

## Naming day

Retired U.S. Air Force Gen. Lori J. Robinson is godmother. The ceremony is Veterans Day, 11 Nov 2026, in Fort Lauderdale. Robinson is the first woman to lead a U.S. combatant command. Royal is tying the date to military service recognition, not only a marketing splash.

## How you get Icon Class from South Florida

After a European summer, Legend moves to Fort Lauderdale for Caribbean seasons. Royal's release lists six-night Western Caribbean sailings and eight-night Southern Caribbean sailings that include Aruba and Curaçao. Every Legend vacation from Fort Lauderdale calls Perfect Day at CocoCay.

Cruise Hive filled in the calendar around naming. A 13-night transatlantic leaves Barcelona on 25 Oct 2026 and reaches Fort Lauderdale on 7 Nov. A three-night CocoCay cruise leaves 8 Nov and returns at 6am on 11 Nov for the ceremony. A marketed Legend Naming Cruise is another three-night CocoCay trip after the naming. Regular Caribbean weeks start 14 Nov 2026 through an April 2027 Barcelona reposition, per that same Tier 2 report.
