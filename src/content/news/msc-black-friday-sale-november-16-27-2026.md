MSC Cruises put a Black Friday window on the calendar. [The line's release](https://www.mscpressarea.com/en_US/press-releases/msc-cruises-announces-black-friday-sale-for-cruises-around-the-world/), datelined Miami on 16 Nov 2026, says the deals are available 16 Nov through 27 Nov.

The photo is the lead image on [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/msc-offers-up-to-40-off-onboard-credit-for-black-friday/) the same day. The alt text is MSC Seashore Ocean Cay. The file name is MSC-Seashore-Ocean-Cay-MSC-Marine-Reserve.jpg. The bow lettering reads MSC SEASHORE. The page does not print a photographer. The image on the MSC release is a logo file, global.png, so this post uses the Cruise Industry News file. The photo is not a picture of a fare.

## What the release lists

Four lines:

- Up to 40% off cruises.
- Up to $1,000 in onboard credit.
- Free balcony upgrades.
- Kids sail free.

The release does not print a sample fare. It does not print a child's age, how many children, or which cabin the balcony upgrade starts from. It does not print a promo code. It says the deal can be used for 2027 vacations, including spring break, summer, and winter holidays.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/msc-offers-up-to-40-off-onboard-credit-for-black-friday/) on 16 Nov prints the same four offer lines and the same dates. It says the window is for bookings made 16 Nov through 27 Nov. The release says the deals are available on those dates. This post does not treat those two phrases as a conflict.

## Where the maps differ

The release says the deal can apply to most itineraries around the world. It then names four U.S. homeports, Miami, Port Canaveral, Galveston, and Seattle, or Mediterranean and Northern Europe itineraries. Rome, Barcelona, and Venice are the European examples. It says Alaska sailings for summer 2027 leave from Seattle.

Cruise Industry News says the deal applies to most itineraries around the world sailing from Miami, Port Canaveral, Galveston, or Alaska, and it attributes that sentence to the company. It does not print Mediterranean, Northern Europe, or Seattle as a homeport name. Those two geographies are not merged here. If you are booking a European cruise, use the release. If you are booking Alaska, the release's port word is Seattle.

The release says four ships will sail from Miami this winter and does not name them. It says Port Canaveral has two ships on three- to seven-night Bahamas and Caribbean itineraries and does not name them. Cruise Industry News does not add those names either.

## The dollar examples are not on the release

Cruise Industry News prints four balcony examples for a family of four: up to $3,100 on a seven-night Seascape cruise from Galveston, up to $1,600 on a four-night Seashore cruise from Port Canaveral, up to $4,400 on a seven-night World America cruise from Miami, and up to $4,500 on a seven-night Poesia cruise from Seattle. The release page read for this post does not print those totals. They stay on the Cruise Industry News article.

The release says to book on MSC Cruises. It does not print a phone number. The sale dates in the release run through 27 Nov 2026. No fare for a named sailing is on that page.
