Norwegian Escape cut the last stretch of its Canada and New England cruise to stay ahead of the East Coast nor'easter. Cruise Industry News reported the change on 26 Sep 2026 and quoted a letter from Captain Simone Croci. No Norwegian newsroom release was located. Every clock below comes from that one Tier 2 report.

## What changed on this cruise

The ship left New York on 18 Sep 2026 on a seven-night Canada and New England cruise. Newport, Boston, and Sydney ran as planned. Halifax did not keep its original window.

The captain's letter said Escape would leave Halifax earlier than planned. A nor'easter was forecast for the northeastern United States and the Canada Atlantic coast. Cruise Industry News says the ship sailed from Halifax at 5:00pm on 24 Sep 2026, five hours early. The letter tied that exit to a safer run into New York before the storm's strongest conditions.

The homeport call moved up with it. Cruise Industry News says Escape was expected in New York just after midnight on 26 Sep 2026, about seven hours early. If you were meeting the ship for a morning pickup, that overnight dock time is the one in the letter. Recheck the app before you lock a car.

Cruise Industry News calls Escape a 2015-built ship with 4,200 passengers. That figure is from the same report.

## What happens to Halifax tours

Norwegian adjusted shore excursions booked through the line to the shorter Halifax window. Tours that could not fit the new hours were cancelled. Cruise Industry News says those refunds posted to the onboard account in full. A tour you bought outside Norwegian is your own refund problem. The letter, as printed, covers company tours.

## The Barcelona crossing

After New York, Escape is scheduled to start a 16-night transatlantic to Barcelona. Cruise Industry News says that voyage calls in Canada, Portugal, Spain, and the United Kingdom. This report does not change that crossing. If you are booked on it, watch for a separate letter. The early New York turn is what this note covers.

## What you should do

If you were meeting Escape in New York, the letter put the dock just after midnight on 26 Sep. Confirm that pier time in the app if a transfer is still in play. If a Halifax tour disappeared, look for the onboard-account refund before you leave the ship. If you join the Barcelona crossing, read any newer letter before you lock the flight out of New York.
