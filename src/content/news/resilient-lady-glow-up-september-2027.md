Resilient Lady's next chapter is a date, not a mood board. [Virgin Voyages' release](https://www.virginvoyages.com/press/latest-releases/uncharted), Miami, 17 Nov 2026, says the refreshed ship debuts on 2 Sep 2027. The first sailing with the new pieces is a 10-night from Barcelona that day. [The Ahoy recap](https://www.virginvoyages.com/ahoy/stories/uncharted) calls it a round trip and names Rome, Amalfi, Malta, and the French Riviera. The release does not print those ports, and it does not print a fare.

The photo is on [Seatrade's article](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/hotel-operations-entertainment/hawaii-new-restaurant-wellness-focus-ahead-for-virgin-voyages) the same day. The credit line is Virgin Voyages. The caption says Resilient Lady will debut a number of the changes after the glow-up in September 2027. The file name is Resilient-Lady.jpg. The stern name reads RESILIENT LADY. The hull reads VIRGIN VOYAGES. The frame is the ship at sea. It does not show Yim or The London Club.

## Yim, in the Razzle Dazzle space

The release says Yim will elevate Razzle Dazzle in September 2027. The name nods to the Thai word for smile. The menu sits under Refuel, one of the wellbeing currents below. The release puts Yim in the same family as Ariya, Rojo, and Lucky Lotus: a restaurant that belongs to one ship, not the whole fleet.

The Ahoy page calls Yim the final Razzle Dazzle takeover. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/virgin-voyages-details-resilient-lady-glow-up-loyalty-only-london-club/) says Yim will replace Razzle Dazzle. Seatrade says the Thai restaurant is housed in Razzle Dazzle. If you liked Razzle Dazzle on this ship, plan on a different room from the 2 Sep 2027 sailing.

## The London Club

The club opens on Resilient Lady in fall 2027. The release stocks it with records, memorabilia, tea, and scones, and a concealed entrance, a Keeper of the Door, and a secret phrase.

You do not buy a ticket. The release says access is earned through loyalty, not through a suite booking and not through an extra fee. Cruise Industry News quotes chief operating officer Michelle Bentubo: it is not a place you can buy your way into. The Ahoy page says the room is for the most loyal sailors in the evenings, and that by day it has papers, tea, fresh scones, chess by the window, and records you can play.

No page in this set prints a voyage count. Cruise Industry News recalls that Sailing Club tiers were designed around three and five voyages, and that some sailors have now been back many more times. Those sentences are about the old tiers and about repeat guests. They are not a published door rule for The London Club. If your account does not say you are in, this post cannot tell you that you are.

## Replenish, and a cabin built for sleep

Replenish is a new space on this ship. The release lists a red-light sauna, a cold cabin, light therapy, compression boots, a hyperbaric chamber designed for two, and loungers looking at the sea. You can book a shorter Cold and Light session, a Full Replenish, or Golden Hour, which is sauna time and loungers as the sun goes down. The Ahoy page adds a rinse in the Golden Hour description and calls the loungers sea-view loungers.

The sleep cabin is still being built as a product. The release describes blackout conditions, a comfortably cool room, cooling linens, and soft evening lighting, and a potential Night Host who would set the room around your evening. It says sailors will be able to book the category on the website or through Sailor Services. The Ahoy page, read 18 Nov 2026, says the cabin is still in development and that the Night Host is something the line is imagining. [Travel Weekly UK](https://travelweekly.co.uk/news/virgin-voyages-to-make-hawaii-debut-in-2028) says the category is yet to be named. It is not a cabin you can add to a booking today from anything these pages print.

## Vibrance, on that first sailing

Vibrance is the name for the wider wellbeing idea: dining, rest, movement, and time with people you travel with. The five currents in the release are Refuel, Reset, Relax, Recharge, and Renew. It starts on the 2 Sep 2027 Barcelona sailing. The release says Bentubo will be on that voyage. Pieces of Vibrance spread to the other ships later next year. The Ahoy page says late in 2027.
