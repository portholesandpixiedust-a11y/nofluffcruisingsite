Oasis of the Seas is running late into Cape Liberty, New Jersey. Cruise Hive reported the delay on 19 Sep 2026, citing Royal Caribbean guest messaging after two medical evacuations that sent the ship back toward Nassau.

## What changed for this sailing

You should expect arrival around 2pm on Sunday 20 Sep 2026. Debarkation is set to start around 2:30pm. The original window was closer to 6am. Build buffer into airport transfers and rides if you leave that morning.

## Next sailing boards late

The following embarkation is also late. Royal told guests the delay looks like seven to eight hours. Everyone is expected onboard by 9:30pm. Plan food, childcare, and luggage drop around a long afternoon, not a morning check-in.

## Port Canaveral is cancelled

Tuesday 22 Sep 2026 Port Canaveral becomes a sea day. That cut is the biggest itinerary hit for guests already booked on the next cruise.

Perfect Day at CocoCay on Wednesday 23 Sep still happens. Arrival moves earlier to 7:45am from the prior 8:30am call. Use the extra morning time on the island if you can.

## Compensation Royal listed

Cruise Hive listed the goodwill package Royal sent guests. Per stateroom you get 60 minutes of complimentary internet.

Flight changes Royal arranged for you are handled by the line. If you booked flights on your own, keep receipts. Royal said it will reimburse change fees up to $200 per person for domestic tickets and up to $400 per person for international tickets.

Onboard credit by cabin category:

| Cabin | Onboard credit |
|---|---|
| Inside / oceanview | $100 |
| Balcony / Neighborhood | $150 |
| Suites | $200 |

Add $50 for each third or fourth guest in the same stateroom.

Prepaid Royal Caribbean Port Canaveral shore excursions are cancelled with the port call. Credit lands as onboard credit first. Leftover balance returns to the original card after that.

## What you should do

Watch your Royal Caribbean app and email for the final dock time. Rebook flights only after you know when you clear the terminal. Save every airline change-fee receipt before you ask for reimbursement. If you bought a Port Canaveral tour outside Royal, contact that operator yourself.
