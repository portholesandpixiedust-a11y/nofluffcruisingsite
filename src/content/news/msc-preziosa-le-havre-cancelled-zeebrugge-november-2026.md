You lose Le Havre on Thursday 5 Nov 2026 if you are on MSC Preziosa. The ship stays overnight in Zeebrugge.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/french-strike-forces-msc-preziosa-to-cancel-le-havre-call/222538), Calvin Musquez, and [Cruise.blog](https://cruise.blog/2026/11/cruise-cancels-france-massive-protests-sweeping-europe) both dated their stories 3 Nov 2026. Both print a notice signed by Capt. Francesco Iacono. Cruise Hive dates that notice 3 Nov. Cruise.blog does not put a separate date on the letter.

The photo is Cruise Hive's lead image. The credit is Alexandre Prevot. The caption says MSC Preziosa docked in France. It does not date the frame. It does not say the photo is Le Havre, or this week. The page has a second frame, credited to Stefan Lambauer. This post does not use it.

## What the notice says

The shared lines are short.

A strike in France on Thursday 5 Nov is the reason. Cruise Hive's quote calls it "planned nationwide strike and protest action." Cruise.blog's quote says industrial action is expected to significantly affect transport and port operations across the country.

Both quotes use the same decision. The call is cancelled "in the interest of providing a safe and comfortable guest experience." The ship stays in Zeebrugge overnight.

Cruise.blog adds the captain's close. The situation is "beyond our control."

## The clocks, from one outlet

The hours are Cruise Hive's. Cruise.blog does not print them.

Zeebrugge was the 4 Nov call. Le Havre was 5 Nov. The ship now stays overnight and leaves Zeebrugge at 6:00 p.m. on 5 Nov, not at 6:00 p.m. on 4 Nov.

Cruise Hive calls Preziosa a 4,345-guest ship. Cruise.blog says the line lists capacity over 4,300, and calls the ship Fantasia class, 139,072 gross tons, launched in 2013. Use those as each page's figure.

## Which cruise this hits

The two pages do not print the same itinerary. Do not merge them into one route.

Cruise Hive says overlapping cruises are affected, with embarkations in Le Havre, Southampton, Hamburg, and Amsterdam. It says some people were booked to start a seven-night cruise in Le Havre on 5 Nov. It says MSC has not said in public how those people will be accommodated.

Cruise.blog says some people were due to get off in Le Havre, because MSC uses different ends of the cruise on the same ship. It points an eight-night Amsterdam-to-Rotterdam list at CruiseMapper, with a second Zeebrugge call on 10 Nov. This desk did not open that page. Treat the port names as Cruise.blog's citation.

## The tour refund

Both articles say a Le Havre shore excursion bought through MSC goes back to the onboard account. Cruise.blog calls the refund automatic. Cruise Hive says the Shore Excursions Desk can sell more Zeebrugge tours.

Neither article prints a fare refund. Neither prints a flat onboard credit for the lost day. A tour you bought on your own is outside that sentence. Ask that operator.

This is a different ship from the October cut. [MSC Virtuosa stayed overnight in Southampton](/news/msc-virtuosa-southampton-overnight-la-rochelle-le-havre-october-2026/) and dropped Le Havre on 30 Oct. That letter is not this notice.

## If your ticket says Le Havre on 5 Nov

Call MSC, or the advisor who booked you, before you go to that pier. The notice these two articles print does not give you a new port, a transfer, or a time to board.

If you are already on the ship, the replacement day is Zeebrugge. Check the daily program for the hours, because only Cruise Hive prints 6:00 p.m. on 5 Nov.
