Virgin Voyages put a loyalty room and a Hawaii date on one release. [The Uncharted page](https://www.virginvoyages.com/press/latest-releases/uncharted), datelined Miami on 17 Nov 2026, says The London Club opens on Resilient Lady in fall 2027, and that you earn it. It also opens bookings on 1 Dec 2026 for Brilliant Lady's first Hawaii cruise.

The photo is the lead image on [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/virgin-voyages-details-resilient-lady-glow-up-loyalty-only-london-club/), also 17 Nov. The file name is res_lady1.jpg. The bow reads RESILIENT LADY. The hull reads VIRGIN VOYAGES. The article text retrieved does not name a photographer. The Virgin page image is a gradient file, not this ship. Seatrade's lead file is an Uncharted graphic. The credit line on that graphic is Virgin Voyages.

## The club you cannot buy

The release says access is earned through loyalty, rather than purchased through a suite booking or an additional fee. It describes a concealed entrance, a Keeper of the Door, and a secret phrase, with records, memorabilia, tea, and scones. It does not print how many voyages get you in.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/virgin-voyages-details-resilient-lady-glow-up-loyalty-only-london-club/) says the room is only for repeat guests. It quotes chief operating officer Michelle Bentubo that it is not a place you can buy your way into, and it quotes chief executive Nirmal Saverimuttu that the old tiers were built around three and five voyages. Those voyage counts are his remarks in that article. They are not a threshold in the release. [Seatrade](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/hotel-operations-entertainment/hawaii-new-restaurant-wellness-focus-ahead-for-virgin-voyages), Anne Kalosh, 17 Nov, calls it a hidden spot for loyalists and does not print a number either.

The release also says Sisters at Sea, the February fleet meetup, counts as two voyages toward Sailing Club status. It does not print the day. The [16 Feb 2027 meetup post](/news/virgin-sisters-at-sea-four-ships-february-2027/) already has the date and the coordinates. This release does not reprint them.

The same release says Sailing Club benefits run through 31 Dec 2028. That end date is already on the [24 Sep post](/news/virgin-voyages-sailing-club-status-match-2028/). This page treats 31 Dec 2028 as a repeat, and the double-voyage credit as the new line.

## Yim, then Barcelona on 2 Sept

Yim replaces Razzle Dazzle in September 2027. The release says the name nods to the Thai word for smile, and that the menu is built around Refuel. The refreshed ship starts with a 10-night from Barcelona on 2 Sept 2027. Bentubo joins that voyage, the release says. Cruise Industry News prints the same Barcelona date and says the yard work was fitted into 14 days. The release does not print 14 days.

Seatrade prints the restaurant as Thailand Yim and calls Resilient Lady Virgin's first ship. The release prints Yim and does not call her the first ship. This post uses the release on both lines.

The release also lists Replenish, with a red-light sauna, a cold cabin, light therapy, compression boots, and a hyperbaric chamber for two. A new cabin category is bookable and has no name and no fare in the release. A Night Host is described as potential. Seatrade prints a night host and does not print the word potential. Redemption Spa becomes The Well after the dry dock. The release says the physical spaces do not change.

Fleetwide, the Test Kitchen adds an a la carte option that is included in the fare. The five-course tasting menu stays. Cruise Industry News and Seatrade both print that the a la carte choice is included.

## Hawaii opens 1 Dec

Brilliant Lady's Hawaii cruise is one time, the release says. It is 18 nights, roundtrip Los Angeles, 11 to 29 Mar 2028. Bookings open 1 Dec 2026. The calls are Hilo and Kona, Kahului, Nawiliwili, an overnight in Honolulu, and Ensenada, plus 10 sea days. No fare is printed.

Cruise Industry News prints the 11 to 29 Mar dates and the 1 Dec open. It does not print the ports. Seatrade prints the ports and the 11 Mar departure. It does not print 29 Mar, the 10 sea days, or 1 Dec.

If you want the Hawaii cruise, the date in the release is 1 Dec 2026, and the fare is not on the page. If you want The London Club, the release does not say which status opens the door.
