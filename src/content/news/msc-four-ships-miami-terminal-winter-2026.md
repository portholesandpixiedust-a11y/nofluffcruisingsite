MSC has four ships on its Miami terminal this winter, and the release tells you which ship matches which length. [The MSC release](https://www.mscpressarea.com/en_US/press-releases/msc-cruises-makes-history-in-miami-with-record-four-ships-sailing-from-msc-miami/) is datelined Miami, 11 Nov 2026. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/msc-marking-portmiami-four-ship-milestone-on-veterans-day/) published the same four names that day.

The photo is the lead image on the Cruise Industry News story. The file name is MSC-World-America-Miami.jpg. The hull reads MSC World America, alongside Miami. The article text retrieved does not name a photographer. The MSC release page on this check showed the line's logo, so that file is not used here.

## Pick the ship by the length

The release pairs the four ships this way:

- MSC World America: seven-night Eastern and Western Caribbean sailings.
- MSC Seaside: three-night or four-night getaways to The Bahamas.
- MSC Meraviglia: Caribbean itineraries of four to 14 nights.
- MSC Poesia: longer Western and Southern Caribbean itineraries.

Cruise Industry News prints 10 nights and 11 nights on that Poesia line. The release does not print those two numbers. Use the release's word, longer, unless your booking shows 10 or 11.

The release does not print a fare. It does not print a guest count. It does not list the ports inside those Caribbean patterns. It does not give the first sail date for each ship this winter.

## The ceremony, and a different Poesia story

The release says the line marked the four-ship winter with a Veterans Day ceremony aboard MSC Meraviglia. Veteran team members, elected officials, community stakeholders, and PortMiami leaders were on the guest list. Lynn Torrent, president of MSC Cruises North America, is the executive in the quote. Jonathan Daniels, PortMiami director and CEO, is quoted on welcoming the four ships.

The release does not mention the Coast Guard hold. If you were on the sailing that did not leave PortMiami on 1 Nov, that is a different post: [the hold](/news/msc-poesia-coast-guard-miami-november-2026/) and [the cancelled sailing](/news/msc-poesia-miami-sailing-cancelled-refund-credit-november-2026/). This release is the winter deployment. It does not reprint a refund or a credit.

## If you are picking a Miami week

Match the length to the ship before you lock a flight. Seven nights is World America in this release. A three-night or four-night Bahamas trip is Seaside. Four to 14 nights is Meraviglia. A longer Western or Southern Caribbean cruise is Poesia, and the 10-night and 11-night figures are the Cruise Industry News wording. No fare is printed in the release or in that article.
