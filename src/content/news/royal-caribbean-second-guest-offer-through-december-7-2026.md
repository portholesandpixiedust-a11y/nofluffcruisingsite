Royal Caribbean's promotion terms still have two offers that run through 7 Dec. [The terms page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/terms-and-conditions/promotions), read 24 Nov 2026, is the page that prints the dates and the cabin amounts.

[The Black Friday deals page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-deals/black-friday) leads with a file named St_Thomas_Catamaran_Snorkeling. The page does not name a photographer. This post does not use that file.

## What still runs through 7 Dec

BOGO60 is 60 percent off the cruise fare of the second guest. The first guest pays the full fare. New, individual, and named group bookings qualify if they are made from 3 Nov through 7 Dec 2026, on a sailing that departs on or after 4 Nov 2026.

Free third and fourth guests are a $0 cruise fare for those two guests, on select sailings of 3 nights or longer, including transatlantic sailings. Bookings run from 6 Nov through 7 Dec 2026. The sailing must depart on or after 7 Nov 2026. Taxes, fees, and port expenses are extra for every guest.

The terms exclude sailings that depart on or during these windows: 19 Nov through 27 Nov 2026; Navigator of the Seas on 29 Nov, 3 Dec, and 10 Dec 2026; 17 Dec 2026 through 4 Jan 2027; Navigator of the Seas Chinese New Year sailings from 4 Feb through 8 Feb 2027; spring-break sailings from 5 Mar through 4 Apr 2027; Alaska Cruise Tour sailings from 14 May through 17 Sep 2027; 28 May through 20 Aug 2027; 18 Nov through 26 Nov 2027; 16 Dec 2027 through 5 Jan 2028; 10 Mar through 31 Mar 2028; 7 Apr through 17 Apr 2028; Alaska Cruise Tour sailings from 11 May through 8 Sep 2028; 26 May through 18 Aug 2028; 16 Nov through 24 Nov 2028; 21 Dec 2028 through 5 Jan 2029; and 9 Mar through 4 Apr 2029. The May-to-August lines, and the March and April 2028 lines, do not name a region. A [consumer sheet](https://creative.rccl.com/sales/royal/promotions/26028557_November_2026_Monthlong_Consumer.pdf), file 26028557, footer date 28 Oct 2026, matches the 7 Dec end for both offers and sends the exclusion list back to the terms. Its headline also says 6 Nov through 7 Dec. The BOGO paragraph on that sheet starts the bookings on 3 Nov.

## What closes on 25 Nov

The Black Friday Preview Sale is bookings made on 24 and 25 Nov only, for sailings that depart on or after 25 Nov. The instant savings is per stateroom. On 5 nights or less: $125 inside and ocean view, $200 balcony, and $400 suite. On 6 nights or longer: $225 interior and ocean view, $300 balcony, and $900 suite.

The Cyber Bonus is bookings made from 17 Nov through 25 Nov, for sailings on or after 18 Nov. On 5 nights or less the onboard credit is $25 inside and ocean view, $50 balcony, and $100 suite. On 6 nights or longer it is $50 interior and ocean view, $75 balcony, and $100 suite. The credit has no cash value, is for the cruise only, and expires if it is unused by 10:00 p.m. on the final night.

The deals page prints "up to $900 off" and a Cyber Bonus of up to $100. Those figures match the top cells above. The page does not print 24 Nov, 25 Nov, or 7 Dec. It names Black Friday as 27 Nov, Cyber Monday as 30 Nov, and Travel Tuesday as 1 Dec. It also prints starting-from prices, including $289. This post does not use those prices.

The four named offers can be combined with each other, and with promotional onboard credit, Next Cruise, instant savings, senior, resident, and military discounts, promo codes, free upgrades, and shareholder benefits. BOGO60, the free third and fourth guests, and the Cyber Bonus can also combine with Crown and Anchor. The preview sale cannot. None of the four combines with Standard Group, Interline, travel-agent rates, travel-agent friends and family, weekly sales events, or net rates.

A booking change can remove an offer. A single guest who pays 200 percent of the cruise fare gets the full offer. A single who pays less gets a prorated amount. The terms limit these offers to residents of the United States, Canada, Puerto Rico, and select Caribbean countries.

The same page closes a Flash Deals block on 24 Nov. This post does not list those sailings. Other promo codes on the page have their own amounts. The offer that is still open after 25 Nov, on the terms read today, is the second-guest cut and the free third and fourth guests, both through 7 Dec, on sailings the exclusion list does not remove.
