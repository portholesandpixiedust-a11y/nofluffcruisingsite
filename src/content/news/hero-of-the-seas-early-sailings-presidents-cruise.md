Royal Caribbean is putting two Hero of the Seas sailings in front of the Aug. 11, 2027 inaugural. [Royal Caribbean Blog](https://www.royalcaribbeanblog.com/2026/10/05/royal-caribbean-moved-the-debut-of-its-newest-cruise-ship) on Oct. 5 quotes the email sent to guests already booked on that inaugural. The new dates are Aug. 1 and Aug. 8. The email says Miami. A retelling from the Top Tier session on this week's [President's Cruise](/news/presidents-cruise-2026-icon-of-the-seas/) has been heard as Port Everglades. That is the detail that still does not line up.

This post rests on that one blog's quotation of the email. We do not have the email itself.

## The dates

Aug. 1, 2027 is a 7-night Perfect Day at CocoCay and Western Caribbean cruise. Aug. 8, 2027 is a 3-night Perfect Day at CocoCay getaway. Aug. 11 stays the inaugural sailing. If you want to stay on it, the email says you can disregard the note.

The blog's quotation gives booked guests three choices. Switch to an earlier sailing. Add one. Or stay put.

Staterooms open at the prevailing rate, based on availability, on the afternoon of Wednesday, Oct. 7. A change fee is waived until Oct. 14 if you move earlier. Suites stay held until Oct. 14, then they can go to new bookings.

The same quotation lists the phones. In the U.S., call 1-888-281-9344. In Europe, call +31-367-117936. UK guests have webchat. Everyone else uses the local office number on the website.

The email, as the blog prints it, tells inaugural guests they were the first to know. It also says the line cannot wait to welcome guests onboard in Miami, and that the trip is opening sooner than expected.

## Where President's Cruise guests come in

Top-tier guests on [Icon of the Seas](/ships/royal-caribbean/icon-of-the-seas/) heard a version of this in the room. The session is Top Tier. It is a loyalty event on the President's Cruise.

[Common Ground](/news/they-expected-a-gripe-session-they-got-greece/) is a different hour. That is Michael Bayley's open Q&A, held Sunday. A passenger Facebook post from Sep. 29 already treated the two as separate. It said Common Ground was on the app for Day 2 at 10:30 a.m., and comments guessed that Top Tier had moved to Day 3 after a clash. Day 3 of this cruise is Monday, Oct. 5.

The speaker passengers are clipping from Top Tier is an announcer. Bayley is the Sunday microphone, at Common Ground.

Treat the announcer's wording as approximate. We are not putting quote marks on it. The dates in that room line up with the email: Aug. 1 and Aug. 8, ahead of Aug. 11. The email is the version you can act on. The room is how the week heard it first.

## One thing to fix

The email says Miami. [Royal Caribbean's Hero page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/cruise-ships/hero-of-the-seas), checked the same afternoon, still says the ship arrives in August 2027 in Miami. The [March 30, 2026 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbeans-hero-of-the-seas-introduces-the-next-evolution-of-family-vacation-experiences-302728652.html) set that homeport. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/hero-of-the-seas-hits-water-in-major-construction-milestone/216774) on Sep. 8 had already called the Aug. 11 inaugural a 3-night cruise from Miami to Perfect Day at CocoCay.

Port Everglades is the other version, from the Top Tier retelling. That is Fort Lauderdale. It is a different airport and a different terminal. It is not in the email the blog quotes. It is not on the Hero page.

[iCruise's August 2027 grid](https://www.icruise.com/ships/royal-caribbean-cruises-hero-of-the-seas-cruise-itineraries-august-2027.html), checked Oct. 5, still lists four Miami departures and starts with Aug. 11. Aug. 1 and Aug. 8 are not on it. Fort Lauderdale is not on it.

Hold the flight until royalcaribbean.com prints the sailing. If the announcer said Port Everglades, that line is the one to fix. The written offer says Miami.

A Pinnacle credit has been mentioned around the same room report. Leave it off the booking. The email lists a waived change fee, a suite hold, and a room at the prevailing rate. The Top Tier speaker is an announcer.

## Why this matters

If you hold the Aug. 11 inaugural, you get the first window on the earlier ships. It opens the afternoon of Oct. 7 and the waived change fee runs through Oct. 14. After that, the suites can be sold to someone who was not on the original booking.

You do not have to move. Aug. 11 is still there. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/hero-of-the-seas-hits-water-in-major-construction-milestone/216774) described that sailing as 3 nights from Miami to CocoCay. The email does not cancel it.

Royal Caribbean Blog says this is the third Icon Class ship moved up. Star of the Seas had four showcase sailings before the official launch. Legend of the Seas had three 7-night cruises, July 11, July 18, and July 25, ahead of an original Aug. 2, 2026 sailing. That comparison is the same article. It is one outlet.

Hero is still the ship that takes Icon's Miami place in August 2027. The musical on that ship is a separate item, in [Hero of the Seas will stage The SpongeBob Musical](/news/hero-of-the-seas-spongebob-musical/).

## What we know, and what we don't

**We know:**

- An email to Aug. 11 guests, quoted by Royal Caribbean Blog on Oct. 5, adds Aug. 1 and Aug. 8.
- Aug. 1 is a 7-night CocoCay and Western Caribbean cruise. Aug. 8 is a 3-night CocoCay getaway.
- Inventory opens the afternoon of Oct. 7, at the prevailing rate. Change fees are waived through Oct. 14. Suites are held through Oct. 14.
- The email says Miami.
- Staying on Aug. 11 requires no action.

**We don't know yet:**

- A royalcaribbean.com page for Aug. 1 or Aug. 8. The seller grid we checked still starts on Aug. 11.
- The port calls on the Aug. 1 week. The email names the cruise. It does not list each stop.
- The announcer's exact words. The room wording is approximate.
- Why that room version says Port Everglades when the email says Miami.
- A Pinnacle credit. The written offer is the fee waiver, the suite hold, and the prevailing rate. The Top Tier speaker is an announcer.

If you are on the Aug. 11 booking and you want to be on Hero sooner, call before Oct. 14 and price the move at the prevailing rate. Book the flight for Miami until the line's own page prints the port.
