Concierge Class on Celebrity Cruises no longer lists afternoon canapés. The line's Concierge Class page, checked 28 Sep 2026, lists two complimentary welcome waters on embarkation day and room service with the delivery fee waived. A footnote says a 20 percent gratuity may apply. Cruise Hive, dated 26 Sep 2026, is the report that puts a start date on the change and names the $9.95 fee. No separate Celebrity newsroom announcement was located.

## What Celebrity lists now

The US Concierge Class page is the Tier 1 list. Under amenities it includes two complimentary welcome waters on embarkation day. Under services it includes complimentary room service, with the delivery fee waived. A footnote on that line says a 20 percent gratuity may apply. The same footnote sits on 24-hour room service.

Daily delectable delights is absent from that amenities list. The welcome aboard Concierge Class lunch is still there. Personalized concierge service is still there. Sparkling wine on request, the pillow menu, and the destination seminar are still there too.

The page does not print a start date. It does not print a dollar amount for the waived delivery fee. Treat the waters and the waived fee as what Celebrity is selling today. Treat the calendar and the old price as Cruise Hive's until Celebrity prints them.

## What Cruise Hive dates and prices

Cruise Hive says the change covers sailings that depart after Monday 21 Sep 2026. On those cruises, Concierge Class no longer receives the afternoon tray Celebrity called Daily delectable delights. Cruise Hive says the trays often included fruit, cheese, shrimp, sushi, hummus, charcuterie, and desserts, and that crew could adjust them to what you wanted.

In place of the tray, Cruise Hive describes the same two changes now on the benefits page. The delivery fee is waived. Two bottles of water arrive on embarkation day. Celebrity's page says "welcome waters." Cruise Hive says bottles. Neither source prints a size.

Cruise Hive puts the standard room-service delivery fee at $9.95 per order, plus a 20 percent gratuity. It says that flat fee replaced a $4.95 late-night fee on 30 Dec 2022 and then applied at all hours. It says the $9.95 charge no longer applies in a Concierge Class stateroom, and that you still pay the gratuity.

Celebrity's footnote says the gratuity may apply. Cruise Hive says you still pay it. Budget the 20 percent on each order until your onboard account shows a zero. The $9.95 figure, the 30 Dec 2022 history, and the 21 Sep start date are in the Cruise Hive report only.

Cruise Hive also says Celebrity did not publish a public announcement. The update showed up on the benefits list. That matches the page checked on 28 Sep 2026. The lunch, the concierge, and the upgraded stateroom features stay, per Cruise Hive. The benefits page still lists the lunch and the concierge.

## What people said about the trays

Cruise Hive describes a Reddit thread titled "To honor the loss of concierge canapes." It says the reaction there was largely approval. Commenters called the quality uneven. Several also raised a food-safety point. Trays were left even when the cabin was empty, and items that needed to stay cold could sit at room temperature for hours. The thread is context. The benefit list is the change.

## What you should do

If your Concierge Class sailing departs after 21 Sep 2026, leave afternoon canapés off your plan. Count on two waters on embarkation day and on room service without the delivery fee. Still budget a 20 percent gratuity on each order. If your sailing left on or before 21 Sep, Cruise Hive does not put this change on that cruise. The benefits page has no start date, so open your booking if you sail near that line. Facts above follow Celebrity's Concierge Class page, checked 28 Sep 2026, and Cruise Hive on 26 Sep 2026.
