Carnival opened two more adults-only sailings for 2027. The booking window closes on Friday 16 Oct 2026.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/carnival-adds-two-adults-only-cruises-to-2027-schedule/), 14 Oct 2026, and [Nautical Flock](https://www.nauticalflock.com/news/carnival-paradise-horizon-adults-only-sea-cruises-2027-tampa-galveston), the same day, both report an update from Carnival brand ambassador John Heald. No Carnival newsroom page for these two sailings was found on 14 Oct.

## Which ships and dates?

| Ship | Leaves | From | Length | Ports named |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carnival Paradise | 12 Feb 2027 | Tampa, roundtrip | 6 nights | Cozumel and Isla Tropicale |
| Carnival Horizon | 13 May 2027 | Galveston, roundtrip | 6 nights | Cozumel and Isla Tropicale |

Both are Western Caribbean cruises. Both call Cozumel, Mexico, and Isla Tropicale, Carnival's stop on Roatan, Honduras.

Nautical Flock says Carnival has not published the day-by-day schedule. Cruise Industry News does not print one either. Book the sailing, then read the port times in your confirmation.

## Who can sail?

Nautical Flock spells SEA as Sailings Exclusively for Adults. Cruise Industry News calls the product SEA Cruises and says it is for people 21 and older. Both reports use that age line.

Every person in the stateroom has to be 21 or older on the sail date. If someone in your group is younger, book a regular sailing.

Both outlets say the dates were added because of demand. Cruise Industry News lists expanded casino access, themed parties, and bingo. Nautical Flock says a SEA sailing uses the regular ship and drops kids' programming.

## When do bookings close?

Both reports say Carnival is taking bookings for these two sailings through 16 Oct 2026. Nautical Flock calls that day Friday. Neither report prints a clock time or a time zone.

Neither report prints a fare.

Nautical Flock says Carnival has not said whether anyone already booked on these two dates is affected. If you already hold Carnival Paradise on 12 Feb 2027, or Carnival Horizon on 13 May 2027, open the reservation and read any email from Carnival before you assume the week is unchanged.

Cruise Industry News also says Carnival earlier cancelled a month of Carnival Firenze sailings to make room for four SEA cruises from Long Beach in October and November 2026. Nautical Flock reports those same four cruises. This post did not find a new Firenze notice dated 14 Oct. The two dates in the table are the new ones.

## What this means for you

If you want one of these two weeks, and everyone in the room is 21 or older on the sail date, book by 16 Oct 2026. If anyone is younger, pick another cruise. If you already hold one of these dates, check the reservation. Facts above follow Cruise Industry News and Nautical Flock on 14 Oct 2026.
