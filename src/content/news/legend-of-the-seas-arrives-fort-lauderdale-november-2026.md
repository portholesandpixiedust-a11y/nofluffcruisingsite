Legend of the Seas is in Fort Lauderdale. [Royal Caribbean's release](https://royalcaribbeanpresscenter.com/news/royal-caribbeans-legend-of-the-seas-arrives-in-the-us-for-the-first-time/), datelined Miami on 7 Nov 2026, says the ship arrived that day. It was ahead of the first sailing from Port Everglades.

If you hold a Fort Lauderdale date, the new fact is the arrival. The release says this is the first Icon Class ship to homeport at Port Everglades. Icon of the Seas and Star of the Seas already sail from Florida. Michael Bayley, president and CEO, is quoted on that point.

The photo is the image on that press page. The file name includes JGraham and 20260620. The page does not print a caption. It does not say the frame is the 7 Nov arrival.

## The short trip, then the naming

The release says the Caribbean season starts with a 3-night getaway to Perfect Day at CocoCay. It does not print the sail date.

[Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/icon-3-legend-of-the-seas-makes-stateside-debut), Anne Kalosh, on 8 Nov, says the ship kicked off the season with Sunday's 3-night getaway to Perfect Day at CocoCay. Sunday was 8 Nov.

[The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/cruise/royal-caribbean-legend-florida-debut), Mackenzie Roche, on 7 Nov, said you would board Sunday for that trip. The article says the sailing calls CocoCay, then has a sea day, and returns to Fort Lauderdale on 11 Nov. It calls the Sunday trip a pre-inaugural sailing added ahead of 11 Nov. The release does not use the word added, and it does not print 8 Nov.

The naming ceremony is still 11 Nov 2026. Godmother is Gen. Lori J. Robinson. The release says executives, crew, loyalty guests, and government officials will be there. The September post, [Legend of the Seas names Gen. Robinson godmother](/news/legend-of-the-seas-robinson-godmother-fort-lauderdale/), already had the godmother and the 11 Nov date. This page does not move either one.

## What you get after the short trips

From Fort Lauderdale, the release lists 6-night Western Caribbean vacations and 8-night Southern Caribbean vacations. Every one of those visits Perfect Day at CocoCay. Seatrade prints the same two lengths.

On board, the release counts eight neighborhoods and more than 40 places to eat and drink. It names Hollywoodland Supper Club and Royal Railway, Legend Station. The theater line is Roald Dahl's Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, and America's Got Talent LIVE. Category 6 is called the largest waterpark at sea. Crown's Edge is a skywalk and a zip line, 154 feet above the ocean.

## What is not in the release

The release prints no arrival clock. Seatrade does not print one either. Do not treat a tracker time as the line's time.

The release prints no guest count and no gross tonnage. The Points Guy prints up to 7,600 passengers, about 2,350 crew, and 248,633 gross tons. Those figures stay in that article. They are not repeated here, because the release does not print them.

The release prints no fare.

## If you are booked

Read the length on your reservation. The release's opening trip is the 3-night CocoCay getaway. Seatrade and The Points Guy put that trip on Sunday 8 Nov, with a return on 11 Nov in The Points Guy's account. The naming day is 11 Nov. After that, the published pattern is 6 nights or 8 nights from Fort Lauderdale, and each of those calls CocoCay. Match the date before you lock a flight. The 7 Nov release does not print a price.
