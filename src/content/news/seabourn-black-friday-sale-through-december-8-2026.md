[The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/seabourn-announces-annual-black-friday-sale-offering-valuable-benefits-on-nearly-all-voyages-through-2029-302942486.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Seattle, 17 Nov 2026, 11:22 a.m. ET. It says new bookings run from 17 Nov through 8 Dec 2026.

## What the release prices

The release lists three benefits. One is a free two-category Veranda Suite upgrade. One is shipboard credit of up to $2,000 per suite: $500 for an Oceanview or Veranda suite, and $2,000 for a Penthouse suite and above. One is a reduced 15 percent deposit in the U.S., Canada, and Australia. The release says that deposit is a lower upfront cost. It does not print the usual deposit rate, so this post does not convert the phrase into a share of the fare.

The release says the sale covers nearly 780 itineraries on any of five ships, for departures in 2026 and beyond. Full World and Grand Voyages are out. The offer is select sailings and new bookings only. No sample fare is printed. The phone number in the release is 1-800-929-9391.

Five sample cruises are named, all in 2027: Quest on 7 Aug, a 7-day Sweden, Denmark, and Kiel Canal cruise; Venture on 17 Jul, 15 days in Greenland; Ovation on 14 Aug, 14 days in the Greek Isles; Pursuit on 13 Mar, 11 days to Antarctica; Encore on 18 Jun, 7 days in Alaska. The release says the credit can go toward shore excursions, spa treatments, the Stream Wi-Fi package, and The Retreat on Encore and Ovation. It does not print a menu of prices for those.

## What the U.S. terms add

[Seabourn's U.S. terms](https://www.seabourn.com/en/terms), last updated 17 Nov 2026, and the [U.S. offer page](https://www.seabourn.com/en/us/special-offers/black-friday-sale), read 18 Nov, both say book by 8 Dec. Neither prints a 17 Nov start. The terms use promo code NW*.

The terms split the credit. An Oceanview or Veranda suite is $500 per suite, written as $250 per guest. A Penthouse suite and above is $2,000 per suite, written as $1,000 per guest. The credit is for the first two guests. A single guest gets the suite amount. A third or fourth guest does not. The credit is onboard only. It is not cash, and it cannot be used in the casino or the medical center. It expires at the end of the cruise. The release does not print that split or those exclusions.

The upgrade in the terms is V1 to V3 and V2 to V4, if the higher category is open, and the price you pay is the fare for the suite you book. The release says two categories and does not print V1 or V4.

The terms call the deposit a 15 percent deposit offer on select sailings that are outside the standard payment window. They still do not print the usual deposit percentage. Final payment on the terms is 320 days before departure, except cruises that sail from 20 Mar 2027 through 28 Apr 2027, which are due in full 10 days after booking or 320 days out, whichever is later. The release does not print that calendar.

An Australia offer page, read the same day, dates the window from 18 Nov and says guests pay a 15 percent deposit. That page is not the U.S. release. Those two lines stay on the Australia page.

If you are booking in the U.S., the close both pages share is 8 Dec 2026, the credit figures match, and the fare is whatever the suite shows when you book.
