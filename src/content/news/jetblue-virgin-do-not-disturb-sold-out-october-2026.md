The Do Not Disturb package is marked sold out. The [JetBlue Vacations page](https://www.jetbluevacations.com/trip-ideas/do-not-disturb), read 23 Oct 2026, heads the offer NOW SOLD OUT. It is the adults-only JetBlue flight bundled with a Virgin Voyages cruise.

The terms lower on the same page still print a booking window through 12:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday 27 Oct. The page does not say how many of the 60 cabins sold, or when the heading changed. If you do not already have a confirmation, treat the heading as the status.

The photo on this page is the desktop image on that JetBlue Vacations page. The page does not name a photographer.

## What the release priced, and what the page still lists

[Virgin Voyages' release](https://www.virginvoyages.com/press/latest-releases/jetblue-vacations-virgin-voyages-adults-only-fly-cruise) is dated 20 Oct 2026 in Miami. It says the package goes on sale at noon EDT on Wednesday 21 Oct, by phone at 1-844-593-7581. There are 60 cabins, first come, first served. The price is $2,750 for two Sailors, including taxes, fees, and gratuities. The JetBlue page, read 23 Oct, does not reprint that fare.

The cruise is Brilliant Lady, 4 nights, from Miami, 4 to 8 March 2027. The ports named are Key West and the Beach Club at Bimini. Flights are JFK to Fort Lauderdale on 4 March 2027 and back on 8 March 2027. The release marks the flight as U.S. Department of Transportation public charter PC-26-091. The age on both pages is 18 and older.

| Cabins | 60, in the release and on the page |

| Status on 23 Oct | The page heading says sold out |

Nirmal Saverimuttu, Virgin Voyages' CEO, is quoted in the release on a kid-free trip from takeoff to sail away. Jamie Perry, president of Paisly, which the release says powers JetBlue Vacations, is quoted on the trip starting before you board. Those quotes are the announcement. They do not say the cabins are gone. The sold-out line is the JetBlue page three days later.

## If you already booked

The JetBlue FAQ says the package is for two guests, non-refundable, and non-transferable. Payment is due in full when you buy. A name change is allowed for the second guest. If either guest cancels, the promotional package will no longer apply.

The terms repeat the limits. Double occupancy only. Sea Terrace only. Perks, vouchers, and priority access have no cash value. The flight and the cruise are 18 and older.

You get one carry-on and one checked bag. At Fort Lauderdale you collect the checked bag and go to the Virgin Voyages desk in the carousel area. The FAQ says the bag then goes to the cabin. The transfer drops you at Terminal V, 718 N Cruise Blvd, Miami, FL 33132.

The passport line in the FAQ is Virgin Voyages' rule on that page. The passport must be valid at least 6 months after the voyage ends.

BlueHouse at JFK is the first 60 customers, first come, first served. The FAQ says the lounge opens at 5:00 a.m. One complimentary drink is included on the flight. The page lists a $100 Bar Tab per cabin, a welcome gift, priority boarding, and a welcome party with a Do Not Disturb cocktail. The release also names priority boarding through the RockStar entrance.

Classic Wi-Fi on the ship is in the FAQ, along with Fly-Fi on the JetBlue flight. The FAQ says Wi-Fi upgrades are bought through Virgin Voyages.

A later section on the JetBlue page offers up to $150 in Bar Tab on Sea Terrace cabins and above. That section says the offer cannot be used on chartered voyages. The release lists this flight as a public charter. This post does not add the $150 to a Do Not Disturb booking. The package's own drink credit, in the list at the top of the page, is the $100 Bar Tab per cabin.

## If you did not get a cabin

The heading says the package is sold out, while the terms still show a phone window through Tuesday. The page does not explain that gap. Do not expect the phone line to open a 61st cabin off the heading alone. Call only if you already have a booking question.

The FAQ answers a different question. If you do not need the flights, you can still book the cruise-only option directly with JetBlue Vacations. The welcome gift, priority boarding, the welcome party, and the $100 Bar Tab are only in this package. That sentence does not say every cabin on Brilliant Lady is gone. It says the package perks are.

This March 2027 week is not the October California change already on this site. [Brilliant Lady's 17 Oct Los Angeles cruise swapped Mexico for California](/news/brilliant-lady-hurricane-trudy-california-october-17-2026/) is a different sailing.

## What to do with this

If you have a confirmation, match it to a Sea Terrace for two, paid in full, and read the cancel line. If one of you drops out, the page says the promotional package will no longer apply. Check the passport date against 8 March 2027 plus six months.

If you were waiting to call before 27 Oct, the page you would book from says the package is sold out. The $2,750 figure is the 20 Oct release. It is not a fare reprinted on the sold-out page.
