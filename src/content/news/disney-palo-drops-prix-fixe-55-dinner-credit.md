The Palo set-price dinner is on the way out. If you are Platinum or Pearl, the credit becomes $55 toward the a la carte dinner.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/disney-cruise-line-ends-palo-prix-fixe-menu-fleetwide-starting-october-22/221025), Melissa Mayntz, 16 Oct 2026, and the [Disney Cruise Line Blog](https://disneycruiselineblog.com/2026/10/palo-dropping-prix-fixe-dinner-menu-enforcing-platinum-pearl-castaway-club-benefit-to-dinner-only/), Scott Sanders, 15 Oct, both print the email. The blog dates that email 15 Oct 2026. This check could not open Disney's Palo FAQ page on 19 Oct. The URL redirected in a loop. The figures below come from the email those two outlets print, and from the benefit wording the blog prints.

## What the email says

The email is addressed to Platinum and Pearl Castaway Club members. It covers Palo, Palo Steakhouse, and Palo Trattoria.

The blog prints this:

> Beginning on October 22, 2026, these restaurants will transition away from the prix-fixe dinner menu to a selection of our Guest's favorite items.

> Members and their Guests staying in the same stateroom can continue to use their $55.00 USD per person credit toward a broad range of à la carte dinner menu offerings. This exclusive benefit is limited to dinner only. As a reminder, brunch is not included in this offer. Guests are responsible for beverage charges, gratuities, and any amount exceeding the credit.

Cruise Hive quotes the same start date, the same $55 credit, and the same dinner-only line.

You pay three things on top of the credit. Drinks. The gratuity. Any food above $55.

The member has to be at the table. Cruise Hive says that is the existing Castaway Club rule. The blog's 15 Oct benefit text says the same, and it says the credit is once per sailing. Unused credit is forfeited. It has no cash value. It cannot be transferred.

The blog prints the benefit line from 14 Oct as a complimentary dinner, one experience per stateroom per sailing. The 15 Oct line is the $55 credit. That before-and-after text is the blog's. It is not in the Cruise Hive article.

## Which ship, which day

The email sentence names 22 Oct 2026. It does not name a ship. Cruise Hive uses that date for the fleet and does not print a later date.

The Disney Cruise Line Blog, [DCL Fan](https://dclfan.com/news/palo-to-get-rid-of-prix-fixe-dinner-menu-and-castaway-club-brunch-benefit/), Hannah Anderson, updated 15 Oct, and [WDW Magazine](https://www.wdw-magazine.com/disney-cruise-line-palo-changes/) print the same ship list. That list is not inside the email text.

| Ship | Date on those three pages |
|---|---|
| Disney Wonder | Thu 22 Oct 2026 |
| Disney Dream, Disney Fantasy, Disney Wish | Fri 23 Oct 2026 |
| Disney Magic, Disney Treasure, Disney Destiny | Sat 24 Oct 2026 |
| Disney Adventure | Mon 26 Oct 2026 |

If you are on Wonder, every report puts you on 22 Oct. If you are on a later ship, the email sentence you can quote still says 22 Oct. The ship table is the three outlets' list. Check the reservation if your sailing falls in that week.

## Brunch

The email says the credit is dinner only. Brunch is outside the offer.

Cruise Hive says using the benefit at brunch had been a manager's choice. The written benefit was dinner. DCL Fan says the brunch menu stays prix-fixe at $55 per person and that you pay it yourself. WDW Magazine says Disney has not announced a change to the brunch experience or to Prima Notte. The email text does not print a brunch price. Use $55 for brunch only as DCL Fan's figure.

The blog says the email points you to My Reservations, and that pre-arranged activities can be changed online up to three days before the sailing. A brunch you already booked is still a booking. The credit rule in the email does not say that booking keeps the old benefit.

## Menu prices

Cruise Hive says it sees no sign that a la carte prices are changing. It prints sides at $6 and a 28-ounce porterhouse at $80. Those prices are in that article. They are not in the email. The email says the restaurants move to a selection of guest favorite items.

If you are Platinum or Pearl and you sail on or after the date for your ship, plan the Palo credit as $55 toward dinner. Price the plate before you sit down. If the table is over $55, the extra is yours, plus drinks and the gratuity.
