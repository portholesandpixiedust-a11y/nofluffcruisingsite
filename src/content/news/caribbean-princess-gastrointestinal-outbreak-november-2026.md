The CDC has a gastrointestinal outbreak page up for Caribbean Princess. [The Vessel Sanitation Program page](https://www.cdc.gov/vessel-sanitation/cruise-ship-outbreaks/caribbean-princess-november-2026.html), published and updated 17 Nov 2026, lists voyage B638 from 11 Nov through 23 Nov 2026. It counts 134 of 2,936 passengers and 14 of 1,132 crew. The causative agent is unknown.

The CDC page has no photograph of the ship. The photo here is a separate file on Wikimedia Commons, taken by Gary Bembridge on 3 May 2017 and licensed CC BY 2.0. It shows the ship at Cobh. The bow reads CARIBBEAN PRINCESS. It is not a picture of voyage B638.

## The count on this voyage

The passenger share on the page is 4.6 percent. The crew share is 1.2 percent. Predominant symptoms are vomiting and diarrhea. The outbreak was reported to the program on 15 Nov 2026.

The page says the cases are totals for the whole voyage. It says that does not mean all of those people are sick at the same time, including when the ship arrives or leaves a port.

The [2026 outbreak index](https://www.cdc.gov/vessel-sanitation/cruise-ship-outbreaks/index.html), also updated 17 Nov, lists the same Caribbean Princess dates and marks the causative agent Unknown. It says the program posts an outbreak when a voyage includes both U.S. and foreign ports and 3 percent or more of passengers or crew report symptoms to the ship's medical staff. The index does not print the 134 or the 14. The outbreak page does not print a homeport or a port list.

## What the ship reported to CDC

The outbreak page says Princess Cruises and the crew reported four actions: more cleaning and disinfection under their outbreak plan, stool specimens for testing, isolation of ill passengers and crew, and a consult with the program on cleaning and on reporting ill cases. The program says it is monitoring remotely, including a review of the response and the sanitation procedures.

Samples are pending confirmatory testing. The page says norovirus is often a cause of this kind of outbreak and that the cause is not always known when an investigation starts. It does not name norovirus for voyage B638.

No Princess newsroom post on this voyage was found on 18 Nov. The CDC pages do not print a cancelled port, a boarding change, a refund, or a fare credit.

## Other 2026 Princess rows

The same index lists three other Princess voyages in 2026 and names norovirus for each one. They are Caribbean Princess from 28 Apr to 11 May, Ruby Princess from 12 Jun to 2 Jul, and Star Princess from 7 Mar to 14 Mar. Those rows do not print case counts. They are not this sailing.

The CDC page prints its own case definition. It is three or more loose stools in 24 hours, or more than normal for that person, or vomiting plus one of diarrhea, muscle ache, headache, abdominal cramp, or fever. That definition is the agency's. If you are on voyage B638, the page says telling the medical center is how these cases get counted. If you are booked on the sailing that follows 23 Nov, these two CDC pages do not say that turnaround is cancelled.
