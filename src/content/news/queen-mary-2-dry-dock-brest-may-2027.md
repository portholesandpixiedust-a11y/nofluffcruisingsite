Queen Mary 2 goes into a French dry dock next spring. The cruise underway this week is the 450th crossing, not the refurbished ship. [Cunard's release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cunard-reveals-new-interiors-of-queen-mary-2-with-cunard-deco-302936461.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Santa Clarita, 11 Nov 2026, 8:07 a.m. ET.

The picture is the release's illustration of a proposed duplex Q1 Queens Grill Suite. The credit is illustrator Tanu Vasu, for Cunard. It is a drawing. The release says some elements were interpreted for the art and may differ from the finished rooms. It is not a photo of the ship, and it is not a room you can book on the crossing that left yesterday.

## The dates that change a 2027 booking

The release says the ship enters dry dock at Damen Shiprepair Brest on 31 Mar 2027 and returns to service on 9 May 2027.

It does not name the sailings in that gap. It does not print a refund, a credit, or a move to another ship. If you hold a Queen Mary 2 cruise in that window, the release is not your cancellation notice. Read the message on the booking.

The release calls the work a refurbishment that adds new Grill Suites, an elevated wellness offer, and refreshed guest spaces. It does not print a price for any of that. It says the redesign covers every accommodation grade, from Britannia staterooms through the Grand Duplex Queens Grill Suites, plus the Princess Grill, the Queens Grill, and the Grill Suites Lounge. The studio named is AD Associates in London. The design language is Cunard Deco. Paul Ludlow, the line's president, is the executive in the quote. The quote does not add a date or a fare.

One artwork note in the release stops a drawn clock at 4:33 p.m., which the release ties to the original Queen Mary's first Southampton departure in May 1936. That is a detail in the drawing. It is not a departure time for this ship.

## The crossing that is already at sea

The release says Queen Mary 2 left Southampton on 10 Nov for the 450th transatlantic crossing, and that this voyage has a program called the 450 Club. It does not list the events.

[The voyage page for cruise M630](https://www.cunard.com/en-us/find-a-cruise/M630/M630), read 11 Nov 2026, is the line's page for that cruise. It runs 10 Nov to 17 Nov, Southampton to New York, and it calls the program The 450 Club: Celebrate the Transatlantic, created by Immersive Octopus. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/hotel-operations-entertainment/cunard-marks-queen-mary-2-s-450th-atlantic-crossing), Holly Payne, on 10 Nov, uses the same program name and the same dates.

The events the voyage page names:

- The Captain's Account, in the Illuminations theatre, as the ship passes close to the Titanic wreck, told through Sir Arthur Rostron. The page limits that telling to people seated in that theatre.
- Ocean Times, a newspaper revived for this crossing only.
- Secrets from the Sea, a tour led by Cunard bellhops.
- War Brides, in the Queens Room.
- A Noel Coward Revue, in the Laurent-Perrier Champagne Bar.
- Cunard Insights, talks by a maritime historian. The page does not print a name.
- A 450th Transatlantic Crossing Gala Dinner, with a keepsake menu.
- The Commodore's Farewell, a murder-mystery dinner in the Verandah. The page says an extra charge applies. It does not print the price. It also says the dinner is booked in My Cunard about six months before sailing. That is the standing line on the page. This cruise has already left, so do not read it as a new booking window.

## What Seatrade adds, and the page does not

Seatrade says Chris Frame and Dr Stephen Payne, described as the lead designer of Queen Mary 2, are giving the Cunard Insights talks. The voyage page says a maritime historian and does not print those names. The release does not print them either. This post does not treat the two names as confirmed by the line.

Seatrade also says a 450 Club gala night in the Queens Room is followed by a midnight party in G32. The voyage page prints the gala dinner and the keepsake menu. It does not print G32. The release does not print G32. If you are on the ship, use the daily program for the late night, not the Seatrade sentence.

Seatrade does not print the Verandah or the extra charge. Those stay on the voyage page. Seatrade does not print the 31 Mar or 9 May yard dates. Those stay on the release.

## If you are booked

On M630, the program is the 450 Club, and the one event the line's page marks as extra is The Commodore's Farewell. The new suites in the drawing are for after the yard.

If your cruise falls between 31 Mar 2027 and the return to service on 9 May 2027, the release does not say you are cancelled and it does not say you are paid. Wait for the line's note on that reservation before you change a flight.
