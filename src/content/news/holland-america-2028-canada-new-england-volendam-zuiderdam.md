Holland America opened bookings for its 2028 Canada and New England season on 1 Oct 2026. The CNW release from Seattle is timed 10:49 ET. Cruise Industry News reported the opening the same day. The season runs May through October 2028 on Volendam and Zuiderdam. Holland America says this season has more calls in Montréal than any other cruise line. That comparison is the line's claim.

Cruises run 7 to 14 days. Longer Collectors' Voyages are also on sale. Departure cities in the release are Montréal, Québec City, Boston, New York, and Fort Lauderdale. Select itineraries include late-night calls in Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, and Saint John, New Brunswick, plus overnight stays in Québec City. The release does not name which dates get the late night or the overnight.

## Zuiderdam after Evolution

The release calls Zuiderdam the first post-Evolution ship to sail Canada and New England. Holland America describes Evolution as its largest fleet investment in 153 years. Additions named in the release are Solo Verandah staterooms, Bridgeview Suites with forward views, and the Grand Dutch Café. The work also refreshes staterooms and suites, updates guest spaces, and adds enhancements inspired by the Pinnacle Class ships.

Cruise Industry News repeats the regional debut. It also says Zuiderdam is the second of six ships in the Evolution program. The release text used here does not say second of six.

## Dates in the release

Use these sailings as the line printed them. Cruise Industry News repeats the same list.

Seven-day cruises run through the season on Volendam and Zuiderdam. The patterns are Boston to Montréal, Boston to Québec City, or roundtrip Boston. Ports may include Prince Edward Island, Sydney, Halifax, Portland, Québec City, Montréal, and Saint John.

A nine-day Lighthouse & Harbors cruise sails on Volendam from Montréal to New York on 30 Sep 2028. Calls are Québec City, Saguenay, Prince Edward Island, Sydney, Halifax, and Portland.

A 10-day Québec & Newfoundland cruise sails on Volendam from Montréal to Boston on 7 Jun 2028. Calls are Québec City, Baie-Comeau, Havre-Saint-Pierre, Corner Brook, St. Anthony, St. John's, Halifax, and Portland.

A 10-day New France & Newfoundland cruise sails on Volendam from Boston to Montréal on 19 Aug 2028. Calls are Portland, Halifax, St. John's, St. Anthony, Corner Brook, Québec City, and Saguenay.

An 11-day Maritimes & New France circle sails roundtrip from Montréal on Volendam on 27 May 2028 and 29 Aug 2028. Calls are Québec City, Saguenay, Cap-aux-Meules, Prince Edward Island, Saint-Pierre and Miquelon, and St. John's.

A 14-day Atlantic & Colonial New England cruise sails on Volendam from Fort Lauderdale to Montréal on 13 May 2028. Calls are Norfolk, Boston, Portland, Halifax, Sydney, Prince Edward Island, Québec City, and Saguenay.

A 14-day Atlantic Coast & Québec cruise sails on the post-Evolution Zuiderdam from Québec City to Fort Lauderdale on 14 Oct 2028. Calls are Sept-Îles, Saguenay, Halifax, Sydney, Prince Edward Island, Boston, and Norfolk.

## The early booking bonus

The release offers a Have It All Early Booking Bonus on select 2028 Canada and New England cruises, for a limited time. The named pieces are shore excursion credit, specialty dining, an Elite Beverage Package, premium Wi-Fi, prepaid Crew Appreciation, and the Best Price Guarantee. The release says availability is limited and terms apply. It does not print a dollar amount for the shore excursion credit.

## What you should do

Book Zuiderdam if you want the Evolution ship on its first Canada and New England season. The dated Zuiderdam sailing in the release is 14 Oct 2028, from Québec City to Fort Lauderdale. Seven-day cruises in the release sail on both ships. Book Volendam if you want the Newfoundland trips or the Montréal circle. Those dates are 7 Jun, 19 Aug, 27 May, and 29 Aug 2028. Open hollandamerica.com or call 1-877-724-5425. Facts above follow the CNW release at 10:49 ET on 1 Oct 2026 and Cruise Industry News the same day.
