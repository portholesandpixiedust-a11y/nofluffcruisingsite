Princess has a Black Friday block with a four-day booking window. [The offer terms](https://www.princess.com/cruise-deals-promotions/terms-and-conditions), read 24 Nov 2026, put three pieces on bookings made from 24 Nov through 27 Nov.

The image on this page is the lead file on [the deals page](https://www.princess.com/cruise-deals-promotions/black-friday-deals), named nov-24-27-hp-hero-desktop. The page does not name a photographer. The frame is an aerial of a white ship in green water between steep, forested slopes, with snow on the far peaks. A mark on the image reads Endicott Arm, Alaska. The bow name is not readable. This is not a photograph of one Black Friday sailing.

## What the terms date to 27 Nov

Three pieces carry the dates 11/24/26 through 11/27/26.

Up to 50 percent off is taken from the applicable Launch Fare. It does not apply to required cruise fees and expenses, or to government taxes and fees. It is for select categories and sailings. It does not apply to Princess Plus, Princess Premier, or any tours, even when a tour is inside an advertised fare. It applies to the first two guests. A third, fourth, or fifth guest pays the prevailing cruise rate.

Instant savings is a separate line. The terms call $800 the advertised amount per double-occupancy stateroom, which is $400 a guest, on a 13 to 17 day voyage in a mini-suite or full suite. The cut is for the first two guests. It comes off the cruise fare after any percentage off, and the terms say it is already inside the advertised fare, so it does not show as its own line at checkout.

Per guest, the terms print these bands. Interior and oceanview: $50 on 2 to 5 days, $50 on 6 to 8 days, $100 on 9 to 12 days, $100 on 13 to 17 days, and $150 on 18 days or longer. Balcony: $50, $100, $150, $200, and $300 on those bands. Mini-suite and full suite: $50, $150, $200, $400, and $500.

Three world cruises are higher. The 115-day roundtrip from Fort Lauderdale, the 115-day roundtrip from Los Angeles, and the 100-day Los Angeles to Fort Lauderdale pay $500 a guest in an interior or oceanview, $1,500 a guest in a balcony, and $2,000 a guest in a mini-suite or full suite.

The deals page prints a per-stateroom table that matches those per-guest figures doubled: inside and oceanview up to $300, balcony up to $600, and mini-suites and full suites up to $1,000, on 18 days or longer. The page headline still says up to $800 instant savings per stateroom. That $800 figure is the terms' advertised example for a 13 to 17 day mini-suite or full suite, not the top cell in the table, and not the world-cruise lines.

Single occupancy is where the two Princess pages disagree. The terms say a single guest receives the single per-guest amount, and they print that range as $50 to $2,000. The deals page footnote prints $50 to $500. Use the terms for the world-cruise amounts. The deals page does not print those three world-cruise lines.

The third dated piece is 50 percent off deposits. It applies to a full standard deposit. It does not cut a deposit that is already reduced. It is not available when final payment is already required, on world cruises, on cruises of 45 days or longer, on full suites, on Sanctuary Collection, or on cruises that must be paid in full at booking.

## Free guests, and the older Blue Friday release

[The deals page](https://www.princess.com/cruise-deals-promotions/black-friday-deals) lists free third and fourth guests, on select voyages, with taxes and fees extra, as part of the sale. The free-guest block on the terms page does not print 24 Nov or 27 Nov. It says the offer is the cruise fare only. Government taxes and fees, required cruise fees and expenses, the non-refundable deposit, and other travel costs stay with the first or second guest. The terms put the approximate value at $99 to $3,605 a person, and they say there is no guarantee. The fare summary shows either $0 or a cruise fare.

[The 6 Nov release](https://www.princess.com/news/news-releases/2026/11/blue-is-back-princess-cruises-unveils-blue-friday-savings) is a different page. It still prints Blue Friday from 6 Nov through 7 Dec, with up to 50 percent off cruise fares, up to $400 in instant savings, and free third and fourth guests on select voyages. It also prints a past-guest private sale from 13 Nov through 23 Nov, with up to $800 off. That private window has closed. The release does not print the 24 Nov through 27 Nov dates. This post does not treat the 7 Dec line and the 27 Nov block as one offer.

The same terms page has a 2028 early-booking block for bookings from 11 Aug 2026 through 16 Feb 2027. That block is not the Black Friday sale. The general lines on the terms page limit offers to legal residents of North America, age 21 and older, on select cruises. A non-refundable deposit is required. The phone in the 6 Nov release is 1-800-774-6237.

## If you are booking this week

The three dated pieces on the terms page close with bookings made on 27 Nov. Read the cabin and the length before you use the $800 headline. A 13 to 17 day mini-suite is the advertised $400 a guest. An 18-day suite on the deals-page table is $1,000 a stateroom. The world-cruise amounts are the three named voyages in the terms. The deals page prints sample starting prices, including a Caribbean figure. This post does not use them, because the terms say the fare is the lead-in category at the time you book.
