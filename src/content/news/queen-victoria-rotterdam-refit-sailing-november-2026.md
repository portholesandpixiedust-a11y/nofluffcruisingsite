Queen Victoria is out of the Rotterdam dry dock, and the new cabins were already on sale for voyages from 11 Nov. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/refurb-design-interiors/cunard-reveals-revamped-queen-victoria-after-refit), Holly Payne, and [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/refreshed-queen-victoria-emerges-from-rotterdam-drydock/) both published that on 12 Nov 2026.

The photo is the lead image on the Seatrade story. The file name is THEATRE_QUEEN_VIC.jpg. It shows a ballroom: a chandelier, a balcony, a blue stage curtain, and a floral inlay lit on the dance floor. The caption credits Cunard and talks about public rooms. It does not name the room. It is not a photo of the hull.

This is a different ship from [Queen Mary 2's 2027 dry dock](/news/queen-mary-2-dry-dock-brest-may-2027/). That post is the liner that left Southampton on 10 Nov.

## What both reports put on the ship

Seatrade and Cruise Industry News agree on the rooms:

- Eight new Britannia staterooms.
- Three of those eight are a new category, Britannia Deluxe Oceanview, with floor-to-ceiling windows.
- A refreshed Grand Lobby. Seatrade calls it triple-height. Cruise Industry News does not print that word.
- A reimagined Queens Room. Both tie the look to floral borders of the Royal Palaces and to Queen Victoria's orange blossom jewelry. Seatrade also names butterflies, afternoon tea, live music, dancing, and Gala Evenings.
- Updated Princess Grill and Queens Grill suites and their restaurants, plus a redesigned Grill Suites Lounge and lift lobby. Seatrade says the lounge and lobby draw on Queen Victoria's favourite jewels.
- The Pavilion Wellness Café, the third Cunard ship to get it, after Queen Anne and Queen Elizabeth. Both say it serves breakfast, lunch, and daytime dining. Seatrade adds the menu line: plant-based cuisine alongside sustainably sourced meat, fish, and dairy.

Both call the yard Damen Shiprepair in Rotterdam and the stay three weeks. Both use a 2,000 figure. Seatrade says passengers. Cruise Industry News says guests. Both quote Paul Ludlow as president of Cunard. Neither prints a fare.

## The cruise Cruise Industry News adds

Cruise Industry News says a company statement welcomed more than 50 travel agents onboard in Southampton on 11 Nov. It says the ship then left on a 16-night Portugal, Spain, and Morocco voyage. This desk did not open that statement. Seatrade does not print the visit or the voyage. Neither story lists the ports on that 16-night cruise.

The same Cruise Industry News story says winter cruises stay roundtrip from Southampton, to Northern Europe, the Canary Islands, the Iberian Peninsula, and Morocco. It also says a 109-night world cruise leaves on 10 Jan 2027 and calls that the longest Cunard itinerary of the 2026-27 winter. Those lines are this article's. Seatrade does not print them. The company statement behind them was not opened here.

## What Cunard printed in July

[Cunard's 20 July 2026 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cunard-reveals-plans-for-extensive-queen-victoria-transformation-302828769.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Valencia, 8:00 a.m. ET. It set the dock for 17 Oct through 5 Nov 2026 and a return to service on 7 Nov 2026. It put the eight new staterooms on sale that day for voyages sailing from 11 Nov 2026. The café menu in that release matches the plant-based line Seatrade prints now.

The July release quotes Katie McAlister as president. The 12 Nov reports quote Paul Ludlow as president. The quotes are not combined. The phone number in the July release is 1-800-728-6273. The July lead photo is a fjord picture of the ship from the planning release. It is not the ballroom on this page.

## If you are booked

If your Queen Victoria voyage sails from 11 Nov 2026 onward, the July release is the one that put the new Britannia cabins on sale for those dates. The 12 Nov reports say the rooms and the café are on the ship. If you need the 16-night port list, it is not in either 12 Nov story. Read the booking. No fare is printed in the July release or in the two 12 Nov reports.
