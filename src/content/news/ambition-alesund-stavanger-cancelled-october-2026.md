Ambition has lost two more Norway calls. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/storms-force-ambassadors-ambition-to-scrap-two-more-norway-ports/221930), Melissa Mayntz, 27 Oct 2026, and [Travel Gossip](https://travelgossip.co.uk/latestnews/ambassadors-ambition-hit-by-ongoing-weather-disruption/), Ruth Lawes, the same day, both say Alesund on Wednesday 28 Oct is cancelled, and Stavanger on Friday 30 Oct is cancelled. Bristol on Monday 2 Nov stays.

This is the cruise that left Bristol on Friday 16 Oct, the 17-night Land of the Northern Lights. The earlier swap is already a separate post: [Ambition swaps five Norway ports for a southern route](/news/ambition-norway-port-swap-storms-austen-boelo-october-2026/). That note put Alesund on Wednesday and Stavanger on Friday. Those two dates are the ones that just came off.

The photo on this page is the lead photo on the Cruise Hive story, credited to Kevin Shipp. A later photo in that article is credited to Peter Titmuss. This page does not use that one. The caption does not date either frame to this cruise.

## The new end of the cruise

Both articles say the same shape. Molde runs overnight. Bergen is the next port. Friday is no longer Stavanger. The ship leaves Bergen that day for Bristol.

Travel Gossip prints Ålesund. Cruise Hive prints Alesund. This post uses Alesund and notes the other spelling.

Cruise Hive says the Thursday arrival is later than the revised schedule, so the ship stays in Bergen overnight and leaves Friday. Travel Gossip says the ship arrives Thursday evening and sails from Bergen to Bristol on Friday. The overnight is the Cruise Hive label. The Friday departure is in both.

## What the line posted

Cruise Hive says the Facebook post went up late Monday 26 Oct. It quotes the wind line and the captain's decision. Southerly gusts up to 28 knots are forecast for Wednesday morning. In those conditions, berthing and departing safely would present significant challenges, so the captain cancelled Alesund. A later quote says the Stavanger call is cancelled too. The article converts 28 knots to 32 miles per hour. Travel Gossip does not print that conversion or the berthing sentence.

Travel Gossip quotes a different Facebook passage. The line apologises for the further changes. Safety, wellbeing, and comfort of guests and crew stay the highest priority. The line says it is still adapting plans to avoid the worst of the weather.

Neither article links the Facebook post. Treat the two quotes as two passages, not one merged letter.

Travel Gossip says the five earlier port changes were for storms Austen and Boelo, and it calls this latest round stormy weather. Cruise Hive ties the new note to the latest forecasts and points back to those same named storms for the earlier change. The 28-knot figure is the reason both articles give for Alesund. Neither new quote prints a 70-knot figure. That number sits in the earlier post.

## What this means if you are on Ambition

Do not plan on Alesund on Wednesday. Do not plan on Stavanger on Friday. Tuesday night is still Molde, in both reports. Thursday evening is Bergen. Friday is the trip toward Bristol. You are still due back on Monday 2 Nov.

Neither article prints a fare refund or an onboard credit. If you bought an Alesund or Stavanger tour through Ambassador, ask the ship. These reports do not say the tour is refunded.

If you are meeting the ship in Bristol, the date in both articles is still Monday 2 Nov. Neither prints the hour. Use Ambassador's message for the clock.
