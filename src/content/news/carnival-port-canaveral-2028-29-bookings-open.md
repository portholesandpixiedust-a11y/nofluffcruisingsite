## What the release prints

Five ships sail year-round from Port Canaveral: Carnival Breeze, Carnival Firenze, Carnival Festivale, Carnival Glory, and Mardi Gras. The release says more than 300 departures. Cruises run from three-day Bahamas getaways to 14-day Carnival Journeys.

The Paradise Collection line is more than 200 visits to Celebration Key, plus RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay; Amber Cove; Grand Turk; Puerta Maya; and Isla Tropicale. Seatrade prints the same stops and adds three glosses: Amber Cove as the Dominican Republic, Puerta Maya as Cozumel, and Isla Tropicale as Roatán. Those place names in parentheses are Seatrade's. The release does not spell them out.

To book, the release says call 1-800-CARNIVAL, open carnival.com, or use a travel advisor.

## The ships, as the release assigns them

Glory and Mardi Gras take the short trips, three to five days, to the Bahamas and the Eastern Caribbean. Every Glory sailing includes Celebration Key. Seatrade prints that Glory line too.

Breeze and Firenze take the week. The release calls them rotating Eastern and Western Caribbean itineraries. The ports it names are Amber Cove, San Juan, St. Thomas, Belize, Cozumel, and Isla Tropicale.

Festivale takes six-day and eight-day cruises to the Eastern and Southern Caribbean. A six-day Eastern cruise includes Celebration Key and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay. An eight-day Eastern cruise includes San Juan, St. Thomas, and St. Maarten. An eight-day Southern cruise includes Aruba and Curaçao.

Breeze also has four 14-day Carnival Journeys between October 2028 and February 2029. The release says those sailings visit 13 Southern Caribbean destinations, including Barbados, Grenada, Antigua, Aruba, Curaçao, Dominica, Martinique, St. Lucia, St. Maarten, and St. Kitts. That including-list is 10 names. The release does not print the other three, and it does not print the four departure dates.

## Short cruises, in Cruise Hive's detail

The release stops at three to five days, the Bahamas, the Eastern Caribbean, and Celebration Key on every Glory sailing. Cruise Hive goes further. Treat this block as Cruise Hive's.

The 3-night sailings visit Celebration Key. The article describes a full day for beaches, lagoons, food trucks, and waterslides, and it places the island near Freeport.

Every 4-night cruise visits two ports. One is Celebration Key. The other is Nassau or RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, depending on the ship and the date.

Mardi Gras 5-night cruises split. Cruise Hive says about half visit Celebration Key, Nassau, and Half Moon Cay, plus a sea day. The other half visit Amber Cove and Grand Turk, with two sea days.

Breeze leaves on Sundays. Firenze leaves on Saturdays. Those weekdays are Cruise Hive's. For the 7-night routes, Cruise Hive adds Nassau, Celebration Key, and Grand Cayman to the release's port list, and it says the calls depend on the date and the route.

## The four long Breeze cruises

Cruise Hive says all four 14-night Journeys are Southern Caribbean roundtrips from Port Canaveral. The release does not print the word roundtrip. The dates in Cruise Hive are 15 Oct 2028, 26 Nov 2028, 7 Jan 2029, and 18 Feb 2029.

Cruise Hive names 13 ports and says the calls depend on the date: St. Thomas, St. Kitts, Grenada, Barbados, Antigua, St. Maarten, San Juan, Grand Turk, Curaçao, Aruba, Dominica, St. Lucia, and Martinique. Set beside the release, the three names that show up only in that Cruise Hive list are St. Thomas, San Juan, and Grand Turk. A date in the release's including-list can still be absent from one of the four cruises. Cruise Hive's own line is that the ports depend on the sailing.

## If you want one of these weeks

Open carnival.com, call 1-800-CARNIVAL, or use your advisor. That is the path the release prints. Pick the ship before you pick the length. Glory is the ship the release puts on Celebration Key every sailing. Breeze is the ship with the four 14-day Journeys.

Nothing on these pages prints a price. If you use the May 2028 start, the guest counts, or the four Journey dates, you are using Cruise Hive.
