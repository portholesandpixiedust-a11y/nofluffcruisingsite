AIDAcosma has swapped the water bottles in the cabin for dispensers in the corridor, and some fares now pay for that water. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/refurb-design-interiors/newly-refurbished-aidacosma-adds-water-dispensers-new-pizzeria), Mary Bond, reported it on 25 Nov 2026.

The photo is the file on that article, CRUISE-Water-dispensers-AIDAcosma.jpg. The credit line is PHOTO: AIDA Cruises. The article does not name a photographer. The caption says 53 water dispensers were installed in the cabin corridors. The frame itself is a hand pouring from a clear glass carafe into a tumbler on a light wood counter, with a second glass turned upside down beside it. The ship name is not in the frame, and the frame does not show a corridor machine.

## Who pays

Seatrade says 53 dispensers were fitted in the cabin corridors during a yard stay of about two and a half weeks at Chantier Naval in Marseille. The water is chilled table water, still or sparkling. Each cabin gets a reusable glass carafe to refill.

The dispensers replace bottles that used to sit in the cabin. Those bottles were included or sold extra, depending on the fare. Seatrade does not print the old bottle price.

Water from the dispensers is unlimited on an ALL IN fare, a PREMIUM fare, or a drink package. Suites and junior suites are included as well. A LIGHT fare or a CLASSIC fare, without a drink package, pays a surcharge. Seatrade does not print the amount. [AIDA's press index](https://aida.de/unternehmen/presse), read 25 Nov, leads with a 23 Nov note about Christmas and New Year sailings and does not show a dispenser release. This post does not fill in a price the article leaves out.

The rest of the fleet gets the same dispensers at later dry docks. Seatrade expects the work to finish by 2030. AIDAmar is next, from 20 Dec 2026. AIDAblu follows in spring 2027. Until a ship has the dispensers, bottled water stays in the stateroom.

## The pizza counter, and the Canary dates

Best Pizza@Sea replaces the pizza station inside the Fuego family restaurant on deck 16. Seatrade says the focus is signature pizzas. It does not print a price, and it does not say the pizza is included.

[AIDA's deck plan](https://aida.de/content/dam/aida/de/medien/kreuzfahrt/vessels/deck-floor-plans/single/AIDAcosma_Decksgrundrisse_2026_ab_26-11-2026.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf), marked valid from 26 Nov 2026, lists Best Pizza @ Sea on deck 16, with Fuego Restaurant on the same deck. The plan puts spaces around the @ sign. Seatrade does not. The plan does not label a water dispenser, and it does not print a surcharge. It is a check on the restaurant name, not on the water rule.

Seatrade also notes a new carpet and new seats in the Yacht Club Restaurant, and renewed upholstery, mattresses, flooring, and tiles. The technical lines cover propulsion, thrusters, stabilizers, a new underwater coating, and the point-of-sale system. None of those lines prints a guest fee.

After the yard, Seatrade says the ship sails a Mediterranean cruise and then an Andalusia and Canary Islands itinerary. The Canary Islands season starts 12 Dec 2026 and runs through April 2027, on seven-day cruises around the Canary Islands and to Madeira. The article does not print the date of the first cruise out of Marseille.

If your AIDAcosma fare is LIGHT or CLASSIC and you do not have a drink package, Seatrade's rule is that corridor water is extra, and the amount is not on the page. If your fare is ALL IN or PREMIUM, or you are in a suite or junior suite, the same article says the water is unlimited. AIDAmar's date in the article is 20 Dec 2026. Ships without the dispensers still have the bottles.
