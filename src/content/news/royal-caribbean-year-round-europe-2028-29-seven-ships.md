Royal Caribbean opened a 2028-29 Europe season on 14 Oct 2026, and one of the seven ships stays for the winter.

The [PR Newswire release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/royal-caribbean-unveils-more-ways-to-explore-europe-in-202829-with-new-year-round-season-302908665.html), Miami, 14 Oct 2026 at 10:00 ET, is the announcement. The [press-center page](https://royalcaribbeanpresscenter.com/news/royal-caribbean-unveils-more-ways-to-explore-europe-in-2028-29-with-new-year-round-season/) carries the same headline. On 15 Oct its article text did not render, so the ship list below follows the release. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/royal-caribbean-going-year-round-in-europe-in-2028-29/) and [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/royal-caribbean-joining-the-ranks-of-year-round-europe-operators), Anne Kalosh, both 14 Oct, track that release.

This is the Europe drop dated for the week of 12 Oct in the [19 Sep post on release weeks](/news/royal-caribbean-2028-2029-itinerary-release-dates/). That post said Part 1 is a portion of Europe. [Royal Caribbean Blog](https://www.royalcaribbeanblog.com/2026/10/14/royal-caribbean-europe-2028-cruises), Elizabeth Wright, updated 14 Oct, calls this release Europe Part 1.

## What is the year-round part?

Rhapsody is the ship the release keeps in Europe past the summer. From May to October 2028 it sails 7-night Turkey and Egypt cruises with overnights in Alexandria and Istanbul, and Greek Isles cruises. From November 2028 it sails 9- to 12-night cruises to the Canary Islands, Portugal, Morocco, Cyprus, Malta, and Greece. The release names Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Casablanca, Tangier, and more overnights in Alexandria.

The release says this season marks a return to Heraklion, on Crete, and to Cagliari, in Sardinia, for the first time in more than a decade. That sentence sits in the Rhapsody section. Liberty's itineraries also list Cagliari. If Heraklion is the port you want, open a Rhapsody sailing and read the port list. The release does not print a Heraklion date.

Those windows end by October. Jewel stops in August. Odyssey starts in April, earlier than the May ships. Explorer, Freedom, and Liberty run May to October. Mariner's paragraph names four homeports and three ports of call, Palma de Mallorca, Porto (Leixoes), and Dubrovnik, and it does not print months. Cruise Industry News does not add months. If you want Mariner, read the dates on the sailing you open.

Freedom's Southampton cruises include 3-night trips. The release names the Norwegian fjords, Copenhagen, Bruges (Zeebrugge), Bilbao, Paris (Le Havre), and Lisbon across the 3- to 9-night range. One sailing is not all of those ports.

Explorer calls Santorini later on every cruise, and Kotor on some of them. Liberty also has later evenings in Santorini, plus Marseille, Palermo, and Cagliari. Odyssey names Ephesus and Mykonos, with longer evenings in Santorini. Seatrade calls the Ephesus port Kusadasi. The release says Ephesus. Search both names, then use the port on your confirmation.

Several of the Greek itineraries offer an Ultimate Santorini Day. The release describes it as a full-day excursion to Oia and Fira with time at Royal Beach Club Santorini. Seatrade says you can book it. The release does not say the beach club is included in the fare. Book it only after you see the price on that sailing.

## What is not in this first release?

Royal Caribbean Blog says this Europe Part 1 list does not include an Oasis Class ship or Legend of the Seas. The blog notes that Oasis Class ships have sailed from Europe for more than a decade, that Legend sailed Europe in 2026, and that Legend is already on the books for Europe in 2027. It says a later Remaining Deployment could add ships for 2028 and 2029.

The press release does not say those ships are out. It names the seven in the table. Cruise Industry News and Seatrade do not add an Oasis Class ship or Legend to this announcement. Treat the absence as the blog's reading of Part 1. The September post on this site already said Part 1 is not the whole year.

The same blog says Liberty and Freedom are the first time two Freedom Class ships are in Europe together. That line is not in the release. This post does not use it as a booking fact.

## What this means for you

Crown & Anchor members could book when the 14 Oct release went out. Everyone else uses Thursday 15 Oct, the public opening in that release. Open the specific ship. A winter Canary Islands cruise in this drop is Rhapsody, from November 2028. A Baltic week is Jewel, and that window ends in August. If you are waiting on Legend or an Oasis Class ship, this release does not have one. Wait for the next deployment drop, and do not hold a 2028 Europe cabin on a ship the line has not named. Facts above follow the 14 Oct Royal Caribbean release, Seatrade and Cruise Industry News the same day, and Royal Caribbean Blog's 14 Oct read of what this first list leaves out.
