Carnival Australia put the 2028/29 program on sale on 28 Oct 2026. [The Australia page](https://www.carnival.com.au/behind-the-seas/news-events/2028-29-program/), datelined Sydney that day, says Carnival Splendor sails year-round from Sydney and Carnival Encounter sails year-round from Brisbane. [Carnival's U.S. newsroom](https://www.carnival-news.com/2026/10/28/carnival-cruise-line-reveals-2028-29-program-for-australia-on-sale-today) posts the same release. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/carnival-releases-two-ship-2028-29-australia-programme), Helen Hutcheon, 28 Oct, reports it.

The photo is the lead image on the Seatrade story. The caption says Carnival Splendor is on the left and Carnival Encounter is the other frame, and it credits Carnival Cruise Line. The bow on the left reads Carnival Splendor. The right-hand frame is a deck and a set of water slides. This post does not read a ship name in that frame.

The Australia page uses a different lead file, named EncounterAirlieBeach. That page does not print a caption or a photographer. This post does not use that file.

## What is on sale?

The Australia page says the voyages are on sale from today. The page date is 28 Oct 2026. It adds 112 voyages to the South Pacific, Far North Queensland, New Zealand, and Tasmania.

The U.S. newsroom post prints 112 as well. [Cruise Weekly](https://cruiseweekly.com.au/news/carnival-28-29-out/97406), 28 Oct, says more than 100. Use 112. That is the figure on both Carnival pages.

Booking paths on those pages are [carnival.com.au](https://www.carnival.com.au/), a local travel agent, or 13 31 94. None of the pages read for this post prints a fare.

## Which cruises are named?

Three highlights are on the Carnival pages and in Seatrade.

A solar eclipse cruise leaves Brisbane on Carnival Encounter in July 2028. The pages do not print the day inside July.

A new 13-day cruise leaves Brisbane for New Zealand on Carnival Encounter. The pages do not print the port list or the month.

A Melbourne Cup cruise leaves Sydney on Carnival Splendor in November 2028. The pages do not print the cup-week dates.

The Australia page also lists shorter trips. There are 23 three-day getaways from Sydney and Brisbane. There are 18 Tangalooma-Moreton Island voyages from Sydney. On those calls, the page says you can book a beachfront cabana at the Tangalooma Dunes Cabana Club. It says the cabanas are exclusive to Carnival guests when the ships are at the island. It does not print a cabana price.

Carnival Encounter has 12 cruises from Brisbane to Airlie Beach. The Mutiny on the Bounty cruise to Fiji returns. Carnival lists six voyages to New Zealand between mid-2028 and mid-2029. Seatrade prints those same counts. Cruise Weekly does not.

## What about the dry dock?

Carnival Splendor enters dry dock in August 2028. The Australia page and the U.S. newsroom post both say she returns for the rest of the 2028/29 program. Neither page names the yard.

[Cruise Passenger](https://cruisepassenger.com.au/news/carnival-announces-2028-29-aussie-sailings-their-first-season-with-just-two-ships/), Tallis Boerne Marcus, 28 Oct, says that dry dock means you can sail to Singapore and back. That line is Cruise Passenger's. It is not on the Carnival pages. Do not book a Singapore leg off this release until Carnival prints the port.

## What did Anton Loeb say?

The Australia page calls Anton Loeb Asst Vice President Sales and Marketing. Seatrade calls him assistant vice president of sales and marketing.

He says the program should be popular with first-time cruisers and with families and friends who want a fun, great-value holiday. He says to book early so you do not miss out. He says Carnival remains the only cruise line sailing in Australia all year. He says this release takes the line through the next three years, to mid-2029.

Those sentences are the quotes. They are not a fare, and they are not a promise that a cabin is still open.

## What do the other write-ups add?

Seatrade ties this program to Carnival Adventure and Carnival Luminosa leaving Australian waters in March 2028. That exit was already announced. The [15 Oct story](/news/carnival-luminosa-adventure-leave-australia-march-2028/) has the dates and the earlier line that this 2028/29 program was still to come. This page is that program.

Cruise Passenger calls this the first Australia season with just two ships, down from four. It says Carnival is not adding new ports, and that the new sailing is the 13-day Brisbane to New Zealand cruise. It also says the only homeports on the schedule are Sydney and Brisbane, so a Melbourne, Adelaide, or Auckland homeport is not in this release. The Carnival pages name Sydney for Splendor and Brisbane for Encounter. They do not print a sentence that shuts the other cities. If you need a homeport outside those two, read Cruise Passenger as a reading of the schedule, then check carnival.com.au for the ship you want.

## What this means for you

If you want Carnival in Australia after Adventure and Luminosa leave, the ships in this release are Splendor from Sydney and Encounter from Brisbane. The trips are on sale now, on the 28 Oct pages. The release prints no fare.

If you want the eclipse cruise, the 13-day New Zealand cruise, or Melbourne Cup week, the Carnival pages name the ship and the month. They do not name the day. Open the sailing on carnival.com.au before you treat a headline month as your departure date.

If you were waiting on a Singapore dry-dock cruise, that port is in Cruise Passenger. It is not in the Carnival text read here. More Carnival reporting is on the [Carnival news hub](/news/carnival/).
