MSC Cruises and Royal Caribbean Group are building a cruise terminal together in Naha, Okinawa. [Royal Caribbean Group's release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/naha-port-authority-cruise-port-naha-gk-msc-cruises-and-royal-caribbean-group-begin-construction-of-new-cruise-terminal-at-naha-port-okinawa-302898538.html), dated 5 Oct 2026, targets completion and the start of operations for March 2028.

Two competing cruise companies will run it together. Nothing in the release changes a 2026 or 2027 Naha call.

## Who owns it

The builder and operator is Cruise Port Naha G.K. The release calls it a joint venture between CTL Maritime and RCL Cruises Ltd. Those two companies represent MSC Cruises and Royal Caribbean Group. The deal behind it is a Cruise Hub Formation Agreement signed with the Naha Port Authority in August 2026.

The release says the terminal will help Naha handle "some of the most advanced cruise ships in the world." It does not name a ship. It prints no cost, no building size, and no passenger capacity.

## When it happened

The release does not give the ceremony date. [Ryukyu Shimpo](https://ryukyushimpo.jp/economics/entry-5533734.html), the Okinawa daily, dates it 2 Oct 2026. It places the new building at Naha Port's No. 2 cruise berth. Its summary says the ceremony took place aboard MSC Bellissima, which was alongside. The release describes a traditional Japanese ceremony at the future terminal site. Both can be true. Most of the Japanese article is paywalled, so we rely on its headline and summary.

[Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/msc-and-royal-caribbean-break-ground-on-new-naha-terminal) and [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/cruise-terminal-groundbreaking-at-naha-marks-cruise-growth-in-japan/) ran the same facts on 5 Oct.

## What the two lines said

Royal Caribbean Group CEO Jason Liberty: "We do not simply bring guests here, we also invest here."

Pierfrancesco Vago, executive chairman of MSC Group's cruise division, said the project will "create new opportunities for guests of MSC Cruises and Explora Journeys to enjoy Asia year-round."

Neither quote names a new sailing or a homeport.

## What this means for you

If you hold a Naha call before spring 2028, plan around the current port setup. If you are pricing a 2028 Japan cruise, such as [Voyager of the Seas in spring 2028](/news/royal-caribbean-voyager-japan-spring-2028/), do not book on the promise of the new building. March 2028 is a target. Check the pier in your final documents.

Facts above follow Royal Caribbean Group's 5 Oct 2026 release, Ryukyu Shimpo on 2 Oct 2026, and Seatrade and Cruise Industry News on 5 Oct 2026.
