Virgin cancelled the 4-night cruise that was due to leave PortMiami on Resilient Lady on Wednesday 28 Apr 2027. The ship still sails that week. It sails as a full-ship charter.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/virgin-voyages-pulls-resilient-lady-cruise-for-full-ship-charter/222446), Melissa Mayntz, 3 Nov 2026, says cancellation emails began on Monday 2 Nov and that the sailing is off Virgin's website. [VV Insider](https://vvinsider.com/virgin-voyages-cancels-resilient-lady-april-2027-cruise/), Rob Sammons, 2 Nov, says sailors and First Mates were being notified that day. [Cruise Fever](https://cruisefever.net/charter-bump-virgin-voyages-cancels-ships-last-miami-sailing-before-transatlantic/), J. Souza, 4 Nov, reports the same cancellation. None of the three names the charter client. None of the three links a Virgin Voyages newsroom post.

The photo is the lead image on the Cruise Hive story. The caption is Resilient Lady Docked in Miami. The credit is denbaim.

## What you can do with the booking

All three reports give you a move or a refund. They do not print the same credit, and they do not print the same refund words.

| Choice | Cruise Hive, 3 Nov | VV Insider, 2 Nov | Cruise Fever, 4 Nov |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valiant Lady | 6 May 2027. Same 4-night, Key West and Bimini, from Miami. | 6 May 2027. Fare and promotions protected. Ports of that sailing are not printed. | 6 May 2027. Original rate and promotions carry over. Ports are not printed. |
| Other cruise | Eligible 4- or 5-night in April or May 2027. | Eligible 4-night or 5-night in April or May 2027. | Qualifying 4-night or 5-night in April or May 2027. |
| Fare | Original fare and promotions protected on both rebookings. | Original voyage fare and promotional benefits protected. | Original fare and promotional rates protected. |
| Sailor Loot | $100 per standard stateroom. $300 per suite. Both rebooking options. | $100 per cabin, standard. $300 per cabin, Suites. Confirm the split on the notice. | $100 per cabin, standard. $300 per suite for RockStar and Mega RockStar Quarters. Check the notice. |
| Refund | Full refund, no fees or penalties. | Full refund of the canceled cruise. A First Mate handles it if you used one. | Full cash refund of the sailing cost. A First Mate handles it if you used one. |

Cruise Hive states the $100 and $300 amounts as the offer. VV Insider says the amounts are expected to apply to eligible rebookings, and that you should match the cabin split to the cancellation notice. Cruise Fever limits the $300 line to RockStar and Mega RockStar quarters and tells you to read the notice for your cabin tier. If your email prints a different suite number, use the email.

Only Cruise Hive prints "no fees or penalties." VV Insider prints a full refund of the cruise. Cruise Fever prints a full cash refund. Those three wordings stay separate.

## Where the ship goes after Miami

Cruise Hive says this is Resilient Lady's last roundtrip from Miami before a 14-night transatlantic to Barcelona. The ship stays in Europe for the summer and returns to Miami in early November.

Cruise Fever says the ship keeps homeporting in South Florida until May 2027, then moves to Barcelona for 5 months, and that this chartered week is the last Miami roundtrip before that repositioning. It does not print 14 nights, a summer season, or an early-November return.

Use Cruise Hive for the 14-night crossing and the early-November return. Use Cruise Fever for the 5 months in Barcelona. Each timetable stays on the article that printed it.

That sailing is already a post on this site: [A charter cancels Brilliant Lady's 30 Oct 2027 Los Angeles cruise](/news/brilliant-lady-october-30-2027-los-angeles-charter/). ## If you hold 28 Apr 2027

Open the email. You can move to Valiant Lady on 6 May 2027, pick another eligible 4- or 5-night cruise in April or May 2027, or take the refund. The shared part of a rebooking is the fare and the promotions. The suite credit is $300 in Cruise Hive, $300 per suite cabin in VV Insider, and $300 for RockStar and Mega RockStar quarters in Cruise Fever. Match it to the notice.

If a First Mate booked the cruise, VV Insider and Cruise Fever say that advisor handles the move or the refund. Cruise Hive does not print that line.
