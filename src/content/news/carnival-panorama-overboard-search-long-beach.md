Carnival Panorama lost a guest overboard on the final sea day into Long Beach. Cruise Hive, Fox News, and ABC7 all quote the same Carnival spokesperson statement. A male guest jumped while the ship sailed from Puerto Vallarta to Long Beach on Saturday 19 Sep 2026.

## What you felt onboard

Cruise Hive's shipboard timeline starts with missing-guest announcements around 11am. Around 3pm the ship turned south, away from the Long Beach track. About 3:30pm guests were ordered to staterooms. Security scanned Sail & Sign cards cabin by cabin.

Around 5pm the captain and cruise director said cameras confirmed the overboard. Local authorities would keep searching. Guests could leave cabins again. That lockdown window is the passenger-impact piece if you were on that sailing.

## Search and schedule notes

Carnival's quoted statement says it notified the US and Mexican Coast Guards and is supporting the guest's traveling family. Fox News and ABC7 repeat that language. Cruise Hive reported the ship later resumed toward Long Beach and was told an arrival near 9am was still the plan. Carnival had not confirmed a delayed next embarkation in that 20 Sep report.

The next published product on that report is a six-night Mexican Riviera sailing leaving Sunday 20 Sep 2026 for Ensenada and Cabo San Lucas. Treat any boarding change as unconfirmed unless Carnival emails you.

## What this write-up will not claim

No public name for the guest appears in the Carnival statement. Motive is not stated. Recovery status after the Coast Guard handoff is not confirmed in the sources above. Do not fill those gaps.
