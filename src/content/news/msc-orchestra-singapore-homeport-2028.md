MSC will homeport a ship in Singapore for the first time. [The Singapore Tourism Board release](https://www.stb.gov.sg/about-stb/media-publications/media-centre/msc-cruises-and-singapore-tourism-board-announce-three-year-partnership-for-homeport-deployment-in-singapore-/), datelined Geneva and Singapore on 16 Nov 2026, says MSC Orchestra starts there in the fourth quarter of 2028.

The STB page does not show a ship photo. The photo here is the lead image on [Seatrade's writeup](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/msc-cruises-to-homeport-in-singapore-from-2028), Holly Payne, 16 Nov. The credit line is MSC Cruises. The file name is msc_orchestra_ship.jpg. The caption is about a stay in the city before or after the cruise. It does not name the ship. The bow lettering reads MSC ORCHESTRA.

## What you can book

The release says the Singapore itineraries open for booking on 14 Dec 2026. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/msc-and-singapore-tourism-partner-for-singapore-homeport-deployment/) the same day prints that date from the release. Neither page prints a fare, a deposit, or a sail date inside the fourth quarter of 2028.

The cruises are eight to 12 nights. The countries named are Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Indonesia. The release does not name the ports inside those countries, and it does not name the Singapore terminal. It does say guests can add a stay in the city before or after the cruise.

Orchestra joins MSC Bellissima as MSC's second ship in Asia for winter 2028/29. After that Singapore winter, the release says Orchestra moves to Japan, the Republic of Korea, and Greater China.

## What the refit adds

Before the Asia season, the release says Orchestra gets a refit. The pieces named:

- MSC Yacht Club, which the release calls its ship-within-a-ship concept
- Butcher's Cut steakhouse
- Kaito Sushi Bar
- An enhanced MSC Aurea Spa
- Upgraded fitness facilities

The release does not say those venues are included in the fare. It does not print a yard date.

Gianni Onorato, chief executive of MSC Cruises, is the executive in the release. He ties the partnership to a winter deployment from Singapore and to putting Orchestra in the region with Bellissima. Jean Ng, assistant chief executive of the Singapore Tourism Board's experience development group, is quoted on longer itineraries. Neither quote prints a fare.

[Seatrade's second story](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/people-opinions/cruise-lines-expand-in-asia-while-pressing-for-smoother-clearance) the same day quotes Oliviero Morelli, president for Japan, South Korea, and Southeast Asia in MSC Group's cruise division. He says the ship will be newly refurbished and equipped with the MSC Yacht Club, and that it will homeport in Singapore before moving north to Japan. That matches the release. Two Seatrade pages do not agree on the dates of the Kuala Lumpur conference where he spoke. This post does not use a conference calendar.

## Explora, in the same release

The release says Explora Journeys will run its first cruises from Singapore in 2027. It does not name the Explora ship or the months. That line is in the MSC release. It is not a separate fare.
