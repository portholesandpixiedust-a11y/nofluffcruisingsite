MSC Cruises will homeport in Singapore for the first time. [The Singapore Tourism Board release](https://www.stb.gov.sg/about-stb/media-publications/media-centre/msc-cruises-and-singapore-tourism-board-announce-three-year-partnership-for-homeport-deployment-in-singapore-/), datelined Geneva and Singapore on 16 Nov 2026, says the ship is MSC Orchestra, from the fourth quarter of 2028.

The photo is the lead image on [Seatrade's homeport article](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/msc-cruises-to-homeport-in-singapore-from-2028) the same day. The credit line is MSC Cruises. The file name is msc_orchestra_ship.jpg. The stern lettering in the photo reads MSC ORCHESTRA. The caption is a sentence about adding a stay in the city. It does not name the ship.

## What you can book

The partnership runs three years. Orchestra offers eight- to 12-night sailings to destinations including Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Indonesia. The release does not print a city list, a departure calendar, or a fare.

Bookings for the new Singapore itineraries open 14 Dec 2026. That date is in the release. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/msc-and-singapore-tourism-partner-for-singapore-homeport-deployment/), also 16 Nov, prints the same date. Seatrade's homeport article does not.

The release says guests can add a pre- or post-cruise stay in the city. It does not print a hotel rate or a package price. Seatrade's caption is that stay, and it does not add a price either.

## The second ship, and the refit

Orchestra joins MSC Bellissima as MSC Cruises' second ship in Asia from winter 2028/29. The release does not reprint Bellissima's current route.

Before the Asia deployment, Orchestra gets a refit. The release says it introduces the MSC Yacht Club, which it calls a ship-within-a-ship, plus Butcher's Cut steakhouse, Kaito Sushi Bar, an enhanced MSC Aurea Spa, and upgraded fitness facilities. Cruise Industry News names the Yacht Club and does not print the restaurant names, the spa, or the fitness line. Those four pieces stay on the release.

## Explora, then north

Explora Journeys, part of MSC Group's cruise division, will operate its first cruises from Singapore in 2027. The release and Cruise Industry News both print that sentence. Neither names the ship, a month, or a fare.

After the Singapore winter, Orchestra relocates to Japan, the Republic of Korea, and Greater China. That line is in the release and in Cruise Industry News. Seatrade's homeport article does not print it.

## What this story leaves out

Seatrade's homeport article says Oliviero Morelli delivered the news and prints Seatrade Cruise Asia as 6-17 Nov in Kuala Lumpur. A second Seatrade article the same day prints the Kuala Lumpur dates as 16-17 Nov and Penang as 18-19 Nov. This post does not pick a conference calendar.

If you want a Singapore round trip on Orchestra, the date in the release is 14 Dec 2026, and the fare is not on the page.
