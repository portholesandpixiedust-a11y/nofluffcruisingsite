Carnival Jubilee on Saturday 31 Oct, and Carnival Breeze on Monday 2 Nov, still board at Galveston Cruise Terminal 25. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-breeze-and-jubilee-guests-warned-of-galveston-construction-delays/222126), Calvin Musquez, 29 Oct 2026, quotes a Carnival note from 27 Oct. Harborside Drive, the road by the terminal, has lane closures. The note does not move the sail time.

The photo is the lead photo on that story, two Carnival ships in Galveston, credited to NAN728. The file path is September 2025. The caption does not date it to this weekend. A Carnival newsroom post with the same note was not found on 30 Oct, so the quote below is Cruise Hive's.

## Who the note names

| Sailing | What Cruise Hive says | Still ahead on 30 Oct? |
|---|---|---|
| Carnival Breeze, 29 Oct | 4-night to Cozumel | No. That departure date has passed. |
| Carnival Jubilee, 31 Oct | 8-night to Nassau, RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, and Celebration Key. The article calls the ship a 5,362-guest ship. | Yes. |
| Carnival Breeze, 2 Nov | 5-night to Cozumel and Progreso | Yes. |

The quoted notice says you embark at Cruise Terminal 25. It says construction is underway along sections of Harborside Drive, and that there will be lane closures. It tells you to check traffic that day. Signs and staff on site will direct cars. It does not print a new departure time. Cruise Hive says Carnival has not announced one. Be onboard by the final boarding time on your boarding pass, and keep the arrival appointment printed there.

## Parking and drop-off

| How you arrive | What the quoted note says |
|---|---|
| Lots A and B | Complimentary shuttle to Terminal 25, run by the Port of Galveston. |
| Express Lot, next to Terminal 28 | That shuttle does not cover this lot. Enter on the west side via 33rd Street, not along Harborside. |
| SMP Garage, across from Terminal 25 | The shuttle does not cover this garage. Cruise Hive says the note does not give a route. The article adds that the entrances are usually on 24th or 25th Street. That "usually" is the article, not the quote. |
| Drop-off | Enter on 22nd Street. Exit on 23rd. You cannot drive through from Terminal 28. |

## If you are driving down on Saturday or Monday

Leave time for the lane closures. The appointment on the boarding pass is still the one the note uses. Cruise Hive says Carnival has said in the past that a guest who misses the ship because of traffic does not get a refund. That line is the article's background. It is not printed as a new term in the 27 Oct quote.

The article also says this Harborside work has been going on for months, with similar notes in May and a Carnival Dream terminal change on 5 Sep. Those earlier notices are not this weekend's routes. Use the 22nd, 23rd, and 33rd Street directions for the two sailings still ahead.

Facts above follow Cruise Hive, Calvin Musquez, 29 Oct 2026, quoting Carnival's 27 Oct note.
