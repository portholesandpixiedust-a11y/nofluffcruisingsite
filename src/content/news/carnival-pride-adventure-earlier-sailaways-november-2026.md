You may need to be at the pier 30 minutes sooner. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-earlier-departures-now-hit-316-cruises-as-two-more-ships-added/222927), Melissa Mayntz, on 6 Nov 2026, says Carnival emailed people booked on Carnival Pride and Carnival Adventure. Every time in this post comes from that article. This desk did not find a Carnival newsroom post for the change.

The photo is the lead image on that page, Carnival Pride, credited to Luis F Ayerves. The caption does not date the frame to one of these sailings.

## Carnival Pride from Baltimore

Forty-seven sailings move from 4:30 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.

The first is 6 Dec 2026. Cruise Hive calls it a 7-night Bahamas cruise, with RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, Nassau, and Celebration Key. Every other December 2026 departure from Baltimore on Pride moves to 4:00 p.m. as well.

In 2027, the article says departures from 3 Jan through 21 Nov also leave at 4:00 p.m.

Two sailings stay at 4:30 p.m. Both are 14-night Carnival Journeys.

- 10 Jan 2027 calls St. Thomas, St. Maarten, Dominica, St. Lucia, Barbados, Antigua, and Tortola.
- 31 Oct 2027 calls St. Thomas, St. Maarten, St. Kitts, Aruba, Curacao, and Grand Turk. Cruise Hive calls that one a mix of Eastern and Southern Caribbean.

Cruise Hive prints 2,134 guests for Pride. That count is in this article only.

## Carnival Adventure from Sydney

Twenty-three sailings move from 3:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. Cruise Hive says all of them leave Sydney. The ship also sails from Melbourne and Auckland. Those homeports are not on this time-change list. Not every Sydney cruise is on it either. If your date is missing below, do not assume 3:00 p.m.

2026: 4 Dec, 7 Dec, and 27 Dec.

2027: 19 Jan, 23 Jan, 8 May, 10 May, 14 May, 21 May, 2 Aug, 6 Aug, 9 Aug, 13 Aug, 21 Aug, 24 Aug, 2 Sep, 19 Sep, 23 Sep, 2 Oct, 10 Oct, 14 Oct, 12 Nov, and 25 Nov.

That is three dates in 2026 and 20 in 2027. Cruise Hive's total for Adventure is 23. Cruise Hive prints 2,636 guests for the ship. That count is in this article only.

## What the email does not change

Cruise Hive says routes, port calls, time in port, and the return to the homeport stay as they were. The notice it quotes says you must be on board no later than the final boarding time on the boarding pass, when that pass is available. Final boarding is earlier than sailaway. The article does not print a reason for the new times.

The same article adds Pride and Adventure to a six-ship list and prints a total of 316 sailings, also naming Elation, Glory, Liberty, and Splendor. That total is Cruise Hive's addition. This post does not recheck those other four ships.

## If your date is on the list

Pride from Baltimore is 4:00 p.m. unless you hold 10 Jan 2027 or 31 Oct 2027. Those two stay at 4:30 p.m. Adventure from Sydney is 3:00 p.m. only on the dates above. Read the boarding pass when Carnival posts it. One outlet, Cruise Hive on 6 Nov 2026, is the source for every clock on this page.
