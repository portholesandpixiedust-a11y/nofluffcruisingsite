Disney Cruise Line published a dry dock account on 30 Sep 2026. If you are booked on Disney Wonder, Disney Fantasy, or Disney Magic, that page is the list of what changed in guest spaces. It does not print a fare change.

## Wonder

The page says Wonder came out of dry dock a few weeks before 30 Sep. It does not name the shipyard. It does not print the dock dates.

Wonder's numbers on the page are these. More than 12 miles of new carpet, which Disney compares with 46 basketball courts. A new sustainable hull coating, which Disney compares with painting 10 football fields. More than 100 projects in 14 days. Nearly 1,000 crew members and cast members on the work.

## Fantasy, Magic, and Edge

On Fantasy, the page lists Ramone's Cantina, a Cars-inspired quick-service restaurant. It lists updates to Senses Spa & Salon. It lists the Fantasy Tower Suite, inspired by Fantasia.

Edge, the tween club, is refreshed on Fantasy, Magic, and Wonder. The page describes a new color scheme and modular pieces that can be rearranged for different activities.

Tracy Wilson, senior vice president and general manager of Disney Cruise Line Operations, says each change starts with how to make your cruise better. The page says nearly 90 percent of Disney Cruise Line guests rate the experience excellent. That figure is for the line. The page does not attach it to one ship.

Alexis Cummins, creative director at Walt Disney Imagineering, says each dry dock is a chance to rework spaces based on how you use them.

Under the waterline, the page lists advanced hull coatings on Magic and Wonder, and a new bulbous bow on Fantasy. Disney says that work is meant to help the ships run more efficiently. A reimagined crew dining room is also named. The page does not say which ship got it.

The Fantasy and Magic work is called recent. The page does not give those dry docks a month.

## What you should do

If you sail Wonder, the guest-facing list is new carpet and the refreshed Edge. If you sail Fantasy, add Ramone's Cantina, the updated spa, and the Fantasy Tower Suite. If you sail Magic, the guest-facing item the page names is the refreshed Edge. Your fare is not discussed. Facts above follow Disney Experiences, published 30 Sep 2026.
