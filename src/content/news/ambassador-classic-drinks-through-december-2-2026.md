Ambassador is including a Classic Drinks Package on selected sailings if you book by 2 Dec. [The deals page](https://www.ambassadorcruiseline.com/deals/cruise-offers/), read 26 Nov 2026, sets the window at 09.00 on 24 Nov through 23.59 on 2 Dec. The page does not name a time zone.

The image on this page is the line's own banner, file black_friday_1600x300-website-landing-alt.png. The page does not name a photographer. The frame is a black banner with neon lettering that reads Black Friday and Free Classic Drinks. It is not a photograph of a ship.

## What you get, and what you still pay

The free package is for the first and second adults in a twin cabin on a Saver Fare. A solo guest in any available single cabin is included. Third and fourth guests are not. They buy the same package as the rest of the cabin, at the full price. Both eligible adults in a cabin take the same package. You must be 18 or over to buy or drink alcohol.

Classic covers wines, beers, spirits, cocktails and soft drinks by the glass from the house list, plus 50 percent off drinks that are not on that list. Gratuities are not included. The page adds £7 a person a night to your onboard account. You can change that amount at Reception.

Premium is a supplement of £19 a person a night, and that price includes gratuities. It adds signature wines, top-shelf spirits, and speciality teas and coffees.

The table prints a standard advance price of £42.95 a person a night for Classic and £49.95 for Premium. A star sits on the words Standard advance price. That block does not explain the star.

The offer has no cash alternative. It cannot be combined with charity, member, association, media or affiliate codes, or with an onboard future-cruise offer. Nectar points can still be collected. The line can withdraw the offer on any listed cruise.

## A different name on the £19 line

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/ambassador-launches-black-friday-offer-with-free-select-drinks/) on 26 Nov says the campaign upgrades you to the all-inclusive Ambassador Fare from £19 a person a night. The deals page calls that £19 line the Premium Drinks Package, with gratuities included. Use the deals page for the name. The article also prints 11:59 on 2 Dec, without a.m. or p.m. The deals page prints 23.59.

The same article names no-fly sailings on Ambience and Ambition, from February 2027 through June 2028, to Spain, the Nordics, France, Morocco, Portugal and the Canary Islands. The cards above are on those two ships, and the dates run from 17 Feb 2027 to 25 June 2028.

The deals page has a separate group block: 5 percent off for 15 or more full-paying adults, one extra free place at 25 adults, and two at 50. That block does not print 24 Nov or 2 Dec. The Ambassador Guarantee is a standing line for a first booking after 19 Jan 2024. It is not inside the drinks terms.

## If you are booking

The window on the deals page closes at 23.59 on 2 Dec. Read the code before you use a from-price. The £7 gratuity is extra on Classic. The £19 line is the Premium package on this page, and it includes the gratuity. A22718DD is on the terms list and has no price on the page that was read today.
