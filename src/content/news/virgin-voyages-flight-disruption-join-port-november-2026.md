Virgin's flight update is still the page to read if you missed the ship. [The update](https://www.virginvoyages.com/travel-advisories/stories/flight-disruptions) is stamped 13 Nov 2026. It was still up on 16 Nov.

The advisory page has no ship photo. The photo here is Scarlet Lady on [Virgin's ships page](https://www.virginvoyages.com/ships/scarlet-lady). The bow reads SCARLET LADY. The file name is IMG-SHP-scarlet-lady-exterior-v1-FIL_CF003181_Edited_V3-1080x1080.jpg. It is not a picture of the terminal on 14 Nov.

## Who the update is for

The page says the FAA grounded flights "today," and that this may affect sailors going to Miami to board Scarlet Lady and to San Juan to board Valiant Lady "tomorrow." The stamp on the page is 13 Nov. Tomorrow on that page is 14 Nov.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/late-miami-departure-gives-norwegian-luna-guests-time-to-beat-airport-delays/223644), Melissa Mayntz, 15 Nov, quotes the same notice for those two ships on 14 Nov. [VV Insider](https://vvinsider.com/virgin-voyages-offers-options-to-sailors-affected-by-us-flight-ground-stop/), Rob Sammons, 14 Nov, names the same ships. VV Insider also names a five-night Grand Turk and Bimini product and a six-night Southern Caribbean product. Those product names are not on the Virgin page. This story does not use them.

## Join at the first port

Start with the airline. The update says the airline has the rebooking options.

If you cannot get to the ship in time, the page says to meet it at the first port. You need a passport. The sentences on the page are: "Sailors who have a passport can join us there, and we'll help make it happen." Then: "Give Sailor Services a call and they'll take care of the necessary arrangements." Cruise Hive quotes both sentences.

The page prints two join ports:

- Sailing out of San Juan: Tortola on 15 Nov.
- Sailing out of Miami: Grand Turk on 16 Nov.

The page does not print a clock, a pier, or a fare for either port. It does not say the ship will wait in Miami or San Juan.

If you are reading this on 16 Nov, Grand Turk is the Miami sailing's join port on the page. Tortola was 15 Nov. The page does not name a later port for Valiant Lady.

## If you cannot fly at all

The update has a separate step. If you are flying "tomorrow" and cannot make the ship because of the flight disruption, Virgin says it will provide a Future Voyage Credit for a future sailing. Sailor Services will talk through the options. The page does not print a dollar amount, a percent, or a date the credit expires.

VV Insider reports that credit. Cruise Hive's 15 Nov account of the notice does not print it. Use the Virgin page.

The update also says to ask your travel insurance provider what your policy covers. It does not describe a policy.

## How to reach them

The update links [ways to get in touch](https://help.virginvoyages.com/helpportal/s/contactus). The update text does not print a phone number. It says wait times may be higher than usual, and that Virgin will keep the page updated.

Call before you buy a flight to Grand Turk or Tortola. The page says Sailor Services makes the arrangements. It does not say a sailor can walk up at the port without that call.
