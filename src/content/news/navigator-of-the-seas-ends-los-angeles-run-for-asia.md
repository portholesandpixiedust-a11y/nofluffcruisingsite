Navigator of the Seas is done with year-round Los Angeles. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/10/royal-caribbeans-navigator-wraps-up-year-round-operations-on-west-coast/), dated 10 Oct 2026, says the ship wrapped up a five-year West Coast deployment and started a repositioning to Asia in early October. It had sailed from Los Angeles since November 2021. [Deep Arrival](https://deeparrival.com/news/navigator-of-the-seas-leaves-los-angeles-singapore-season/), dated 11 Oct 2026, reports the same exit.

If you were on the 25 Sep Los Angeles cruise, the Ensenada and Cabo cuts are already on [the Hurricane Polo page](/news/navigator-of-the-seas-hurricane-polo-ensenada-cabo-cancelled/). The change below is the ship leaving Los Angeles for Asia.

## Where is the ship going first?

Cruise Industry News says the first leg is a 15-night Pacific crossing to Tokyo, with calls such as Hakodate, Muroran, and Shimizu. That port list is from Cruise Industry News. The Royal Caribbean page read for this post is the 18 Oct Tokyo sailing, and its port list starts at Yokohama.

The next cruise is on Royal Caribbean's own site. [The 11-night Tokyo (Yokohama) to Singapore sailing](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/itinerary/11-night-tokyo-to-singapore-cruise-from--on-navigator-NV11YOK-478781971?sailDate=2026-10-18) leaves 18 Oct 2026.

Cruise Industry News calls the Hong Kong stop a 17-hour call. Royal Caribbean's page shows 7:00 AM to 11:59 PM on 24 Oct. The arrival text names the Singapore Cruise Centre.

Royal Caribbean says ports on that cruise can still change for weather, safety, or operations.

## What leaves Singapore on 29 Oct?

[Royal Caribbean's 4-night Penang and Phuket page](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/itinerary/4-night-penang-phuket-from-singapore-on-navigator-NV04SIN-2921829329?sailDate=2026-10-29) is a separate cruise on the same ship. It leaves Singapore at 4:00 PM on Thursday 29 Oct 2026. That is the same afternoon the Tokyo cruise arrives. Deep Arrival reported this date as the first listed Penang and Phuket round trip. A Royal Caribbean product page for the cruise listed 29 Oct ahead of 12 Nov.

| Date | Port | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Thu 29 Oct | Singapore | Depart 4:00 PM |
| Fri 30 Oct | Penang | Docked 2:30 PM to 9:00 PM |
| Sat 31 Oct | Phuket | Tendered 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM |
| Sun 1 Nov | Sea | |
| Mon 2 Nov | Singapore | Arrive 7:00 AM |

Cruise Industry News says the late-October Singapore season runs from two to ten nights, to Thailand, Malaysia, and Vietnam, from the Marina Bay Cruise Center. Some dates are open-jaw between Singapore and Japan. Marina Bay and the Singapore Cruise Centre are different terminals. Use the terminal printed on your documents.

## How long does Asia last?

Cruise Industry News says Navigator stays in Asia for summer 2027. The 10 Oct article says the ship now offers Yokohama and Singapore itineraries through next October, then returns to California for a winter season of Mexican Riviera and Baja cruises. In a story dated 10 Oct 2026, next October is October 2027. The article says the ship had been set to return to Los Angeles for that season. [Travel Trade Desk](https://traveltradedesk.com/articles/royal-caribbean-pulls-navigator-of-the-seas-from-los-angeles-1789f1e4), filed the same day, gives the same outline: Asia through about October 2027, then a winter 2027 California season. Neither page prints a calendar day for the California return.

## What can you sail from Los Angeles now?

[Cruise Industry News on 26 Sep 2026](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/09/royal-caribbeans-ovation-arrives-in-los-angeles-for-winter-season/) is the report used here for the ships that remain. Ovation of the Seas keeps sailing from Los Angeles through November 2027, including next summer. The article describes three-night Baja cruises, four- and five-night cruises that also call Catalina Island and Cabo San Lucas, and, from December, seven- and eight-night cruises with up to two overnights in Cabo.

Voyager of the Seas replaces Navigator for the 2026-27 winter season. That is one Cruise Industry News account. Open the ship name on your reservation before you treat it as booked.

## What this means for you

If a future Los Angeles cruise was sold to you as Navigator, read the ship name in the reservation. Ovation is the ship Cruise Industry News says stays in Los Angeles through November 2027. Voyager is the winter replacement in that same report. If you are on the 18 Oct Tokyo sailing, or the 29 Oct Singapore sailing, use the Royal Caribbean clocks above and check the terminal on the documents. A short Baja cruise and a Singapore cruise ask for different passports and entry rules. Royal Caribbean's page tells you to review the documents for every port on the date you booked. Facts above follow Royal Caribbean's itinerary pages, read 12 Oct 2026, and Cruise Industry News on 10 Oct and 26 Sep 2026. Deep Arrival and Travel Trade Desk match the dates those pages already state.
