Carnival will take four drink packages ashore in February 2027. [The newsroom post](https://www.carnival-news.com/2026/11/19/carnival-cruise-line-unveils-the-next-wave-of-vacations-from-sea-to-shore-including-a-new-ship-class-and-expansion-celebration-key), dated 19 Nov 2026, names CHEERS!, CHEERS! Zero Proof, Bottomless Bubbles, and CHEERS! Social. They will apply at Celebration Key and at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay.

The image on this page is a file on that newsroom post, named 2030 2B Waterpark.jpg. The page does not print a caption or a photographer. The frame shows bright waterslides, palm trees, yellow umbrellas, a pier, and a white cruise ship. It sits with the paragraph about the 2030 water park. The lead image on the page is Christine Duffy at the New York announcement. This post uses the water park file. It is not a photograph of Celebration Key as the island stands today.

## What changes on the islands in 2027

The drink-package line is the nearest date in the post. Beginning in February 2027, those four packages extend to Celebration Key and to RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay. The post says a guest who buys them gets more value. It does not print a day rate. It does not say whether a package you already bought carries onto the island.

[Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/shipbuilding-equipment/carnival-unveils-new-ship-class-celebration-key-water-park), Anne Kalosh, on 19 Nov, prints the same four package names and says the change begins in February. That sentence does not print 2027. Use the newsroom year.

Serenity Beach is the other 2027 date. The post calls it a complimentary retreat for guests 21 and older at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, in spring 2027. It lists dedicated loungers and bar and beverage service. Daybeds and cabanas cost extra. The post does not print the price. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/carnival-cruise-line-announces-new-ship-class-celebration-key-expansion/) on 19 Nov prints the same beach rules and does not print a price either.

## Celebration Key in 2028 and 2030

The post says Celebration Key has welcomed more than 3.25 million guests since it opened in July 2025. Two later phases add land-side capacity of 6,000 guests, 3,000 in each phase.

In 2028, a new portal adds a heated beach-entry pool with a swim-up bar, a social waterslide for four or five guests, and an aqua play area. That area has five waterslides, a dry climbing structure, and a drenching bucket for younger children.

In 2030, a water park adds 18 waterslides. The post calls the lazy river the longest in the cruise industry, with float-up bars and cabanas you can swim into. It also lists an exclusive area with an infinity pool, more beach, and new places to eat and drink. The water park file on this page is the image that sits with that paragraph.

The post lists Paradise Collection as six places: Celebration Key, RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, Isla Tropicale in Roatan, Amber Cove, Puerta Maya in Cozumel, and Grand Turk. The 2027, 2028, and 2030 work in this post is Celebration Key and Half Moon Cay. The post does not assign a new build date to the other four.

## The ships, which are later

Two new ships introduce a class the post does not name. Fincantieri builds them. Delivery is 2033 and 2035. The post says they are about 130,000 gross tons, LNG-powered, and smaller, so they can reach more ports. Christine Duffy, president, is quoted on a 29-ship fleet and on the gap between Excel Class ships and the line's smaller ships. The post does not print a guest count for the new class.

Seatrade adds two figures the newsroom post does not print. It says the ships have 3,300 lower berths, and it names Fincantieri's Marghera yard. Treat those two lines as Seatrade's. Seatrade also calls Carnival Destiny 230,000 gross tons. The newsroom post does not print that number, so this page leaves it out.

Carnival Destiny is a different ship. The post says it debuts in 2029 as the first of three Ace Class ships. It lists eight pools, six of them infinity pools, and more than 11,200 square feet of water. It calls that more outdoor pools than any other Carnival ship, including the first outward-facing main pool. It also lists an ocean-front lanai, an exclusive pool on Deck 18 Aft, 25 new dining and bar venues, and a reimagined Limelight Lounge.

Family space on Destiny is nearly twice the space on Carnival's newest ships, in one zone with mini golf, arcades, and dining. Dr. Seuss Splash Station is a splash pad with The Cat in the Hat, Thing One, and Thing Two, plus water cannons and a spot for children in swim diapers. The post ties that to a 13-year partnership with Dr. Seuss Enterprises.

Adults get a first on Destiny: an adult-only zone on four decks, the first adult-only staterooms, an expanded Serenity retreat, and an exclusive rooftop pool. The post's line that more than 6.5 million guests sail each year is a fleet figure. It is not a count for Destiny or for the 2033 ships.
