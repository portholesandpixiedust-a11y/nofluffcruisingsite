Princess already prints Half Moon Cay on its drink terms. Celebration Key is the island with a later date. [The Plus Beverage Package terms](https://www.princess.com/content/experience-fragments/princess/en-us/modal/food-dining/the-plus-beverage-package/the-plus-beverage-package-v2.html), read 23 Nov 2026, say the package may be used ashore on Half Moon Cay and Princess Cays. Starting 1 Feb 2027, it may also be used ashore on Celebration Key. The page does not print 23 Nov.

The image on this page is the hero file on [Princess's Half Moon Cay page](https://www.princess.com/cruise-destinations/bahamas-cruises/half-moon-cay), named relaxaway-halfmoon-hero. The page does not print a caption or a photographer. The frame is an aerial of a curved white beach, a pier, and small boats.

## The date the trade reports, and the date the terms print

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/princess-extends-package-perks-to-celebration-key-and-half-moon-cay/) on 21 Nov says package benefits become available at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, starting 23 Nov 2026, and at participating locations throughout Celebration Key beginning 1 Feb 2027. It calls that a statement. It does not name the statement. [Cruise Critic](https://www.cruisecritic.com/news/cruisers-react-carnival-drink-package-expansion), Nusreta Beranac, updated 22 Nov at 11:43 a.m. EST, says Princess confirmed the same two dates.

The Plus terms do not print 23 Nov. They already list Half Moon Cay. Use 1 Feb 2027 for Celebration Key, because the terms, the [Celebration Key page](https://www.princess.com/cruise-destinations/bahamas-cruises/cruises-to-celebration-key), and both trade reports print that date. Use 23 Nov only as the date those two reports give for Half Moon Cay.

This is a different line from yesterday's Carnival note, [Carnival drink packages reach two islands in February 2027](/news/carnival-drink-packages-celebration-key-half-moon-cay-february-2027/). Carnival's newsroom date for both islands is February 2027. Princess's terms split the islands.

## What you pay above $15

On Celebration Key, the Plus terms limit included drinks to $15.00 USD and under. A drink over that is the full menu price. The 25 percent discount on a bottle of wine, a one-liter bottle of water, or canned soda does not apply there.

[The Celebration Key FAQ](https://www.princess.com/cruise-destinations/bahamas-cruises/cruises-to-celebration-key) says the same full-price rule for Princess Plus, Princess Premier, and other qualifying beverage packages. The $15 value cannot be applied toward a drink priced above $15. Before 1 Feb 2027, that page says the packages do not extend ashore and drinks are a la carte. Only the All-Inclusive Pearl Cove Beach Club package includes beverages at Pearl Cove Beach Club. From 1 Feb, the same FAQ says the packages can be used for food and drink purchases, and that some restrictions apply. It does not list the restaurants.

At Half Moon Cay and Princess Cays, the Plus terms work differently. If a drink is over $15.00 USD, or over $22.00 AUD on an Australia and New Zealand voyage, you pay the difference, not the full menu price. The Half Moon Cay FAQ does not print that difference.

## The side of the island

The Half Moon Cay FAQ says Princess Plus and Princess Premier will extend to the island. It then says that, because of operating systems, the beverage packages will be honored only on the same side of the island where your Princess ship arrives. It names north or south. The Plus terms do not print that sentence. Cruise Industry News and Cruise Critic do not print it either.

The same Half Moon Cay page says an active onboard internet plan works on the island, and that you buy the plan on the ship. The Celebration Key page says an active onboard Wi-Fi plan works there, and that you cannot buy the plan on the island. Neither page dates the Wi-Fi line to 23 Nov or to 1 Feb. Cruise Industry News says the packages bring savings on drinks, dining, and Wi-Fi. It does not print those Wi-Fi sentences.

## Which 15-drink rule you have

For a Plus Beverage Package bought on or after 22 July 2025, on a cruise that leaves on or after 14 Jan 2026, the cap is 15 drinks in each 24 hours, from 6 a.m. to 6 a.m. The terms say that cap covers alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks. Specialty coffee and tea do not count. Bottled water of 500 ml or less does not count, and it stops at 12 bottles a day. One drink per order.

A package bought before 22 July 2025 uses a different cap on the same page. That cap is 15 alcoholic drinks. Non-alcoholic drinks do not count. The island pages do not print a separate island cap.

[The Plus and Premier page](https://www.princess.com/cruise-deals-promotions/plus-premier-cruise-packages) says Premier includes drinks up to $20 USD, or $30 AUD, and that there is no daily limit on included alcoholic beverages. It still puts a $15 limit on Celebration Key from 1 Feb 2027, and it says the bottle discount does not apply there. This post does not treat Premier as unlimited at Celebration Key.

The Plus terms also print a day rate of US $64.99 plus 18 percent, totaling $76.70, and AUD $110.00 a day on Australian-based ships. That rate is for the Plus Beverage Package. It is not a new island fee.

## If you are going ashore

If the ship is at Half Moon Cay, read the side of the island on your call. The FAQ limits the package to that side. If the ship is at Celebration Key before 1 Feb 2027, the Celebration Key page says the packages do not extend and drinks are a la carte, apart from the Pearl Cove package. From 1 Feb, a drink over $15 is the full menu price. The 69 Celebration Key calls in the 2026-27 season are already in [Princess lists 69 Celebration Key calls this season](/news/princess-celebration-key-69-calls-2026-27/). This note does not rename those ships. Cruise Industry News still does not name the six ships.
