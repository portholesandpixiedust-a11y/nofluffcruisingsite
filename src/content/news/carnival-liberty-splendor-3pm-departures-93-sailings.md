Carnival is moving 93 departures up by 30 minutes. The new time is 3:00 p.m. The old time was 3:30 p.m.

[Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-cruise-line-moves-up-departure-times-on-93-more-sailings/222607), Melissa Mayntz, 4 Nov 2026, says the count is 47 sailings on Carnival Liberty from New Orleans and 46 on Carnival Splendor from Sydney. [Cruise News IO](https://www.cruisenews.io/carnival-moves-93-liberty-and-splendor-sailings-to-3-p-m-departures/), Jed Stafford, 6 Nov, prints the same 47 and 46. Cruise Hive says it obtained the letters. Dates run from December 2026 into November 2027. Neither article quotes a Carnival newsroom post.

The photo is the lead image on the Cruise Hive story. The caption is Carnival Splendor in Sydney. The credit is Lowfii Studio. Cruise News IO credits its image to cruisehive.com. This page uses the Cruise Hive file.

## 93 now, and the earlier wave

Cruise Hive says this round follows an adjustment of 153 sailings on Carnival Elation and Carnival Glory. The same article puts the four-ship total at 246 earlier sailaways.

Cruise News IO also prints 246 for the four ships. It does not print 153. Its figures for the earlier Florida wave are 77 Carnival Elation departures from Jacksonville, from 3 Dec 2026 through 22 Nov 2027, and 76 Carnival Glory sailings from Port Canaveral. Glory's changes begin 14 Dec 2026 and cover selected departure periods through November 2027. Cruise Hive's figure for the Elation and Glory wave is 153. Each count stays on the article that printed it.

## Carnival Liberty from New Orleans

Both articles say every Liberty departure from 3 Jan 2027 through 20 Nov 2027 leaves New Orleans at 3:00 p.m. That is 47 sailings. Most of them are 7-night cruises.

Cruise Hive prints 2,974 guests. Cruise News IO does not print a guest count.

Both articles count 46 earlier departures. December 2026 is 8, 13, 17, 26, and 30. The rest are 2027. Cruise News IO prints the same dates Cruise Hive prints.

August 2027 is empty on both lists. Cruise Hive says the ship is out of service in dry dock. Cruise News IO says a scheduled dry dock. Neither article names the yard.

The sailing that keeps 3:30 p.m. is 31 Jul 2027. Both articles call it a 15-day one-way cruise from Sydney to Singapore. Cruise Hive adds the calls: Moreton Island, Airlie Beach, Cairns, Lembar, and Benoa. Cruise News IO does not print those ports. Departures from Sydney start again on 17 Sep, and that sailing is on the 3:00 p.m. list.

Cruise Hive says Splendor's lengths in this period run from 4 to 15 nights, and that the longer sailings are outside this time change. It prints 3,012 guests. Cruise News IO agrees that the longer voyages are outside the change. It does not print 3,012.

Port visit times and the time you return to the homeport stay put, on both ships, in both articles.

## Why the time moved

Carnival has not given a reason. Cruise Hive says the line has not offered more details. Cruise News IO says Carnival has not disclosed a specific operational reason.

Cruise Hive then says changes like this are not uncommon. It lists port operations, berth schedules, and pilot availability as factors that can affect a sailaway. That list is the article's. It is not a sentence from Carnival about these 93 cruises.
