Navigator of the Seas has called Langkawi. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/ports-destinations/navigator-of-the-seas-makes-langkawi-debut-amid-cruise-growth), Holly Payne, on 21 Nov 2026, says the visit that day was the ship's first call at the Malaysian island.

The photo on this page is the welcome picture on that article. The caption reads: Waffa Wahida Zainol, third from right, as Langkawi welcomes Navigator of the Seas. The credit is PHOTO: LADA, the Langkawi Development Authority. The frame shows the group on the pier and the ship's white hull, with the Royal Caribbean anchor and the start of the word International. The bow name is not readable. A second photo on the article, a plaque exchange, is credited to Langkawi Cruise Terminal. This post uses the welcome file.

## The cruise Seatrade dates

The ship left Singapore on 19 Nov. Penang was 20 Nov. Langkawi was 21 Nov. Phuket was 22 Nov. Seatrade says the ship is scheduled back in Singapore on 24 Nov.

The article does not print an arrival time or a departure time. It does not print a fare. It does not say Langkawi replaced another port. It calls the visit the first one.

A Royal Caribbean Singapore address for a five-night Penang, Phuket, and Langkawi cruise, opened on 23 Nov, redirected to a find-a-cruise search page. This post does not take a sail date from that page. The dates above are Seatrade's.

The October note on this site, [Navigator of the Seas ends its year-round Los Angeles run](/news/navigator-of-the-seas-ends-los-angeles-run-for-asia/), is the repositioning into Asia. It does not record this Langkawi call.

## What LADA said is next

Zainol, tourism manager at LADA, told Seatrade that Disney Adventure's first port call in Langkawi is 31 Dec. She said the ship is expected to bring an estimated 150,000 to 200,000 passenger movements from late December 2026 through December 2027. That range is her projection in this interview. Seatrade does not print a Disney release, a cruise length, a homeport, or a fare for the 31 Dec call.

She also said 2027 projections point to more passengers, alongside deployments by Royal Caribbean, Princess Cruises, and European lines. She listed lines that have already called: Navigator of the Seas, Spectrum of the Seas, Anthem of the Seas, Celebrity Millennium, Celebrity Solstice, Mein Schiff 6, and Oceania Nautica, plus luxury and expedition ships the article does not name.

The rest of the interview is about shore tours LADA wants to add, and about a hope for turnaround calls later. Those sentences do not change a booking you hold. Seatrade does not list the next Navigator date in Langkawi after this cruise.

## If you were on 19 Nov, or you hold 31 Dec

If you sailed from Singapore on 19 Nov, Seatrade's account of your ports is Penang, Langkawi, Phuket, and a return on 24 Nov. It does not print the hours. If you hold Disney Adventure around 31 Dec, the first-call date in this article is Zainol's, and the article does not print the cruise you would match it to. Read the booking for the port and the clock.
