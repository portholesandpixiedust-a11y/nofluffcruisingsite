Virgin Voyages has two live offers, and one of them ends at 11:59 p.m. east coast time on 11 Nov. The [promotional terms](https://www.virginvoyages.com/promotional-terms-and-conditions), version line 3 Nov 2026, were read 11 Nov 2026. [Cruise Industry News](https://www.cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/virgin-announces-black-friday-and-cyber-monday-deal/) published a Black Friday and Cyber Monday story the same day. The clocks below are the terms page.

The photo is the lead image on the Cruise Industry News story. The file name is Virgin-Voyages-Brilliant-Lady-at-Sea.jpg. It is an aerial of a Virgin Voyages ship, with the Virgin mark on the stern. The article text retrieved does not name a photographer. The loot dates on the terms page name Scarlet Lady and Resilient Lady. They do not name Brilliant Lady.

## The credit that ends on 11 Nov

The November Promotional Sailor Loot is up to $300 in onboard credit per cabin. New bookings qualify from 12:01 a.m. east coast time on 3 Nov through 11:59 p.m. east coast time on 11 Nov 2026. The terms say the credit applies automatically at booking.

The amounts:

- $50 per cabin on a Sea Terrace.
- $300 per cabin on a Rockstar Quarters suite or a Mega Rockstar suite.

The page names the departures. It does not name the region or the ports. Resilient Lady has 18 dates, from 2 May 2027 through 24 Oct 2027. Scarlet Lady has 23 dates, from 24 Apr 2027 through 31 Oct 2027. If your sailing is not on that list, this credit is not the one in the terms. Match the date on the page before you book.

Unused Sailor Loot is not cash. It does not move to another sailor or another voyage. Charters are out. Incentive and contracted groups are out. Lock It In rates are out of this credit. Refer a Sailor is out of this credit. Reduced-rate cabins are out. Travel agents do not earn commission on the credit.

The loot terms say this credit may be combined with the Black Friday preview below. The preview section does not name this November loot. It does name an Exclusive Sailor Loot Program on its own stack list. Those are two different labels on the same page.

## The fare cut that runs through 19 Nov

The Black Friday preview is headlined 80% off a second sailor, plus up to $600 in free drinks. The terms posted the same day, 3 Nov 2026, describe the math as 40% off the retail base voyage fare for each of two sailors sharing an eligible cabin. Four sailors in one cabin each get 40% off. The window is 12:00:01 a.m. east coast time on 3 Nov through 11:59:59 p.m. east coast time on 19 Nov 2026.

Eligible voyages are any non-charter voyage open for sale. The page does not print a last sailing year.

The percentage covers Sea Terrace cabins, including a Lock It In Ltd View Sea Terrace, plus Sea View, Lock It In Sea View, Insider, and Lock It In Insider. Solo Sea View and Solo Insider are excluded. The sailors have to be in the same cabin. Base, Essential, and Premium fares qualify. Charter voyages are out.

## The Bar Tab, and who does not get it

The drink credit is a Bar Tab for the booking. The terms say that amount is divided across the sailors in the cabin. It is not $600 a person.

| Nights | Rockstar suite | Sea Terrace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 or more | $600 | $250 |
| 10 to 13 | $400 | $200 |
| 6 to 9 | $300 | $200 |
| 4 to 5 | $150 | $50 |

A cruise of 3 nights or fewer can still take the fare percentage. It does not get the Bar Tab.

The Bar Tab list is Rockstar suites (Brilliant, the Cheeky Corner terrace sizes, Seriously, and the Sweet Aft terrace sizes) and most Sea Terraces. It does not include Lock It In cabins, Mega Rockstar suites, Sea View, or Insider. A Lock It In Sea Terrace can sit inside the percentage and outside the Bar Tab. Read the cabin name on the invoice against both lists.

The preview can stack with resident rates for Florida, Georgia, Puerto Rico, Canada, the Northeast, Mexico, the West Coast, and Texas, with military and first responder rates, with the reduced single supplement, and with Refer a Sailor. The loot, above, cannot stack with Refer a Sailor. The preview also says it is not combinable with incentive or contracted circles, or with any other offers. Virgin says it can change the offer.

## What Cruise Industry News printed on 11 Nov

The 11 Nov article says guests buy one fare and get the second sailor 80% off, plus up to $600 in free drinks depending on length. It says the offer runs from 3 Nov to 3 Dec 2026, for sailings through 2028, for all available inventory, and that the website applies it automatically.

The terms page does not print 3 Dec. It does not print a 2028 cutoff. It does not say every cabin is in. The automatic line on the terms page is attached to the Sailor Loot, and that loot ends at 11:59 p.m. east coast time on 11 Nov.

The article's sample routes are a 7-night Eastern Caribbean and Bimini Beach Club from Miami, a 4-night California coast and Mexico trip from Los Angeles, a 12-night Scandinavia cruise from Amsterdam with a Stockholm overnight, and a 10-night trip from Athens to Rome. Those are the article's examples. The terms page does not tie them to the loot dates.

The same article says Valiant Lady will sail from Amsterdam in summer 2028 on Scandinavia and Baltic itineraries, including a crossing of the Arctic Circle. That deployment sentence is not in the terms, and it is not one of the 41 loot dates.

## If you are booking on 11 Nov

For the $50 or $300 credit, the terms close at 11:59 p.m. east coast time on 11 Nov, and only on the named Scarlet Lady and Resilient Lady dates. For the 40% cut, the terms run through 19 Nov, on eligible cabins across voyages that are open for sale. A Lock It In cabin can take the percentage and miss both the Bar Tab and the loot. The Cruise Industry News date of 3 Dec is not the date on the terms page read on 11 Nov.
