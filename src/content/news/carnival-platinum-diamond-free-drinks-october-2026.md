If you are Carnival Rewards Platinum or Diamond, you have two complimentary drinks. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/carnival-clears-up-confusion-over-free-drinks-for-diamond-and-platinum-guests/222013), Melissa Mayntz, 28 Oct 2026, says John Heald posted a Facebook video that day telling you how the bar applies them. The [benefits page](https://www.carnival.com/carnival-rewards/benefits), read 29 Oct 2026, lists the two drinks. It does not print his bar instruction.

The photo is the open-deck file on that Cruise Hive article. Cruise Hive credits denbaim. The ship is not named. This post does not treat the frame as a photo of the bar, or of Heald.

## What your status lists

The benefits page sorts the drinks by stars.

| Status | Stars on the benefits page | Complimentary drinks listed |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | From 10,000 | One, on cruises of five days or longer |
| Platinum | From 50,000 | Two |
| Diamond | From 100,000 | Two |

Each of those three levels also lists a complimentary 1.5L bottle of water. That bottle is its own bullet. Heald's example, a bottle of water from the bartender, is a drink order. This post does not treat the cabin bottle and the bar bottle as the same item.

Cruise Hive's report of the video is about Platinum and Diamond. It does not give Gold the same "tell the bartender" instruction. If you are Gold, use the one drink the page lists, and ask the bar on board how that one is rung.

## What to say at the bar

This part rests on Cruise Hive's report of Heald's 28 Oct video. Carnival's benefits page does not print it.

Heald said the bar will use your first two drinks as the complimentary ones unless you say otherwise. His example is a bottle of water when you are thirsty. Unless you tell the bartender not to count it, that water uses one of the two.

If you want the water, or any other drink, charged to your Sail & Sign so you can save the free ones, say that before the drink is rung. If you want the free one now, say that as you order.

Heald also said to tell the bartender or bar server when you are using a complimentary drink. The article does not print a price cap on what those two drinks can be.

## FunPacks sit beside the two drinks

Carnival put four FunPacks on sale in a [14 Oct release](https://www.carnival-news.com/2026/10/14/carnival-cruise-line-introduces-funpacks-delivering-more-value-on-popular-cruise-extras). Booking runs through 30 Oct 2026, for sailings from 31 Oct 2026 through 31 Dec 2027. Prices and what each pack includes are in [Carnival drink and Wi-Fi prices start varying on Dec. 1](/news/carnival-dynamic-pricing-drink-packages-wifi-december-2026/).

Heald's answer on the video, as Cruise Hive quotes it, is that a FunPack does not take the two Platinum or Diamond drinks away. "That does not interfere with that at all."

Cruise Hive also prints a purchase rule the 14 Oct release does not: buy the FunPack at least three days before the sailing. Use Cruise Hive for that line. Heald said he did not know if the packs would return after the window. On 28 Oct he told viewers they had two more days.

## What you should do

If you are Platinum or Diamond and you board with a plan to save the two drinks for a later round, tell the bartender before the first drink is rung. Say it again when you want the free one.

If you want a FunPack as well, the Carnival window closes 30 Oct 2026. The two loyalty drinks are a separate benefit in Heald's answer. Check the live offer in My Cruise Manager before you pay. The bar script above follows Cruise Hive's 28 Oct report of his video. The star levels follow Carnival's benefits page as read 29 Oct 2026.
