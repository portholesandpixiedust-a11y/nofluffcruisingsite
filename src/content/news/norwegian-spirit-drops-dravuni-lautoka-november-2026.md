Norwegian Spirit's 17 Nov 2026 cruise from Papeete loses Dravuni and spends the last night in Lautoka instead. [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/norwegian-spirit-drops-fiji-island-stop-and-trims-four-south-pacific-port-calls/223327), Calvin Musquez, on 10 Nov 2026, and [Cruise News IO](https://www.cruisenews.io/norwegian-spirit-drops-dravuni-for-overnight-in-lautoka/), Jed Stafford, on 11 Nov 2026, both describe a notice Norwegian sent to guests and travel partners.

The photo is the lead picture on the Cruise Hive story. The credit is Bbits Photos. It is not a photo of this sailing. Cruise News IO credits its picture to The Cruise Web. That file is not used here.

No Norwegian newsroom post for the notice was open at this check. Neither article links the notice file.

## The port that comes off

Both reports say Dravuni on Sunday 29 Nov is cancelled. The old hours they print are 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. In place of that call, the ship arrives in Lautoka at noon on 29 Nov and stays overnight. Guests still get off on Monday 30 Nov. Lautoka was already the end of the cruise. The new piece is the extra night there, not a new final port.

Cruise Hive calls the ship a 1,972-passenger ship. Cruise News IO does not print that count. The figure stays with Cruise Hive.

## The four shorter calls

Both reports shorten Moorea, Bora Bora, Raiatea, and Suva, and both put the lost time at eight hours.

- Moorea on 18 Nov arrives at 8:00 a.m. instead of 7:00 a.m. Neither report prints a new departure time.
- Bora Bora on 19 Nov leaves two hours earlier. Neither report prints the new clock.
- Raiatea on 20 Nov ends three hours earlier. Neither report prints the new clock.
- Suva on 28 Nov leaves at 5:00 p.m. instead of 7:00 p.m.

Cruise News IO says the French Polynesian cuts are six of the eight hours. Do not invent a clock the reports do not print.

Cruise News IO says Rarotonga, Pago Pago, and Apia stay. Cruise Hive lists them on the original route and does not say they were cut.

The notice, as both reports describe it, says the changes support the operation of the voyage. It does not name a specific fault. Neither report prints a fare refund or onboard credit.

## The tours

Dravuni excursions booked through Norwegian are cancelled and refunded to the original payment. On the four shorter calls, the line moves a tour when it still fits, or cancels it and refunds it. Both reports say that is automatic.

A tour you bought from someone else is not in that sentence. Cruise News IO says you have to check those plans against the new hours.

## The cruise after this one

Cruise Hive says the next sailing is a 12-night repositioning that leaves Lautoka on 30 Nov for Sydney, and that Dravuni is still on that cruise. Cruise News IO also says the Sydney trip is 12 nights and that Dravuni stays on it. It does not print 30 Nov. Both say South Pacific itineraries resume in May 2027. That later Dravuni call does not put the island back on the 17 Nov cruise.

## If you are booked on 17 Nov

Dravuni is off. Lautoka starts at noon on 29 Nov and runs overnight, and you still get off on 30 Nov. Read a tour confirmation again if it was for Moorea, Bora Bora, Raiatea, or Suva. A Norwegian tour should move or come back to the original payment. An outside tour will not do that on its own. The notice these two stories describe does not say why the day was cut.
