Holland America put one 2029 cruise on sale later this week, if you use the line's own page. [That page](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/news/2026/November/news-11-16-2026-coffee-tea-spice), datelined Seattle on 16 Nov 2026 and read 17 Nov, says Eurodam sails 3 Jan to 30 Jan 2029, roundtrip from Singapore. The name on the page is the Coffee, Tea and Spice Route. It is a 27-day Legendary Voyage.

The photo is the lead image on [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/holland-america-to-trace-coffee-and-tea-routes-in-2029-legendary-voyage/) the same day. The alt text is Eurodam. The file name is Eurodam-2.jpg. The bow reads EURODAM. The hull reads Holland America Line. The page does not print a photographer or a port. The photo is not Singapore. The Holland America page, as fetched, did not include a ship photo.

## The calls the page names

The headline says 13 ports across seven countries. After Singapore, the body names these calls:

- Tanjung Priok (Jakarta) and Semarang (Borobudur), on Java.
- Bali, two days, and the highlights call it an overnight.
- Muara, Brunei Darussalam.
- Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, Malaysia.
- Manila.
- Kaohsiung, Taiwan.
- Naha, on Okinawa, and Ishigaki. The body says the Japanese islands of Okinawa and Ishigaki. The highlights name Naha.
- Hong Kong, an overnight that the page also calls two days.
- Nha Trang, then an overnight at Phu My, which the page calls the gateway to Ho Chi Minh City and also describes as two days.
- Back to Singapore on 30 Jan 2029.

That is 12 named calls plus the Singapore turnaround. This post does not add a port the page does not print. [TravelPulse](https://www.travelpulse.com/news/cruise/holland-america-introduces-27-day-coffee-tea-and-spice-route-through-asia), Noreen Kompanik, 16 Nov at 12:06 p.m. ET, repeats the 13 ports and seven countries, then lists Hong Kong in the same breath as Indonesia, Brunei, Malaysia, the Philippines, Taiwan, Japan, and Vietnam. The line page's number stays seven. This post does not pick a country count for Hong Kong.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/holland-america-to-trace-coffee-and-tea-routes-in-2029-legendary-voyage/) on 16 Nov prints the same dates and the same calls. It does not print a fare either.

## What you can book, and what is still open

The line page says bookings open later this week. Read on 17 Nov, that sentence was still on the page. No fare was on it. No hour for the opening was on it. The phone on the page is 1-877-724-5425.

For a limited time, booking this cruise with Have It All keeps the package's shore excursions, specialty dining, Signature Beverage Package, and Surf Wi-Fi, and adds free prepaid crew appreciation plus free upgrades to the Elite Beverage Package and Premium Wi-Fi. The page does not print a dollar value for the excursions or a last day for the bonus. Mariner Society members can get up to $400 in onboard credit per stateroom. The page does not name a tier. TravelPulse prints that bonus. Cruise Industry News does not.

Tea blends and single-origin coffees are described as plans, with Art of Tea and Beukenhorst. The line page says more programming will be revealed as details are confirmed. Do not treat a menu as published.

This cruise is not the fleet Black Friday sale. That earlier offer is [Holland America opens Black Friday early access today](/news/holland-america-black-friday-early-access-november-2026/). The 16 Nov page does not say the two stack.

If you want this sailing, the date in the release is 3 Jan 2029 from Singapore, and the fare is not on the page.
