Cunard's Queen Elizabeth is running a shortened Alaska week after a late Seattle departure. Cruise Industry News reported the change on 22 Sep 2026. World Ports Organization carried matching details the same day. The Juneau and Endicott Arm cuts rest on guest reports filtered through those Tier 2 outlets. No Cunard newsroom release was located.

If you are already on this sailing, check your Folio and the Cunard app for refunds or credits on cancelled tours. Ketchikan and Victoria are still on.

## What changed

The ship was due out of Seattle late on the afternoon of 17 Sep 2026. It left at 5pm on 18 Sep instead. An onboard statement blamed essential maintenance. The ship also changed berths and spent the night in Seattle, per Cruise Industry News.

Juneau is gone. Endicott Arm scenic cruising is gone. Those losses come from guest reports, not a Cunard press release.

Ketchikan stays. Victoria stays. The Seattle return on 24 Sep 2026 stays on the published schedule.

## Why this sailing matters

This seven-night loop is part of Queen Elizabeth's last Alaska summer for Cunard on this deployment. After Seattle, the ship is set to reposition toward PortMiami with a Panama Canal transit, then run nine- to 11-night Miami cruises into early 2027, according to Cruise Industry News and World Ports Organization.

## What you should do

If you are on this sailing, treat Juneau and Endicott Arm tours as cancelled unless Cunard confirms otherwise in writing. Check your Folio and the Cunard app for automatic refunds or credits on those shore excursions. Confirm Ketchikan and Victoria times in your latest itinerary update. Ask Guest Services what compensation, if any, Cunard is posting for the lost port and scenic day. This write-up rests on Cruise Industry News and World Ports Organization on 22 Sep 2026.
