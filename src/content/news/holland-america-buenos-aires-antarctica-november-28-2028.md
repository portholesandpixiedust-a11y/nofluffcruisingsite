Holland America has put a 2028-29 South America and Antarctica season on sale, with a new 17-day round trip from Buenos Aires. [The wire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-roundtrip-buenos-aires-voyage-highlights-holland-americas-2028-2029-south-america-and-antarctica-season-302949719.html) is datelined Seattle, 24 Nov 2026, 12:30 p.m. ET. [The line's news page](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/news/2026/November/news-11-24-2026-south-america-antarctica) prints the same itinerary block and dates the copy Seattle, Wash., 23 Nov 2026. The page address uses 11-24-2026. This post follows the wire for the clock and the phone, and both pages for the ports.

The wire's lead file is Holland-America-Line-Logo.jpg. This post does not use a logo. The wire points to a photo library. The library page, read 25 Nov, did not put a photographer's name on a ship photo this post could use.

## The 17-day cruise, and the other dated trips

The new cruise is the 17-day South America and Antarctica Circle. It leaves Buenos Aires on Zuiderdam on 28 Nov 2028 and returns to Buenos Aires. The release names Punta Arenas, Chile, Ushuaia, Argentina, and the Falkland Islands, plus Antarctica. It calls this a more compact choice than the line's longer Antarctica itineraries. It does not print a day-by-day, a port clock, or a fare.

The season runs from October 2028 through March 2029. Lengths run from 15 to 32 days. The release does not name a 32-day sailing.

Zuiderdam's 22-day South America and Antarctica cruises sail between Buenos Aires and San Antonio (Santiago). They combine Antarctica with the Chilean Fjords, Glacier Alley, Cape Horn, and the Falkland Islands, and they include an overnight in Buenos Aires. The dated departures are 15 Dec 2028, 6 Jan 2029, and 28 Jan 2029. The release does not say which of those dates leaves Buenos Aires and which leaves San Antonio.

The 15-day South America Pathways cruises, Chilean Fjords and Glaciers, also sail between Buenos Aires and San Antonio. Select itineraries add scenic cruising at Pío XI Glacier. The release calls that glacier the largest in the Southern Hemisphere outside Antarctica. That comparison is the line's.

The 16- and 17-day Inca and Panama Canal Discovery cruises sail between Fort Lauderdale and San Antonio. They include a Panama Canal transit, calls in Ecuador and Peru, and an overnight at Callao (Lima). The release does not print a departure date for this pair.

Volendam's Legendary Amazon Explorer goes into the Amazon with an overnight in Manaus. The 30-day version leaves New York for Fort Lauderdale on 23 Nov 2028. The 27-day version is a Fort Lauderdale round trip "departing Nov. 26." The release does not print a year on that date. This post does not add 2028 to it.

## What the scenic days and the ship work include

Scenic cruising in the release covers the Chilean Fjords, Glacier Alley and the Beagle Channel, Cape Horn and the Strait of Magellan on select itineraries, and the Sarmiento, Cockburn, and Darwin channels on select cruises. Pío XI is on select Pathways voyages.

Zuiderdam brings the Evolution work to this region for the first time, in the release's words. The named pieces are the Grand Dutch Café, 32 Solo Verandah staterooms, two new Bridgeview Suites, 14 Vista Suites, a reimagined Pinnacle Suite, and refreshed rooms. The [1 Oct Canada and New England post](/news/holland-america-2028-canada-new-england-volendam-zuiderdam/) already recorded Solo Verandah staterooms, Bridgeview Suites, and the Grand Dutch Café for a different season. The counts in this sentence are from the 24 Nov wire.

Shore trips in the release, with no prices, are a multiday Overland Tour to Machu Picchu on select sailings, an optional multiday Galápagos tour that names giant tortoises and blue-footed boobies, and a trip inland from Punta Arenas to Torres del Paine. On board, Destination Dining in this season names ceviche and empanadas, and South American and Antarctic dinners. The release does not print a cover charge.

## The bonus, separate from the Black Friday percent

Have It All, for a limited time, includes shore excursions, specialty dining, a Signature Beverage Package, and Surf Wi-Fi, plus free prepaid crew appreciation and free upgrades to the Elite Beverage Package and Premium Wi-Fi. The release does not print an end date, and it does not print a dollar value for the excursion credit or the crew appreciation.

Mariner Society members can get up to $400 onboard credit per stateroom. The release does not name a membership tier.

That bonus is not the percent-off sale already on this site. [The 11 Nov Black Friday post](/news/holland-america-black-friday-early-access-november-2026/) lists South America and Antarctica among the regions for up to 30 percent off, on a public window that runs through 29 Nov, and it excludes Grand Voyages and 2028 Alaska Cruisetours. This 24 Nov release does not print that percent, that end date, or those exclusions. Do not treat the two as one offer.

The wire's guest phone is 1-877-724-5425. The news page text read on 25 Nov does not include it. Neither page prints a sample fare. If you want the 17-day Antarctica cruise, the date in both pages is 28 Nov 2028 from Buenos Aires on Zuiderdam. If you want the 27-day Amazon round trip, the release prints Nov. 26 and does not print the year.
