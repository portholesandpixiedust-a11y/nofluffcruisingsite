Bookings are open for Holland America's 2028 Alaska Cruisetours. [The line's release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/holland-america-line-opens-2028-alaska-cruisetours-pairing-reimagined-oosterdam-with-adventures-into-the-great-land-302944926.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Seattle, 19 Nov 2026, 6:31 a.m. ET.

The photo is the lead image on that release. A passenger is photographing a river and mountains through a train window. The seat pattern is gold and brown. The caption says guests travel on the McKinley Explorer to Denali during an Alaska Cruisetour. The file name is McKinleyExplorer3-Alaska-Photographer-Andy-Newman.jpg. The frame does not show the railcar's name. The release paragraph does not print a photographer credit. The file name includes Photographer-Andy-Newman.

## What opened today

The release says this is the first chance to pair an overland trip with a cruise on the reimagined Oosterdam. Oosterdam makes its Alaska debut in 2028, after the Holland America Evolution refit.

You can choose from 19 itineraries on four ships. They run nine to 17 days, from May through September. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/hal-opens-2028-alaska-cruisetours-on-four-ships-including-oosterdam/) on 19 Nov prints the same counts.

[The 27 Aug news page](https://www.hollandamerica.com/en/news/2026/August/news-08-27-26-alaska-2028), read 19 Nov, already opened the 2028 cruise season. That page says Oosterdam will be the primary Cruisetour ship, and that details and availability for 2028 Cruisetours would be announced later. Today's release is that later announcement. This post does not recap the cruise-only dates on the August page.

## Which ship you get

Classic, Signature, and Ultimate Alaska and Denali Cruisetours pair the land trip with a seven-day Glacier Discovery cruise on Oosterdam or Eurodam.

Alaska, Denali and Yukon Cruisetours go farther, past Skagway to Whitehorse and Dawson City. Those use the same seven-day Glacier Discovery cruise on Oosterdam or Eurodam, or a shorter Alaska Inside Passage cruise on Koningsdam or Zaandam. The release says Holland America is the only cruise line that takes Cruisetour guests into the Yukon. Cruise Industry News prints that line too.

The four styles in the release:

- Classic: three itineraries, nine to 10 days, one night at Holland America Denali Lodge, and the Denali Natural History Tour.
- Signature: six itineraries, 10 to 12 days, two or three nights at the lodge, and the Tundra Wilderness Tour.
- Ultimate: three itineraries, 14 days, two nights at the lodge, and an extended stay in Alyeska, Homer, or Seward. A tour director is on the land portion. The Alaska Wildlife Conservation Center is included. Other included stops depend on the itinerary. The release names the Tundra Wilderness Tour, Riverboat Discovery, and Gold Dredge 8.
- Yukon: seven itineraries, nine to 17 days, fully escorted, with a tour director on the land portion, a lodge stay, a Denali park visit, and the Yukon.

One-night lodge stays include the Denali Natural History Tour. Two- and three-night stays include the Tundra Wilderness Tour. Every Cruisetour includes the glass-domed McKinley Explorer. Select itineraries add the White Pass and Yukon Route Railway, and Direct-to-Denali service from the ship to the lodge in one day.

The release says 2028 guests can be in Dawson City for the destination's 130th anniversary. Select itineraries include two nights at the Holland America Dawson City Inn and the Klondike Gold Tour.

## The lodge, and the $869 package

Every Cruisetour includes a stay at Holland America Denali Lodge. The release says the $70 million project finishes in 2027, before these trips. It prints two new guest buildings adding 72 rooms, refreshed public spaces, and expanded dining. [The October lodge post](/news/holland-america-2027-alaska-80th-season-denali-lodge/) already recorded the room work for the 2027 season. This release repeats the 72-room line for guests on the 2028 Cruisetours. Cruise Industry News prints the $70 million project and does not print the 72-room count.

For a limited time, guests who book with the Have It All Early Booking Bonus get the Cruisetour Dining Package on the land portion. The release values that package at $869 a person. It includes select meals at Holland America properties, lunch on the McKinley Explorer, and, depending on the itinerary, dining tied to Music of Denali and the Tundra Wilderness Tour.

The same bonus also names shore excursion credit, specialty dining, an upgrade to the Elite Beverage Package, an upgrade to Premium Wi-Fi, prepaid crew appreciation, and the Best Price Guarantee. The release does not print a dollar amount for the shore credit. It does not print an end date for the words "limited time." It does not print a cruise fare. Cruise Industry News does not print the $869 package.

## What this is not

The [11 Nov Black Friday post](/news/holland-america-black-friday-early-access-november-2026/) already recorded the exclusion. That sale leaves out 2028 Alaska Cruisetours. Today's release does not put these trips into that percent-off offer. The bonus on this page is the Have It All package above, not the 30% line from 11 Nov.

The release also cites a U.S. News ranking, No. 1 for Alaska, and a Good Housekeeping award for the 12-day Signature Denali Cruisetour. Those lines are in the release. They are not a fare, and the release does not print how either award was scored.
