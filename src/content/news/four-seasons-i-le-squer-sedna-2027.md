Chef Christian Le Squer is the culinary ambassador for Sedna, the fine-dining restaurant on Four Seasons I, through 2027. He is on the ship in person for two voyages. [The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chef-christian-le-squer-named-culinary-ambassador-for-sedna-the-signature-fine-dining-restaurant-aboard-four-seasons-i-302936972.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Toronto, 12 Nov 2026, 9:00 a.m. ET. [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/people-opinions/george-v-chef-christian-le-squer-tapped-for-four-seasons-i), Anne Kalosh, reported it the same day. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/chef-christian-le-squer-joins-cruises-on-four-seasons-i/) named the same two dates on 13 Nov.

The picture is the release photo. The file name is Christian-Le-Squer-001.jpg. It is a portrait of a chef in a white coat, indoors. It is not a photo of Four Seasons I, and it is not a photo of Sedna. The release text does not name a photographer. Seatrade's credit line is Four Seasons Yachts.

## What changes on every 2027 cruise

The release says guests at Sedna get his French cooking through a signature tasting menu and an a la carte list. That role runs through 2027. It is the menu, not a promise that he is in the restaurant on your week.

He will also help set Sedna's direction and advise on later Chef-in-Residence bookings. Sedna's chefs train with him and his team at Le Cinq, the three-Michelin-star restaurant at Four Seasons Hotel George V in Paris. The release says he led Pavillon Ledoyen while that restaurant kept three Michelin stars for twelve years, and that he joined George V in 2014. He calls his menus moving rather than seasonal. That is a kitchen description. It is not a list of dishes.

Ben Trodd, CEO of Marc-Henry Cruise Holdings, the joint owner and operator named in the release, is one of the executives quoted. Philipp Blaser, senior vice president of food and beverage at Four Seasons, is the other. Neither quote prints a price.

## The two voyages where he is aboard

The release names the residencies:

- Lesser Antilles featuring Guadeloupe and Anguilla, 21 Feb through 28 Feb 2027. The release calls it a winter week from Guadeloupe to Anguilla.
- Greek Isles featuring Mykonos and Göcek, 12 Sep through 19 Sep 2027. The release calls it a late-summer sailing from the Cyclades to the Turquoise Coast.

Seatrade and Cruise Industry News print those same dates and those same place names. If your Four Seasons I cruise is not one of those two, the release does not put the chef on board.

## The room, not the bill

Sedna seats 66 guests indoors and 40 outdoors, with a private dining room for up to 10. The release says the room is emerald green, the cooking is French technique, and there is a sommelier wine list and cocktails. Seatrade repeats the three seat counts. Those numbers are capacity. They are not a reservation you already hold.

The release does not say the tasting menu is included in the cruise fare. It does not print a cover charge. It does not limit the restaurant to a cabin grade. It does not print a yacht guest count. If you are deciding whether Sedna is in the fare, the release is not the price list. Read the restaurant note on the booking.

## If you are booked

On the 21 Feb 2027 and 12 Sep 2027 voyages, the release says the chef is aboard and that guests can meet him. On other 2027 dates, the change the release describes is the Sedna menu. Nothing in the release moves a port or cuts a fare.
