If you are on a Carnival ship in the two weeks before 6 Nov 2026, the line has added a movie week. It has not told you whether your ship is in it.

[Carnival's newsroom post](https://www.carnival-news.com/2026/11/02/carnival-cruise-line-celebrates-the-theatrical-release-of-the-cat-in-the-hat-with-new-family-experiences-at-sea), dated 2 Nov 2026, says 23 ships are running limited-time Cat in the Hat events up to the film's premiere on 6 Nov. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/carnival-ships-celebrate-the-cat-in-the-hat-movie-premiere/) the same day repeats the 23 and the date. Neither page names the ships.

The photo is the lead image on Carnival's post. The alt text says Carnival hosts The Cat's Hollywood Debut Party onboard, with a balloon drop, to mark the movie. The page does not name a separate photographer.

## What the two weeks include

The newsroom list, matched by Cruise Industry News, is short.

- A 40-foot Cat in the Hat inflatable on select open decks while you board.
- The Cat's Hollywood Debut Party in the atrium. Balloon drops, a red-and-white premiere carpet, characters, entertainment, and crafts.
- The Cat's Secret Surprise. Deliveries to select staterooms: postcards, snack packs, and invitations to meet-and-greets.
- A Build-A-Bear workshop with Thing 1 and Thing 2 shirts, plus a Cat in the Hat bear.
- Camp Ocean activities called The Cat's Messy Room Mission, The Cat's Stacking Challenge, and The Cat's Rhyme-O-Rama.
- Green-screen photos tied to the movie.

The inflatables are on select ships, not on a promise of all 23. Deliveries go to select staterooms. The post says availability and timing vary by ship and sailing.

Christine Duffy, the president, is quoted on the 15-year Dr. Seuss partnership and on more family entertainment. The quote does not price anything. The post prints no fare, no credit, and no fee for these events.

A co-branded spot is also set for the in-cabin television, plus online, TV, and streaming. That is advertising on the screen in the room. It is separate from the atrium party.

## What is added to Seuss at Sea

The post says Seuss at Sea already has the Seuss-a-palooza parade, Story Time, the Thing 1 and Thing 2 Birthday Breakfast, and Dr. Seuss Bookville.

Two pieces are new.

Seuss-a-palooza Game Time is in the main theater. Music, dancing, tongue twisters, and games run before Story Time.

Seuss-a-palooza Family Time has you move like the characters, act out scenes, and play charades.

The post does not put an end date on those two. It puts the six movie items in the two weeks before 6 Nov. It then says timing for the experiences it has described varies by ship and sailing. Cruise Industry News prints the same split.

## What to do on board

Open the daily program on your ship. That list is how you learn whether the hat, the party, or the new theater piece is on your sailing.

The newsroom count of 23 does not identify the hull. A sailing that starts after 6 Nov is outside the window the post gives the movie extras. The two new Seuss pieces are the ones it does not lock to that date.
