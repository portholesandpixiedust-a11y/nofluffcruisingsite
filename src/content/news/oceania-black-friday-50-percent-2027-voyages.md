Oceania's Black Friday offer is a percentage off 2027 voyages, and the booking window is short. [The release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oceania-cruises-unveils-black-friday-savings-of-up-to-50-across-all-2027-voyages-302941570.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Miami, 17 Nov 2026, 9:00 a.m. ET.

The photo is the lead image on that release. The caption says Oceania Allura in Split, Croatia. The credit line is Oceania Cruises and David Rossow. The file name is Oceania-Cruises-Oceania-Allura.jpg. The frame is a white ship seen through a stone arch, with red tile roofs in front. The bow reads OCEANIA. The hull reads ALLURA. The photo is not a picture of a discounted fare.

## The window

Book from 18 Nov through 1 Dec 2026. The release says the offer covers more than 200 voyages, with up to 50% off all 2027 voyages. [TravelPulse](https://www.travelpulse.com/news/cruise/oceania-cruises-unveils-50-discount-on-2027-sailings), Patrick Clarke, updated 11:16 a.m. ET on 17 Nov, prints that same window and the same "more than 200" count.

[The Black Friday page](https://www.oceaniacruises.com/special-offers/black-friday), read 19 Nov, says the offer ends 1 Dec 2026 and repeats up to 50% on all 2027 voyages. The fetched text on that page does not print a dollar fare. [The special-offers index](https://www.oceaniacruises.com/special-offers), read the same day, shows 210 available cruises under Black Friday. That index does not print the 1 Dec date. Use the release and the Black Friday page for the end date. The 210 figure is the index, not the release's "more than 200."

## The percentage is a ceiling

The release says discounts are subject to change and apply to specific stateroom categories. It does not say every cabin on every sailing is 50% off. The examples it prints run from 50% down to 10%:

- Oceania Marina, Istanbul to Trieste, 10 days, 28 Oct to 7 Nov 2027, 50% off. The release names ancient Olympia, Kotor, and Croatia's Dalmatian Coast.
- Oceania Allura, Athens to Rome, 7 days, 15 Oct to 22 Oct 2027, 45% off. Calls named: Ephesus, Crete, Olympia, and Sicily.
- Oceania Allura, Barcelona to Rome, 9 days, 17 Nov to 26 Nov 2027, 45% off. The release names Menorca, Sardinia, Sicily, and Italy's coastline.
- Oceania Aurelia, Trieste to Athens, 12 days, 6 Dec to 18 Dec 2027, 35% off. Ports named: Kotor, Crete, Rhodes, Ephesus, and Istanbul.
- Oceania Marina, Miami round trip, 7 days, 20 Dec to 27 Dec 2027, 35% off. Calls named: Costa Maya, Belize City, Roatan, and Cozumel.
- Oceania Riviera, Seattle round trip, 10 days, 24 May to 3 Jun 2027, 30% off. The release names Ketchikan, Juneau, glacier fjords, and Victoria.
- Oceania Sonata, Trieste to Barcelona, 9 days, 21 Aug to 30 Aug 2027, 30% off. The release calls Sonata the newest ship in this bullet and names Dubrovnik, Sorrento, Rome, Tuscany, and Provence.
- Oceania Sirena, Miami round trip, 12 days, 28 Nov to 10 Dec 2027, 25% off. Calls named: Aruba, Curacao, Bonaire, Grenada, and St. Lucia.
- Oceania Riviera, Hong Kong to Tokyo, 11 days, 16 Apr to 27 Apr 2027, 10% off. Stops named: Taipei, Busan, Nagasaki, Kyoto, and Tokyo.
- Oceania Sonata, Rome to Trieste, 14 days, 7 Aug to 21 Aug 2027, 10% off. The release names Malta, Mykonos, Corfu, Montenegro, and Croatia.

TravelPulse does not print this list. None of the opened pages prints a dollar fare next to these percentages. The release phone is 866-985-1318.

## The terms page is a different block

[The promotion terms](https://www.oceaniacruises.com/legal/promotion-terms), read 19 Nov, still show a block titled Black Friday Early Access. That block says up to 50% is valid from 4 Nov through 17 Nov 2026, on select categories and select sailings, and it says to mention code HOBF. It also prints a choice of complimentary wine and beer, or a shore excursion credit that runs from $400 to $1,200 by voyage length.

That block does not print a public window of 18 Nov through 1 Dec. This post does not treat HOBF as the code for the sale that is open now, and it does not add the wine or shore credit to the public offer. If a fare page shows a code, read that fare. The early-access dates on the terms page had already ended when this page was checked.

Oceania Cruises is not in the line list on this site, so this story has no line hub.
