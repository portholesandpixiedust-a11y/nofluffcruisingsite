Compensation scales by cabin category:

| Cabin | Onboard credit | Future cruise credit |
|---|---|---|
| Interior / Oceanview | $100 | $100 |
| Balcony | $150 | $150 |
| Suite | $250 | $250 |

Weather disruption in the region is normal for this time of year. What is worth noting is that Royal Caribbean compensated the shortened sailing as well as the extended one, which does not always happen.
