Princess Cruises put a number on its Celebration Key season. [The line's release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/princess-cruises-commemorates-inaugural-season-at-celebration-key-with-star-princess-302939740.html) on PR Newswire is datelined Fort Lauderdale, 15 Nov 2026, 12:50 p.m. ET. It says Star Princess made the inaugural call, and that the call begins 69 scheduled visits in the 2026-27 season, then 32 planned calls in 2028, across six ships.

The photo is the release image of president Gus Antorcha and Captain Paolo Arrigo. The file name is Gus-Antorcha-and-Captain-Arrigo.jpg. They stand beside a sand plaque. The plaque reads Princess inaugural visit, November 15, 2026, and it prints both names. The wire caption names the two men and does not describe the plaque.

## What the release counts

The six ships are not named. The release does not print a fare, a sample sailing, or a homeport for the 69 calls. Antorcha's quote uses the same 69. It does not add a guest total.

[Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/11/princess-commemorates-celebration-key-inaugural-season-with-princess/) on 16 Nov restates the 69, the 32, and the six ships. It does not name the other ships.

The island is on the south side of Grand Bahama. The release calls Celebration Key Carnival Corporation's beachfront destination. The ceremony in the release brought in Ginger Moxey, Minister for Grand Bahama, with Arrigo and Antorcha for a sand-impression handprint. A Junkanoo band played The Love Boat theme. A sand sculpture sat under the Sun Portal. Cruise Industry News prints those same pieces.

## A different post has the guest totals

The 30 Sep story, [Celebration Key expects 3.5 million guests in 2027](/news/celebration-key-princess-aida-costa-3-5-million-guests-2027/), uses Cruise Industry News figures from an earnings call. This release does not print 3.5 million, 2.7 million, or a brand count. Leave those numbers on that post.

The phone number in the release is 1-800-774-6237. If you already hold a Princess Caribbean cruise, this release does not say which of the six ships is yours. Match the ship on the booking.
