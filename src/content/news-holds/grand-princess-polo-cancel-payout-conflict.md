This draft stays in news-holds. Princess changed Grand Princess for Hurricane Polo, and the two reports agree on most of the route. They do not agree on the money if you cancel.

Cruise Industry News, dated 26 Sep 2026, says a cancel pays a future cruise credit equal to 100 percent of the base fare. Cruise Hive, dated the same day, says a cancel pays a full 100 percent refund of the fare. Both say guests who stay aboard get $100 in onboard credit. No Princess newsroom release was located. Until the payout is one thing, do not publish a number a booked guest might spend against.

## Where the route agrees

Both outlets say the cruise left Los Angeles on Friday 25 Sep 2026 and dropped La Paz, Guaymas, and Puerto Vallarta. Both say San Diego was added as an overnight around 26 Sep, and Mazatlan was added.

Cruise Hive is the only one with clocks: La Paz on 29 Sep, Guaymas on 30 Sep, Puerto Vallarta on 2 Oct, San Diego from 26 Sep to 27 Sep, Mazatlan on 1 Oct. Cruise Industry News says the ship arrived in San Diego for an overnight on 26 Sep and names Mazatlan without a date.

## Where they split

Cruise Industry News says Cabo San Lucas was cut from two days to a late-night departure on the first day. Cruise Hive does not mention a Cabo change. That gap is smaller than the payout split.

Cruise Industry News calls the ship 1998-built. Cruise Hive says 2,610 passengers. Leave both figures out of any published version until you only need one of them.

## What you should do

If you are on this sailing, use the Princess app for the port list. If you are deciding whether to cancel, ask Princess whether the offer is a future cruise credit or a refund to your card. The $100 onboard credit for staying is the one figure both outlets print the same way.
