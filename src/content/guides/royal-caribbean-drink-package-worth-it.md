## How much does the package actually cost?

Royal Caribbean prices the Deluxe Beverage Package dynamically. The range across the fleet in 2026 runs from about $57 a day at the cheap end to $120 at the expensive end, with a median near $84. Royal Caribbean then adds 18% gratuity on the package price itself.

## What is the break-even?

Drinks on board run to about $14 for a cocktail. At the fleet median of roughly $84 a day plus gratuity, you need about six to seven drinks a day before the package pays for itself. On a cheap sailing that drops to four or five.

## When it is not worth it

If you drink two or three a day, pay as you go. If you hold Diamond or above, your complimentary drinks already cover a moderate day, and the math on that sits in our Crown &amp; Anchor breakdown.
