## 1. A tier between Diamond Plus and Pinnacle

[Crown & Anchor rules](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/content/dam/royal/resources/pdf/crown-and-anchor-program-rules.pdf), effective Jan. 30, 2026, put Diamond at 80 points, Diamond Plus at 175, and Pinnacle Club at 700. You earn one point per night. A suite night adds a second point. A solo traveler paying the single rate also earns that second point, in any category.

The gap the video walks is the last one. From 175 to 700 is 525 points. At one point a night, that is 75 seven-night cruises. At two points a night, it is still more than 37 of them. The step from Diamond to Diamond Plus is 95 points. The last jump is more than five times that step. That math is the rules, not a guess.

The [benefit grid](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/content/dam/royal/resources/pdf/crown-and-anchor-society-benefit-grid.pdf) for sailings from Sep. 1, 2026 shows why the top matters. Diamond Plus gets five drink vouchers a day, two free days of VOOM Surf+Stream on one device, and priority show seating on select ships if you reserve. Pinnacle gets six vouchers, unlimited Surf+Stream on one device for the sailing, Coastal Kitchen when capacity allows, and the same show seating. Each voucher is worth up to $14.

Two video lines are shorter than the grid. It says Pinnacle gets free laundry. The grid gives Diamond Plus and Pinnacle one free $39.99 wash-and-fold bag, on Wash & Fold Day, on sailings of five nights or longer. It says milestone cruise certificates at 700 points and every 350 after that. The grid's perk is a complimentary cruise, or a dollar amount toward a cruise, at those same marks.

The five-word answer is the one the video builds on. [Royal Caribbean Blog](https://www.royalcaribbeanblog.com/2026/10/05/royal-caribbean-wont-merge-loyalty-benefits-celebrity-cruises), updated Oct. 5, quotes Charles from Tampa asking if Royal had considered a tier in between. Bayley's reply, as that story prints it: "Yes we have. Next question."

Matt Hochberg, who was in the room, calls it quick wit. He also reads the joke as a sign of how hard a change would land. That reading is his. Bayley did not say it. The sentence is one published recap. It is not in the Common Ground recording checked for this guide. Nobody on that stage named a point level or a date.

The [Crown & Anchor guide](/guides/royal-caribbean-crown-anchor-tiers/) has the rest of the ladder. If you are at 300 or 400 points, this answer does not add a perk.

**What to do:** Open your point total next to the Jan. 30 rules. Count a new tier only when Royal prints the name and the number.

## 2. Making more money from loyalty

The same Oct. 5 story has the money line. Bayley, as printed: "A lot of companies go through this on how to monetize more loyalty, how to make it more efficient. And, we've seen with some of our competitors that they've made changes that are not well viewed." Then, "we're very conscious of that."

## What it means for your booking

Five checks cover the week.

If your points sit between 175 and 700, nothing in this Q&A adds a tier. Read the grid for the perk you think you already have, and assume a sold-out sailing full of Pinnacles can shut the lounge.

Keep Royal perks on Royal ships. Celebrity status and a future Sandals stay are separate products until a release says otherwise. The [Sandals guide](/guides/royal-caribbean-sandals-five-things-confirmed/) is the file to use for the deal.

Do not pay extra for a 2026 or early 2027 Western Caribbean cruise only to reach Royal Beach Club Cozumel or Perfect Day Mexico. If Labadee is on your invoice before July 2027, read the ports again. The spokesperson's window and Bayley's "until we're confident" line point the same way.

For the ships, use what is already on sale. Icon from Galveston in the 2027 season. Symphony from Fort Lauderdale, on the April spokesperson statement.
