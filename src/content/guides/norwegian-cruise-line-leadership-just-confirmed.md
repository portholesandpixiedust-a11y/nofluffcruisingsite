## 1. Norwegian says it has been too cheap (0:45)

[Marc Kazlauskas](https://www.nclhltd.com/news-media/press-releases/detail/752/norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-appoints-marc-kazlauskas-as) became president of the Norwegian Cruise Line brand on 19 Jan 2026. He came from the agency side. His last job was CEO of Avoya Travel.

On 11 Apr 2026, he told [Travel Weekly](https://www.travelweekly.com/On-The-Record/Marc-Kazlauskas-Norwegian-Cruise-Line) two things that sit next to each other. "We should get our fair price for a premium product." And, "I think that we had been a little too much known as a discount or promotion cruise line."

He named the guest he wants. "Our big focus is premium families and seasoned travelers." Premium families, in that answer, means several generations in one party who will pay for service, food, entertainment, and activities. The video's captions say "season travelers." The published answer says seasoned.

The person hired to sell that idea is Lee Applbaum. [Norwegian's 29 Jun release](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/news-events/press-releases/detail/801/norwegian-cruise-line-names-lee-applbaum-as-chief-marketing) makes him chief marketing officer beginning 6 Jul 2026. He was global marketing chief at Patrón and later Bacardi, then chief marketing officer at the private-jet company Wheels Up. The video shortens that to tequila and private jets.

On 4 Sep, Kazlauskas told [Seatrade Cruise News](https://www.seatrade-cruise.com/people-opinions/-we-re-a-different-company-from-when-i-started-ncl-s-kazlauskas) what Applbaum keeps telling him. "He has said to me 50 times 'I cannot believe what you get for what we charge.'" Kazlauskas's answer: "We have to communicate this."

Read the next sentence from the same interview before you treat this as a price hike. The focus he described is a disciplined booking curve, the best offers out first, and baseloading business earlier.

That matches what CEO John Chidsey said on the 30 Jul earnings call. [Cruise Industry News](https://cruiseindustrynews.com/cruise-news/2026/07/norwegian-shifts-to-base-loading-pricing-to-rebuild-booking-curve/) and Travel Weekly UK both quote him. In some places, Norwegian "were holding price too high, too far out," which pushed the line into late discounts. The new plan puts more competitive fares up earlier. "This is not about discounting the product." Our [6 Oct write-up](/news/norwegian-cruise-line-earlier-competitive-pricing-2026/) has the booking-curve detail.

So you have two on-the-record lines. April: the brand got known for promotions, and Kazlauskas wants a premium price. July: some fares were too high too early, and the posted price is supposed to come down to a level that holds. Check the fare on your sailing. Do not assume it only moves up.

In the video, Matthew says he has spent 37 nights on six Norwegian ships, from Dawn to Aqua. He says premium fits the newest ships, and he wants proof before he pays a premium price on the older ones. That count is his.

**What to do:** Price the sailing you want against the fare Norwegian is posting now. If the ship is an older one, ask what on board is supposed to justify a higher price.

## 2. Advisors earn commission on the whole fare (2:03)

A non-commissionable fare is the slice of the cruise fare your advisor used to earn nothing on.

[Travel Weekly on 23 Dec 2025](https://www.travelweekly.com/Cruise-Travel/Norwegian-Cruise-Line-eliminates-NCFs) reported that Norwegian is eliminating those slices beginning 26 Dec, for cruises departing 1 May and after. "It said the change will be permanent."

That word is the point of the chapter at 2:23. Norwegian tried a version in 2023. It did not cover every booking. Advisors had to file a marketing plan to qualify. Norwegian ended it in early 2024 and said it had not brought in enough business.

The new version was still in place on 12 May 2026. [Travel Weekly](https://www.travelweekly.com/Cruise-Travel/Insights/NCF-elimination-survive-NCL-shakeup) quotes chief sales officer John Chernesky: "The feedback we have received since we announced the elimination has been overwhelmingly positive, reinforcing our commitment to keeping the policy intact." The same story says sister line Oceania followed, for cruises going on sale for the first time.

The video says taxes and fees still do not earn commission. The 23 Dec and 12 May stories describe the cruise fare. They do not add a sentence about taxes. Treat the fare rule as the checked fact.

Kazlauskas told [Travel Market Report on 15 May](https://www.travelmarketreport.com/cruises/articles/a-cruise360-chat-with-norwegian-cruise-lines-marc-kazlauskas) why he kept it. Advisors said the non-commissionable slice was in the way and they needed to be paid fairly. "And we, as a company, agreed." He added a line to the advisor program: "listen, act, listen." His wording: "It's not just listen. We act on their suggestions and then we listen again to see if we have to make alterations or changes."

An advisor now has a reason to put Norwegian in front of you, and a reason to push for you on it. The video's warning holds. Ask why this ship beat the others.

**What to do:** If you book with an advisor, ask what they earn on this sailing, and why they picked Norwegian over the next line on your list.

## 3. Free at Sea, the Haven, Starbucks, and dining (3:35)

Kazlauskas's "premium families" line is the frame for the perk changes.

On 5 Nov 2025, [Norwegian's own release](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/news-events/press-releases/detail/744/norwegian-cruise-line-announces-biggest-sale-of-the-year) brought Free at Sea back "by popular demand" and retired the More at Sea name for new bookings. For a standard seven-night sailing, that release includes three specialty dining meals for the first and second guest. You still pay gratuities.

The video says a December announcement used those same words for the Free at Sea Plus upgrade. [Travel Weekly on 18 Dec 2025](https://www.travelweekly.com/Cruise-Travel/NCL-brings-back-Free-at-Sea-Plus-bundle) reports the Plus bundle, at $49.99 per day, for sailings from 1 Feb. That story does not say "popular demand." The phrase is on the 5 Nov release, for Free at Sea itself.

Three 2026 changes are narrower, and two of them check out on a primary page.

**Haven restaurant, from 23 Apr.** [Cruise Critic](https://www.cruisecritic.com/news/norwegian-cruise-line-opens-the-haven-restaurant-to-non-haven-guests) quotes a Norwegian spokesperson. You need a non-Haven cabin, such as a balcony, that physically connects to a Haven cabin. You have to be traveling with the Haven guests. They have to be at the meal. The meal is free, including no service fee. One outlet has that quote. The video lists the who, and skips the price.

**Starbucks, from July, as the video tells it.** Guests 13 and older can buy the unlimited package, and one person can buy it without the rest of the reservation. [The terms URL in the video description](https://www.ncl.com/uk/en/content/all-terms-and-conditions-starbucks-package), opened 20 Oct 2026, does not show that paragraph. It loads the guest ticket contract. [Mad About Cruises on 30 Aug 2026](https://madaboutcruises.com/norwegian-cruise-line-package-changes/) is the write-up that prints the age drop from 18 to 13, the solo purchase, a 20 percent gratuity, one drink per transaction, and no package at Harvest Caye or on sailings of two days or less. One outlet. Check the package screen on your booking before you rely on it.

**Specialty dining, reported from 26 Aug.** [Cruise Hive](https://www.cruisehive.com/norwegian-cruise-line-quietly-updates-popular-dining-perk/216324) quotes an update: "For all new bookings made on or after August 26, 2026, Specialty Dining will be available to guests 3-8." Before that, the perk stopped at the first two guests. That matches the 5 Nov release, which names the first and second guest. Cruise Hive is the only outlet used here for the 26 Aug line.

The [Free at Sea terms page](https://www.ncl.com/cruise-deals/promotion-terms/free-at-sea-terms-and-conditions), opened 20 Oct 2026, still says the specialty dining package applies to guests 1 and 2. It does not contain the 26 Aug sentence. Its price table is labeled as prices from 7 Jul 2023. Until your booking confirmation shows guests 3 through 8, do not count those meals.

Cruise Hive also says children ages 2 through 12 are not charged the package price before the cruise and can use the children's menu. A regular menu item triggers the fee. Guests 13 and older pay the package fee. That detail is the same single story.

**What to do:** Open the terms attached to your reservation. Check the booking date. A family of four only gets the extra specialty meals if that reservation is on the 26 Aug rule, and the public terms page checked today still shows the old limit.

## 4. Who runs Norwegian now (4:56)

Most of the names in this chapter made their reputations outside cruise lines. The releases back that up.

[John Chidsey](https://www.nclhltd.com/investors/news-events/press-releases/detail/765/norwegian-cruise-line-holdings-appoints-john-w-chidsey-as) became president and CEO of the parent company on 12 Feb 2026. He had been CEO of Subway for five years, and CEO of Burger King before that. He was already a director, appointed in February 2025, and he had served on the board from 2013 to 2022. The March board deal also made him chairman, effective 31 Mar.

## What you can do

Before you book, do four checks.

Ask your advisor why this ship, now that the commission covers the fare. Read the fare next to the [July pricing shift](/news/norwegian-cruise-line-earlier-competitive-pricing-2026/), so you know whether you are looking at a premium ask or an earlier posted price. Open the Free at Sea terms on your reservation before you count specialty meals for guests 3 through 8. If you want the Haven restaurant from a connecting cabin, confirm the cabin connects and that the Haven guests will be at the table.

The same questions are worth asking of the other big lines. [Carnival's leadership guide](/guides/carnival-leadership-confirmed-q3-2026/) and [Royal Caribbean's leadership guide](/guides/royal-caribbean-leadership-just-confirmed/) put their bosses' lines next to the filings.
