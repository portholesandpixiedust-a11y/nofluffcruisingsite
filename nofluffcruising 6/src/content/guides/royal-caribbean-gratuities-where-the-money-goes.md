Automatic gratuities are the charge people forget when they compare cruise fares, and they have gone up again.

The video breaks down the current rate by cabin category, what the money actually funds, and whether removing them does what people think it does.

The short version: at over $500 for a family of four on a week-long sailing, this belongs in your budget from the start rather than as a surprise on the final bill.
